## Brief

> I want to put solar panels on my Prius. It should take a bunch of C60s in strings and mppt them. They should fit on the top roof so you can measure the size of the roof of a 2017 Prius and shrink it a little bit for margins. It should take that raw voltage, step it up, and dump it into the battery. It should make sure that the battery can't go over voltage. It should make sure that if the wires outside of the battery are shorted, that the battery can't dump, you know, the 200 or whatever volts into it. It should make sure it doesn't overheat, so it might need a fan depending on how much current it's pushing. Or maybe it just has a bit of an aluminum heatsink.
> Follow-up 1: on the prius it must work on or off. it will have to have some smarts, and to be weak enough that the batteries can't get themselves in trouble. so that means it would have to stop charging by 80%, have a voltage clamp, it would need temp compensation, etc. also i know on older prius the pack was electrically split in two while off. check to see if that's still the case. this is a complex board. that's the point
> Follow-up 2: it's a prius prime with li-ion

## Assumptions

- **ASM-1** Prime roof: the flat-enough area is about 1.1 m (fore-aft) x 1.1 m. Toyota covered ~0.80 m2 of cells (180 W at 22.5 %) on the same roof for the Japanese Prius PHV, leaving the curved edges bare [S]; the baseline 56-cell field (0.86 m2 of cell) assumes a similar flat area (confirm: human)
- **ASM-2** Pack cell window 3.00-4.20 V (NMC-class chemistry, 25 Ah prismatic); pack 285-399 V. The car's own top-of-charge is NOT known (Q-13): no Toyota, Panasonic or teardown figure was found; the hardware layers assume it can be as low as 4.00 V/cell average [I] (confirm: human)
- **ASM-3** OCV at 80 % true SOC is 3.97-4.02 V/cell for NMC [I]; at the provisional 3.79 V/cell stop the pack is nearer 55-65 % [I]; re-set after Q-13 and calibrated on the car via CAN at commissioning (confirm: human)
- **ASM-4** Pack short-circuit current ~3.3 kA (95 cells x ~1.0 mOhm + busbars ~0.12 Ohm) [I] (confirm: human)
- **ASM-5** Main box in the cargo area; battery-end box next to the HV battery under the cargo floor [P: DM shows the pack in the luggage area] (confirm: human)
- **ASM-6** LV ground (PV-, PGND) bonded to chassis at the main box (EMC); conflicts with the C60 'positive ground' note (Q-7) (confirm: human)
- **ASM-7** The HV battery case is metal at chassis potential and isolated from the cells [P: DM p.10], so NTCs bolted to it are LV parts (confirm: human)
- **ASM-8** Pack thermal mass ~70 kJ/K (95 cells x ~0.75 kg x ~1 J/gK); case-to-cabin conductance ~10 W/K with the battery fan off [I] (confirm: human)
