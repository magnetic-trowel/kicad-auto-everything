- **DRC.** Final errors and their types are the same in both arms on all 4 boards. Unconnected items are equal on 3 boards. On paicorea they fall from 2 to 0, but its critical copper is identical in both arms (nothing kept), so that is bridge noise.
- **The KiCad gate is needed.** Without it, on lenaschimmel the tightened +5V copper sat 0.12 mm from J1/J5 pads where KiCad requires 0.2 mm. The rule port (`Space.need`) reads those pads' class (`kae_pin1`) as 0.12 mm; the grid router's raster margin had hidden that. The gate restores every group a KiCad error names.
- **Physics.** On aifryz the guard refused two decap groups. Taut shortened them, but the quoted loop did not improve within the tolerance. On the synthetic board, the buck's full-width input hop gets square rule-D entries that raise the hot loop from 5.2 to 7.4 nH, and the guard refuses them (test).
- **Run time.** Tightening plus gate takes 0–8 s of a 450–530 s run. Lenaschimmel's bridge took 388 s against 200 s for the grid arm, with the same 600 s budget. That is a repair round more; its final DRC is equal.
