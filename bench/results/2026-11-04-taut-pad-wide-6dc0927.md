- No neck and no hazard: no track on these boards is wider than its land (the one such entry, lenaschimmel IC4 pad 1, enters one 0.15 mm shape of a pad that is 0.4 mm wide merged).
- DRC by type no worse than the base on any board; errors and unconnected as the base on all 12.
- Worse than the base: metro-m7 one more oblique entry (C7 pad 2, 13°): pad_entry ends a near-square land's track on its centre (|w − h| ≤ 10 % of w), taut's own copy ended it (w − h)/2 off it, and the branch kept a different tail. Physics within 0.2 % of the base on every board.
- A first version (ad9df02) dropped pad_entry's "straight" target, which is the only one returned where it coincides with the axial or long target (a release point on the axis); taut then fell back on input tails: oblique/acute 455/97 against the base's 441/93, copper +94 mm. 6dc0927 keeps it.
