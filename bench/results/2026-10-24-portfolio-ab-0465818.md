# Restart portfolio on splits A+B at 0465818

Run on sk-tibia, 2026-10-24, KiCad 10.0.7, route tasks of splits A+B (33 boards), k = 3, 1800 s per
attempt, Freerouting fork heap 2 GB, kaebench scoring. Both arms at the same commit.

- single: one `kae.route.cli` attempt (last mile on), `-j 20`.
- portfolio: `kae.route.portfolio --parallel 4 --first-s 60`: waves of 4 differently seeded
  attempts in parallel, the wave budget doubling (60, 120, 240 s, …), the first clean attempt
  stopping the rest; `-j 5` (20 processes).

| arm | cp1 | cpk | clean attempts | DRC errors | wall s per attempt, median / p90 / max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| single | 0.909 | 1.000 | 91/99 | none | 10 / 154 / 1,626 |
| portfolio | **1.000** | **1.000** | **99/99** | none | 12 / 65 / 224 |

- Every portfolio attempt ended clean. Attempts launched per board: median 4, max 10. The
  winning wave was the 60 s one on 66 attempts, 120 s on 23 and 240 s on 10 (olimex dcdc-50-5-12,
  paw3395, lt6c on every attempt, siderakb-pmw3610 once).
- Winners come from deep in the seed sequence (paw3395 won with its 9th and 10th seeds): on
  these boards it is diversity of item order that closes the last connections, not a longer
  budget; the slowest portfolio attempt took 224 s against 1,626 s for a single one.
- Cost: up to 4 cores per board. kaebench's CPU measurement does not see processes inside its
  sandbox, so CPU is not compared here.

For context: Freerouting 2.5.0 on the human placement scored cp1 0.615 (A) and 0.35 (B) at
k = 10 ([baseline](2026-10-22-qualified-k10-baseline.md)).

## Mixed routers on split C (ff641dd) and the preference fix

`--router "kae.route.critical_cli:--planes cost --last-mile-soft" --router "kae.route.cli:--planes cost"`,
`--parallel 4 --first-s 300`: 12/12 clean, 0 errors, 25–451 s, but the bridge won all 12 because it
finishes first, so routed physics matched the bridge (critical + soft over mixed: cells 0.783,
decap 0.952). From the next commit, a clean result from a later-listed router no longer ends the
run at once: the first-listed router's running attempts finish first and their clean board wins.
