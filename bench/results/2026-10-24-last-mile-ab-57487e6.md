  clean on every attempt: 20 → 29; only siderakb-pmw3610 is never clean.
- **After critical copper** it ran on 31 attempts and closed 11 (121 of 204 joins): the locked
  critical copper blocks the grid router's paths on noisecard, paw3222, paw3395, badjeff-pmw3610,
  tuxkeychain-expansion and siderakb-pmw3610.
- **Physics** (`crit_compare`, median ratio): last mile costs nothing (all medians 1.0 against the
  same arm without it). Critical copper keeps its edge: decap 0.961 of human with last mile vs
  1.155 for the bridge (0.745 critical/bridge), rail resistance 0.97 vs 1.14.
- **Time** was not the limit at 1800 s; runs ended on the router's own stop.
