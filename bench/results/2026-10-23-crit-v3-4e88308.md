## Findings to act on

1. **Plane layers carry foreign tracks.** On the C6 incomplete boards, signal track on inner
   plane layers is 1,362 mm for crit and 781 mm for the bridge, against 282 mm for the human
   boards; return-coverage findings follow it. The bridge should keep signals off plane layers
   unless no other route exists.
2. **Completion on split C.** The critical copper blocks Freerouting on dense boards; island and
   completion margins (the `c` arm) are the lead.
3. The `kae-fr-v3-ab` time column is inflated by a stray overlapping scope on its first arm.
