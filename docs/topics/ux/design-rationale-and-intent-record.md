# Design rationale and the intent record ("the why")

**TL;DR**
- **Classic design-rationale capture failed on capture effort and retrieval:** captured rationale answered only 41% of designers' questions [S].
- **LLM-drafted rationale has high recall (0.63–0.72) but low precision (about 0.27),** so every claim needs a provenance tag [P].
- **2026 systems converge on four intent mechanisms:**
  - intent as checkable named relations (RAC);
  - structured diagnoses (evidence, hypothesis, falsifier);
  - audit trails (stored prompts, ledgers, lineage);
  - the design as version-controlled, deterministically regenerated code.

  [P]
- **In our system the committed relation bundle *is* the intent record.** Each relation links source evidence → planner diagnosis → checker verdict and margin. Explanations are generated by **walking those links**, not composed afterwards [I].
- An agent that logs structured rationale **at decision time**, anchored to board objects, removes the historical capture and retrieval barriers [I].

### Rationale literature

| Work | Finding | Source |

| IBIS / QOC (gIBIS 15 years on) | Capture overhead and interruption of flow; early successes didn't scale; retrieval from large, decontextualized argument records is hard | [S] |
| LLM-generated design rationale (ACM TOSEM 2025) | 100 architecture problems, 5 LLMs: **precision 0.267–0.278, recall 0.627–0.715**. Of arguments the experts didn't mention, 64.45–69.42% were helpful and 1.59–3.24% potentially misleading | [2504.20781](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20781) [P] |
| ADR generation | Naming "Architectural Decision Record" in the prompt improved zero-shot output, still below human level | [2403.01709](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.01709) [S] |

### PCB-specific need
- **Diode:** review is "reconstructing the intent of the engineer", and "intent lives outside the file" ([Diode](https://blog.diode.computer/hardware-review)) [V].
- **DeepPCB on RL:** it "can't articulate the underlying principle" [V].
- **Reuse:** you must work out "which layout decisions mattered" when reusing reference circuits (KiCad forum) [S].
- **StationX:** "keep the AI's inferences labelled as inferences, or the workflow starts laundering guesses into 'facts'" [P, anecdote].
- **Gaps:** no standard PCB design-intent schema exists, and no user study of whether engineers trust LLM layout explanations.

## Recommendations for our system

1. **Intent sidecar** next to `.kicad_pcb` (e.g. `board.intent.json`; schema owned by [../architecture/constraint-ir.md](../architecture/constraint-ir.md)) [I]. For each relation it holds:
   - the relation;
   - its source: datasheet KG node and page, user requirement, recipe, or corpus prior;
   - the planner diagnosis that introduced it;
   - the checker verdict and measured margin;
   - accepted / rejected / relaxed status and who decided.
2. **Explanations walk these links.** "Why is C12 here?" returns the relation `near(C12, U3.VIN, tight)`, sourced from TPS62933 p.X ("as close as possible") plus the corpus prior, with the realized margin (2.3 mm) and the measured loop area.
3. **Object-anchored rationale log (IBIS/QOC-lite):** issue, options considered, criteria, choice and evidence, attached to the parts, nets or zones affected. Captured automatically at action time, searchable, surviving edits, exportable as a design-review document (CC-BY-4.0 docs).
4. **Provenance tags on every claim:** measured / rule / datasheet / estimate / inference. LLM-drafted rationale must not be shown untagged, given its precision of about 0.27.
5. **Store prompts before calls,** with a per-role ledger and lineage logs for reproducibility.
6. **Residual-risk list** at each handoff: what was not checked and what was assumed.
7. **Design as code where possible,** so the board regenerates deterministically from intent plus the solver version plus seeds.
8. The relation bundle re-solves after ECOs and resizes without LLM calls (RAC: 89.5% vs 65.6%), so intent persists through change.

## Conflicts and open questions

- There is no standard PCB intent schema and no user-trust study of PCB explanations.
- How should non-checkable preferences (aesthetics) be recorded? Possibly as house-style preference records learned from edits.
