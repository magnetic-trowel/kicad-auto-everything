# Closing the last open connections: rule fixes, restarts, placement screening, BGA escape

**Status:** worked for most items (combined on branch `rules-merge3`, merged to main 2026-11-01; not yet measured as a whole against the merit methods' kae-complete 78d3de7, an A/B is running; the BGA window repair is on its own branch); did not work for the items in [what did not work](#what-did-not-work).
**First published:** 2026-10-28 to 2026-11-01 on the branches named per item below. The combined, tested code is branch `rules-merge3` ([6d3ee11](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/6d3ee113abe619bc72dab19830c0558725bb2e49)).

The goal of this round: route 100% of the connections, with no DRC error beyond what the board had before routing (and, for methods that place, beyond the human layout's own), and where the board's own rules make that impossible, say why. Everything is judged by `kicad-cli` DRC on the re-opened board. Scoring was not changed. Numbers are open connections (unconnected items) and DRC errors summed over boards and seeds; most runs are 1-3 seeds, and run-to-run noise is about ±10-30% per board, so per-board differences below that are noise.

Benchmarks used:
- **merit route/joint:** 213 merit boards, the restart wrapper (`tools/kaeres/restart.py`, parallel 4, resume 0.5) at the merit budget 900 s × sqrt(footprints / 40).
- **merge2-eval:** 14 boards routed on the human placement and 20 boards placed and routed, the boards of the congestion, DRC and outline studies, 3 seeds.
- **near-miss set:** the 11 merge2-eval route boards that still had 1-12 open connections.

## How the failures were found

Agents compared our routings with the human routings of the same boards, read the router code, and classified every remaining open connection:
- **(a)** a path exists in our copper, or after ripping 1-2 nets: a closing or search failure;
- **(b)** a path exists only after ripping 3-30 nets: congestion; the human layout proves the capacity;
- **(c)** our placement makes it impossible;
- **(d)** no path even with all our copper removed: a board rule, a kae default, or geometry.

On the near-miss set the 114 open connections split a 27 / b 55 / d 32. On the place-and-route set, 347 open split a 32 / zone islands 52 / b 74 / c ~189. On the dense BGA boards, 25-50% were class (a). The classification scripts are in `tools/research/routing/close-nearmiss/` (branch `close-nearmiss`).

## What did not work

Each is off by default where the code was kept.

- **Re-running Freerouting with other seeds.** It is deterministic; restarts were duplicates and were removed from the merit methods.
- **Region re-routes for congestion** (class b). Ripping every net within 2-4 mm of an open connection and re-routing through Freerouting made every round worse (fm4dd 3 → 7-35). A grid PathFinder over the same regions never converged (8-15 conflicts left). `close-nearmiss` [6329417](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/6329417739c07be60cd609ef86ff24b7714112be), `region_level` off.
- **Negotiated re-route of a whole BGA window.** nekoink U100, 208 nets: conflicts 110 → 17, but open connections 51 → 104. `close-bga-region` [4ed6bc6](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/4ed6bc60f7222c40ef070310d489217b49ce1345), `region_mode=full`.
- **Laying the escape plan before Freerouting.** gecko5 54 → 241-269 open, nekoink 91 → 144-176; even the plan's vias alone slowed Freerouting's first pass. The plan and certificate are reported; the fixed dog-bones stay the default (`escape_lay=plan` to lay it). [fddaabb](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/fddaabbc376c482e48ff053fcf9e923a538ca01c).
- **No-via keepout inside fine-pitch pin rings.** 7 → 17 open on 3 boards, 1 seed (a fourth, atlantis, improved 8 → 6; all four 15 → 23): the net-class via does not fit outside a 0.4 mm-pitch ring, and humans put vias there on 33 of 82 exposed-pad parts. `close-topology` [2466bb6](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/2466bb63cc831a4d795c12a3e38d055461c07358), `pin_fields` off.
- **Feeding bus nets to Freerouting in pin order.** 53 → 51 open, noise. Same commit, `bus_order` off.
- **Explicit Steiner branching.** Not needed: our router already makes T-junctions as often as the human (1.49 vs 1.48 branch points per multi-pad net, the same length over the minimum spanning tree). Scripts `tools/research/routing/close-topology/` ([414bdac](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/414bdacd3d1cd4bc46bea182be29e5c99ccc3f1b)).
- **Routing nets that failed earlier first.** A failure history that put those nets first made later attempts route alike: 83 → 100-104 open (kata0510 2/2/3 → 5/5/7). `close-nearmiss` [6d2dfa2](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/6d2dfa2177b49cc25556af4ed9041a32afbe6cbe) [d284ba7](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/d284ba77ef3f26e50e188a7026b146a24a255b3f), `--history` off.
- **Joint repair loop as the resume step.** 215 vs 214 open on 5 boards; its gain on lenaschimmel came from more time for the last mile, not from moving parts.
- **Parts turned in 45° steps** (`--set angle_step=45`, [9ef3788](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/9ef37883c19a7acb3e5beef47c62503a149e3dfc)). lenaschimmel had 199 mask bridges on every seed and can485 18-34, but the baseline already carried about 97 excess errors on lenaschimmel; partly confounded by a cold numba cache, see below.
- **More congestion (RUDY) weight in the placer.** RUDY does not rank placements within a board (mean Spearman 0.13 with opens after 240 s; a 60 s route gets 0.53).
- **Strict planes on every seed.** z280 86 → 2 open against the same escape base (600 s single runs), but heypcb kestrel 28 → 53 on seed 1 (means 25 → 57), whose human routes 1.6 m on its planes. Hence the odd-seed policy. `rules-power` [454e3d6](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/454e3d63c8cbcad0cac7884301571d18bafb96d1).
- **Regressions caught while merging.** A "mask reach" rule (protocentral 5 → 12-13 open) and a 3 mm plane-escape window (fm4dd 5 → 10) were simplified away; transactional rip-up alone hurt heypcb until it went greedy when a reroute follows.
- **Outer-layer cost parity** (`outer_surcharge=0`): moved gecko5's bottom-layer length 443 → 1,202 mm, but the open count changed within noise.
- **pnr placements routed by the kae bridge** (hybrid, PR #18): worse than kae staged (resume) on 104-109 of the 213 merit boards, better on 19-22; cp1 0.254-0.263 vs 0.291, and never clean where no kae method is (experiment hybrid-213). It does beat pnr's own router (clean boards 47-49 → 54-56).
- **More Freerouting time on dense boards.** nekoink plateaus at about 75 open; sdi-mipi needs 1,800 s for one pass.

## Things learned about the benchmark

- KiCad's DRC reports `solder_mask_bridge` nondeterministically with more than one thread. Single-thread scoring would need new baselines; not changed.
- A host-wide Freerouting slot limit (`KAE_FR_SLOTS`, about 23 on the benchmark host) made attempts wait 50-682 s and caused 2-hour job timeouts; studies used private slots.
- A numba cache keyed to a different code path makes the placer compile inside its time budget and place in a rush (lenaschimmel: 1 vs 199 mask bridges). Comparisons must warm the cache for the code they run.
- 112 of 159 inputs already have DRC errors before routing, so "clean" (0 errors) and "routed fully" (0 open, no error beyond the input's) are different targets.

## Untested ideas from this round

- Raise the price of nets that failed earlier instead of fixing their order (the history done softly).
- An ordered fan-out planner for fine-pitch QFN rings (pin-ordered L-shapes to a staggered via column, the human pattern on fm4dd), and the smallest via the board's minimum allows where room is scarce; about 30 of the 114 near-miss opens are stranded QFN pins.
- Zone edits (outline notches, keepout areas) and thermal-spoke moves inside the BGA window repair, and richer flexibility measures (escape options per ball, best-vs-second-best route cost, plane cut tests).
- The research survey's top picks: closed-form ring and gap cut checks, MAPF-LNS2-style window repair with floating terminals, and a duality lower bound from negotiation prices ([ranked list](../../topics/routing/bga-routing-ranked-ideas.md)).
