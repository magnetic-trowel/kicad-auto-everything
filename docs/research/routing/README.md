# Research: routing

Scope: index of our routing methods (package `kae.route`): Freerouting as inner engine behind a constraint-complete translation, KiCad DRC on the re-opened board as the only judge, our exact grid router for local work. Facts live on the linked pages.
Keys: cp1 (share of boards clean on attempt 1), cpk (clean on any of k attempts), A+B (33 boards of splits A and B), C6 (six hard split-C boards with switching cells), merit method, benchmark era

Results from different benchmark eras (task specs, engine versions) are not comparable.

## Eras and caveats

- Bridge, last mile, critical copper, portfolio, dead ends: 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-25, branch `claude/vigilant-mayer-i91rrr`, A+B / C6 / 112-board suite.
- Closing open connections: 2026-10-28 to 2026-11-01, merit 213 boards, merge2-eval, near-miss set; open-connection and excess-error sums, not cp1/cpk.
- Merit 500-board comparison of the kae methods: pending (merit-500r3 / merit-500r3k).
