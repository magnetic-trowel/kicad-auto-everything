# kae exact placement legality and the KiCad DRC loop

Scope: defensive publication of the exact placement legality model, human gate, KiCad DRC loop, protection order, silkscreen and board-label passes. Not here: placer pipeline -> [kae placer](kae-placer.md).
Keys: ExactModel, findings_at, repair, diagnose, human gate, humangate.py, kicad_feedback, _kicad_loop, pair separation, avoid-geometry, edge-clearance growth, fixed_only, safety net, kicad_written_round, protection order, w_order, OrderGuard, fix_order, silkscreen, w_silk, fix_silk, refine_silk_text, silk_text_at, SILK_AREA, board labels, LabelTerm, w_label, copper text glyphs

**Status:** worked (board-label term partial).
**First published:** 2026-10-23, commit [b296354](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/b296354bce9e3d560755448ea119ad81912b5349) on branch `claude/vigilant-mayer-i91rrr` (human gate [ea517f9](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/ea517f91c79166002d7e2a1d88a6849f5dae6fab), KiCad feedback [c6ae9be](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/c6ae9be0d935bea6e481386ee2863f35a3c62206)).

The [kae placer](kae-placer.md) ends with a legality stage that reproduces what KiCad's DRC checks for a placement on exact polygons, repairs violations by moving parts, then runs kicad-cli DRC on the written board and turns KiCad's remaining complaints into constraints for another round. Passes for protection order, silkscreen and board labels share the same machinery. Split C went from 66/73 legal to 72/72, and the exact model reports no finding type that KiCad does not on any of the 112 human layouts.

## Idea and how it works

**Exact model** (`place/exact.py`, `ExactModel`). Holds each part's real shapes at its pose and checks: courtyard overlap per side (with KiCad's 5 um deflation), pad clearance with net-class and pad-local overrides, `hole_clearance` and `hole_to_hole`, copper to edge, items in rule areas, solder-mask bridges (including mask-only apertures and pad openings over other nets' zone fills, following KiCad 10's `drc_test_provider_solder_mask.cpp`), footprint copper and mask graphics, board copper graphics and board copper text as KiCad glyph outlines (fetched through the [pcbnew bridge](kae-board-model.md)). The copper-text check was added after pic-programmer's ZIF socket landed on "1=>>". Candidate pairs come from clearance envelopes. `findings_at(i, X, Y, K)` prices a trial pose without committing it. `repair` moves each culprit to the nearest grid-free and exactly clean spot, evicting smaller parts, with re-seed and lookahead escalation; `diagnose` reports why a part is stuck.

### Human gate

`tools/research/place/humangate.py`: read each human reference board (human poses) into a Problem, run the exact model on every part, compare finding types with KiCad's DRC on the same board. A type kae reports where KiCad reports none is a false positive (it would make the placer avoid spots KiCad allows). Silk gated the same way where it is an error. The gate must stay at A 13/13, B 20/20, C 79/79 clean. Every new rule (text knockout margins, mask-only apertures, copper text, silk glyphs) had to pass it. The same discipline applies to estimates: an estimate that flags human boards the human routes fine is not used.

### KiCad loop, order, silk, labels


**KiCad loop** (`kicad_feedback`, `cli._kicad_loop`). After writing, kicad-cli DRC runs on the board and placement errors become constraints the next repair honours:
- pair separations: current distance + a step, growing while KiCad still objects;
- avoid-geometry: keep a part off a given text or graphic (by uuid);
- edge-clearance growth: +0.05 mm per report, blaming only the part whose copper KiCad names (blaming the neighbour left paw3222 illegal);
- findings that involve only fixed items count as `fixed_only` and are not the placer's.

Up to 6 rounds; a kicad-cli run killed by a signal (rc < 0, seen on buspirate5) is retried up to 2 times on a freshly staged copy; a **safety net** keeps the best KiCad-judged placement (fewest placement errors) and writes it back if the loop ends worse or on a repair KiCad never judged (`kicad_written_round`). Since 2026-10-25 a round that moves parts re-runs the silk pass (on a fixed pic-programmer, 40 evictions had left 18 silk errors).

**Protection order.** Brief protection chains (connector -> TVS -> series part -> protected IC pin) become rules "the protection part is nearer the connector than the protected part". The annealer prices violation distance / mean part size (`w_order`). `OrderGuard` forbids any repair or silk move that raises a part's broken-rule count. The final pass `fix_order` moves, per broken rule, the protection part (else the protected part) to the nearest grid-free, exactly clean spot with fewer broken rules; all moves together may cost at most +3% physics, <= 8 priced spots per part. This is a design choice, not learned: only 60% of human layouts put the TVS nearer the connector.

**Silkscreen.** Only where the project makes `silk_overlap` or `silk_over_copper` an error (KiCad's default is a warning). Other parts' silk and pad mask openings are modelled with KiCad's own glyph outlines fetched from pcbnew (`refine_silk_text`), per quarter turn (`silk_text_at`: KiCad keeps text readable, so a part turned 180 degrees does not show its text turned; before this, sonde-xilinx's model saw 1 of KiCad's 5 conflicts, now all); overlap threshold `SILK_AREA` 1e-5 mm^2 (KiCad reports overlaps of 5e-4 mm^2: a glyph stroke over a silk line). A soft annealer term `w_silk` (default 1; overlap / mean part area on a packed silk grid) and a final pass `fix_silk`: the smaller part of each conflict moves within +3% physics and +10% local HPWL, without breaking order rules, <= 3 rounds. Reference text cannot be moved because the scorer accepts footprint poses only.

**Board labels** (`place/labels.py`). Infer which part each board-level silk text names: `ref` (token = a reference), `pin` (token = pin function or net of a connector-like part's pad), `keyword` (PWR, ACT, RESET and the like: >= 3 chars in an LED's or switch's net names; ON/OFF for <= 2 switches), `row` (>= 3 numbered labels aligned within 0.3 mm, matched in numeric order to exactly one family of LEDs, switches or connectors with consecutive numbers). `LabelTerm` pulls a part's labelled pads to its label with a dead zone (one third of the row pitch along a row, preserving order). On by default only for `row`, the one kind at ≥ 90% precision.

**Severities** (`place/problem.py`: `FIXED_ERROR_CHECKS`, `Problem._severities`; option `place_severity`, `kae place --severity`, project text variable `KAE_PLACE_SEVERITY`). Until 2026-11 legality followed each board's `.kicad_pro`: a check the project set to warning or ignore was dropped, so on those boards parts were placed on courtyard-free pads, copper-to-edge became 0 and rule areas were ignored. That matched the scorer's `legal`, not physics. Now:

- `strict_then_level_c` (default since 2026-11-05): the severity ladder below; `strict` first, `level_c` only when strict cannot fit.
- `level_c`: courtyard overlap, copper-to-edge, rule areas, clearance, shorts and hole clearance are errors whatever the project says. On a project that waives `courtyards_overlap`, each courtyard is taken in by 0.15 mm (IPC-7351 Level B 0.25 mm excess minus Level C 0.10 mm, [dfm-fab-limits](../../topics/rules/dfm-fab-limits.md)), so neighbours' margins may overlap but no part sits on another; elsewhere it is `strict`. The KiCad loop runs DRC on a staged copy whose project has the checks at error (`route/drc.promote_severities`), except waived courtyards, which KiCad cannot check partially (it skips a negative `courtyard_clearance`), so the exact model alone checks them and the scorer's `legal_strict` still counts the margin overlaps.
- `strict`: drawn courtyards everywhere.
- `board`: the old behaviour.
- Kept as waivers in every mode: custom `.kicad_dru` rules with their own severity; KiCad's own exemptions from copper-to-edge (castellated pads, `Pad.fab`, and edge-connector `connect` pads, as in `drc_test_provider_edge_clearance.cpp`); a violation whose items all belong to one footprint (its own cut-out against its own pads, a resistor array's pads), which no move fixes (`exact._intrinsic`).
- Every downgraded check gives a hazard in the place report: `project_check_kept_strict` (info; warn under `level_c`) or, under `board`, `project_check_ignored` (warn).

Why: the human layouts of boards that waive a check do break it. With the check at error, KiCad's DRC flags 25 of 30 sampled boards that ignore courtyards, 20/25 for copper-to-edge, 14/20 for clearance and 7/11 for shorts. Of 490 courtyard pairs on those boards, 233 overlap by less than 10% of the smaller courtyard (margins), 153 by 10-50% and 104 by more than half (parts under modules, encoders and other parts). Copper-to-edge waivers come mostly from footprints' own cut-outs (1,298 of 2,464 findings; exempt above) and connector pads at the edge (810, 25 boards). Clearance and short waivers are mostly footprint-internal (hot-swap MX/Choc sockets, net ties, resistor arrays) or routing. On the 64 merit-500 or sampled boards that waive courtyards, utilization rises from 0.05-0.75 to above 1 on 8 boards under `strict` and on 5 under `level_c` (dense drone ESCs, a compute-module carrier): those cannot be placed legally in full, and the report says which parts are stuck. Why `level_c` is the default (smoke, kae place 300 s, seed 0, courtyard-waiving git4dcc decoders): d12 is legal in all three modes with HPWL 186 mm (`board`), 228 (`level_c`) and 362 (`strict`); d24 is legal with `board` (184) and `level_c` (297) but not with `strict` (57 courtyard and 31 short errors left by KiCad: the board does not fit). The A/B of `strict`, `level_c`, `board` and the body modes is [legality-ab.sh](../../../tools/kaeres/experiments/legality-ab.sh).

**Severity ladder** (`place/pipeline.py`: `place`, `LADDER`, `placement_errors`, `rung_key`). The decide-ab follow-up A/B (legality-ab2-place-down: 113 boards whose projects waive a check, k=2, kae place at 680c30e, scorer legal under the real rules = `legal_strict`) gave: `board` (old) 18% legal_strict, 5 boards with placement shorts; `level_c` + escalate (the then default) 28%, 6 boards; `strict` + escalate 46%, 12 boards. Strict keeps courtyards as drawn and wins legality where the board has room; where it has not, the placer piles parts into shorts (git4dcc d24, solderparty rp2040-stamp, crimier altmode-friend, apfaudio soldiercrab, dpmj compute-module carrier). So the default is now both, in order:

1. `strict`, body relaxation off, in 55% of the time (`LADDER_STRICT_SHARE`; a small board's place budget ends sooner and leaves the rest for the next rung). Skipped, so that level_c gets the whole time, when the drawn courtyards cover more than the board on a side (`LADDER_MAX_UTIL` = 1.0: altmode-friend 1.03, d24 1.10, rp2040-stamp 1.06, openaio about 2; no waiving board over 1.0 was ever legal_strict under strict in the A/B, and ravens-b-io at 0.97 was, so the line is not lower).
2. If strict leaves any placement error, `level_c` from a fresh seed in the time left (at least 20 s, else strict is kept with a warn hazard), with `relax_bodies` as set (`escalate`: bodies freed only when legalization fails there too).
3. The kept result (`rung_rank`): a rung KiCad has judged beats one only the exact check has seen; between two judged rungs, KiCad's counts decide (no shorts, clearance or hole-clearance error; then fewer of those; then fewer placement errors). Ties keep strict.

Each rung's share includes its KiCad loop: placement stops `LADDER_KICAD_FRAC` (15%, at least 30 s) before the share ends (`hard_end` caps escalation, final legalization, the closed loop, its per-part diagnosis, and the order and silk passes), and the KiCad loop runs in the rest. The first ladder A/B (legality-ladder-place-down, 113 boards, k=2, 5ce841b) had the strict rung legal_strict 0.437 against strict 0.46 and level_c 0.275, but more shorts than level_c (9 boards and 212 shorts against 8 and 88), from two faults this fixes. On opendrone openaio the strict rung's placement used 930 of its 966 s, its KiCad loop never ran, and its 3 exact-check electrical findings (the exact check stops at 60 findings) beat level_c's 11 KiCad errors; the scorer found 38-55 shorts. On tinyvision pico-ice the diagnosis of stuck parts (37-70 s a part, unbounded; now cut off at the rung's end) ran the run past its timeout, so it was scored on a partial output.

The trigger is kae's own final verdict, not a floorplan or legalization failure: the exact check's findings plus KiCad's errors the placer can repair on the board as written (`kicad_verdict`, after the KiCad rounds; `kae.cli` judges each rung inside its own time share). In the A/B every strict run whose KiCad verdict had a short or clearance error between movable parts was one kae already called illegal (`placement_illegal`). The 34 of 178 strict runs kae called legal that the scorer saw shorts or clearance errors on (12 with shorts) had them only between fixed items or inside one footprint (KiCad's `fixed_only` = all its errors), the same in every arm, so no fallback could change them. A proven-infeasible floorplan was not used as a trigger, because strict boards with an infeasible floorplan still ended legal (black-fx rp2040-mouse). Replaying the A/B's own runs (strict arm, then the level_c + escalate run of the same board and seed where strict was illegal): 102 of 218 runs legal_strict (47%; strict 47%, level_c 28%), and placement shorts on 16 runs, as many as level_c's 16 against strict's 29. The fallback would have fired on 40 of the 218 runs (18%).

Order: Level C courtyards come before body relaxation, because a relaxed body can sit on another part (`body_overlap`, block) while Level C only lets margins touch. On a project that keeps `courtyards_overlap` an error, level_c is strict, so the ladder is one strict rung with `relax_bodies` as set. A single `Problem.build` cannot ladder; given the default mode (the joint loop's windows) it is `level_c`. Report: `severity_used`, `severity_ladder` (each rung's errors by type, time and HPWL, the fallback's reason, the rung kept), and the hazard `severity_fallback`: info when level_c was kept, with each pair of parts whose drawn courtyards share margin at the final poses; warn when level_c still left courtyard overlaps; info when level_c was tried and strict kept.

**Bodies** (`Problem.relax_bodies`, option `relax_bodies`). A footprint without a courtyard occupies its body (the box of its fab drawing and pads). `relax_bodies` frees through-hole ones to their pads, on the grounds that "KiCad does not check bodies". It used to run up front on any board over 95% utilization and as the last escalation step. On merit-500 the 95% trigger fires on 64 of 499 boards, 52 of them with parts to relax; the escalation fired on 6 of 145 joint-loop boards, and in 3 of them kae's output then had a USB receptacle over 0402 parts, overlapping axial resistors, and a USB-C receptacle over diodes and a button, none of which the human layout has. A body on the board cannot share space; only a part standing off on headers can, and kae cannot tell which. So the up-front trigger is gone: `relax_bodies` is `escalate` by default, relaxing only when legalization fails; `off` never relaxes and `auto` is the old behaviour. The decide-ab tier-1 A/B (52 dense boards, k=2) chose `escalate` over `off`: same legal rate (0.27 vs 0.25), and `off` cost 10 pp of probe completion (0.82 to 0.72) and left as many boards with shorts. Escalate was within 2 pp of the old behaviour. Every relaxed part gets a hazard: `body_overlap` (block) when its body ends over another part at the final poses, else `body_relaxed` (info).

Split-C placement sweeps across versions (`csweep.py`, 600 s, seed 0), the human gate on all 112 boards, silk A/B on the 5 projects that make silk an error, label precision on the 91 tasks that carry board silk text.

**Physics cost of the physics-blind stages** (2026-11, decide-placement 15d29fd). The legalizer, the exact repair and the KiCad repair choose spots by displacement only: no physics term, no decap distance. Only the order and silk passes carry the +3% physics bound. `physics_trace` in the place report now records the linear physics after each stage. On 14 merit boards (25-141 footprints; human routing stripped; `kae place --time 150`, seed 0; of 16 boards, one has no physics structures and one failed in the pcbnew writer on legacy footprint ids), 11 kept their annealed physics. Three lost it:

| board | anneal -> final (Σu) | decap Σu | stage | final legal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ubertooth | 17.9 -> 23.0 (+28%) | 14.1 -> 19.2 | grid legalizer, one window | no |
| can48 | 1.63 -> 3.74 (+130%) | 1.43 -> 3.54 | exact repair (81 -> 30 findings, one reseed) | no |
| 1.3in TFT bonnet | 0.41 -> 1.52 (+274%) | 0.41 -> 1.52 | exact repair (87 -> 30 findings) | no |

Summed over the 14 boards: 59.5 -> 67.8 (+14%), all of it decap loops. The "kept only if not costlier than the seed" guard runs before the exact repair, and in ubertooth's case the legalized candidate still beat the seed (36.1). It concentrates where repair already struggles: all three ended with KiCad errors left. Proposed fix, not built: a physics tie-break in `exact.move_clean`, which takes the cheapest of the first few exactly clean candidates within ~2 mm of the nearest, using the `phys` callable `fix_order` already takes. A matching term for members of physics macros would go in `legalize._window_solve` (rigid offsets to the macro anchor, as `legalization.md` recommends with "decap distance"). Measure either with `physics_trace` before an A/B.

Residuals: one paw3222 copper_edge_clearance on one attempt (fixed in [644b268](https://github.com/magnetic-trowel/kicad-auto-everything/commit/644b26865698f2be6d2e252cfc5d896dcb6fc7f3)); a pcbnew zone-fill assert on pingdevkit is pcbnew's.

## Verdict

Worked. Two things made it work: the human gate (a model of KiCad's DRC is only trustworthy when it never flags what KiCad accepts on real boards) and the closed loop with KiCad, which catches what the model misses and a safety net that never returns a worse placement. Labels are partial: only numbered rows are precise enough to turn on. The checks were built against KiCad 10.0.7; new KiCad DRC providers need new rules and a new gate run.
