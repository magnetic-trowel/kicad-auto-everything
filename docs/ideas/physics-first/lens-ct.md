Scope: ideas from reading Kirchhoff's laws as the exact, metric-free half of Maxwell and the board as a cell complex with cycles, cuts and holes (CT1–CT32). Not here: principles CT-P1–CT-P7 and computed checks -> [topics/physics/lens-ct](../../topics/physics/lens-ct.md).

## A. Cuts, holes and sheets

- **CT1** One cut-flux price for every cut (*sharp, S; CT-P5, CT-P7*). Any line of removed conductor (slit, merged antipads, keep-out edge, routing channel through a pour, shield-can seam, heat-spreader cut) costs ΔE = κ·Φ²/σ, Φ = intercepted flux (current, return current, induced shielding current or heat), σ = sheet conductance; κ = π/4 interior, π/2 touching an insulating boundary (exact, mirror symmetry), × F(x) = (8/π²)·ln sec(πx/2)/x² for a cut taking fraction x of the local strip; ∞ for a self-closing cut or a severed terminal. One law with exact constants replaces the empirical edge factors of XD2, TM5, FE2. [C strip] confirms both ln sec forms and the exact notch factor 2 ([D1](../../topics/physics/lens-ct.md#3-five-derivations)). Test: openEMS leakage of a slotted can vs Δψ² of the induced current.
- **CT2** Crack-augmented adjoint price map (*sharp, S; CT-P7*). Add a crack term to the adjoint price map (SIM24, OP38): from the base solve read normal current J_n along each candidate cut, expand in Chebyshev U polynomials, charge ΔP = πa²R_s·Σc_k²/k × ligament factor F; exact Woodbury update (rank k = cut edges) only for finalists (k-edge form of lens CD's attribution ÷ (1 − leverage), [lens-cd](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md)). A cut's cost does not vanish with width, so area derivatives miss it: first-order adjoint underestimates by ≈ aspect ratio ([solver-ladder slot](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md#6-caching-and-incremental-re-extraction)). [C pour]: crack form within 9% there and within 2× on all 36 random cuts ([D1](../../topics/physics/lens-ct.md#3-five-derivations)). Lens SY's (π/4)·R_s·Δψ_u·Δψ_λ extends it to any objective via the adjoint stream function ([lens-sy](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md)). Test: 1,000 cuts on 10 real pours vs exact cost.
- **CT3** Euler-characteristic event log for references (*sharp, S; CT-P5*). Track b0 (islands) and b1 (holes) of every declared reference layer across router/optimizer iterations; log each change with location and price at once (new hole by CT1 vs critical nets above; new island by its quarter-wave and connection). Stops perforation inside the loop for the cost of counting polygon rings (PC8 finds it after routing). Test: Freerouting runs on the qualified suite; events vs post-hoc PLANE-PERF findings.
- **CT4** Persistence margin of reference webs (*combo, S; CT-P5*). Per antipad cluster under a critical net, clearance growth δ* at which neighbouring holes merge into one slot (half the narrowest web) = topological robustness margin vs etch and registration (TM31); price the merged slot by CT1. FE4, XD95 find narrow webs; this says how much process variation turns a sieve into a slot. Test: σ-margin of 20 BGA breakouts; slot appearance in erode-dilate sweeps.
- **CT5** Seams and apertures along shielding-current streamlines (*combo, M; CT-P7 from DC pours to shields*). For shield cans, enclosure seams and plane slots near cables solve the induced surface current once at the band of concern; orient seams/slots along ψ contours. Aperture leakage follows CT1: longest dimension, not area, sets leakage; the λ/20 slogan ignores orientation. Test: openEMS can with a slot at 0°, 45°, 90° to the induced current.
- **CT6** Shorted-turn critic (*new, S; CT-P5*). Enumerate H1 generators of each net's copper (pour holes, closed rings, via-closed loops across layers); flag those linking flux of a magnetic part (inductor/transformer axis, stray dipole) or a hot loop; report EMF jωΦ and circulating current; fix: radial slit. Each hole is one circulation mode driven by its flux; no PC critic sees closed rings; magnetics practice always slits shields and bands. Test: planar-transformer and buck boards with pours around the inductor; PEEC circulating current with/without slit.
- **CT7** Notch, don't hole, under open-core magnetics (*sharp, S; CT-P5*). When copper under an open drum core must go, notch to the pour edge instead of a closed cut-out: a hole conserves AC flux (its rim is a shorted turn above its L/R corner); a notch has no rim. The Rohm/TI dispute (P13, PW46) compares only "solid" vs "removed". Test: PEEC of a plane with hole, notch, no cut under a drum core: L, loss, rim current.
- **CT8** Hole-mode augmented PEEC for perforated planes (*new, M; CT-P5, Hodge: image part + b1 harmonic modes*). Image plane plus one circulation unknown (harmonic mode) per hole instead of meshing; carries slot detours and shorted turns an image plane misses, at F1.5 cost (SIM16, SIM17 ignore or mesh holes). Test: vs meshed PEEC ([fe_peec_plane.py](../../../tools/research/physics/fe_peec_plane.py)) for 1–20 holes.

## B. The board–world cut and external cycles

- **CT9** Board–world charge ledger (*new, M; CT-P1 on the board boundary*). One-cable board: I_CM = −jω·Σ_i C_i,ext·V_i, V_i referenced to the world (circuit voltages: divide by 1 + jω·C_tot·Z_A, Z_A = CM path impedance). One electrostatic solve (outside at 1 V, all board conductors at 0 V) gives every C_i,ext by reciprocity; × node spectra from schematic simulation (DR1) = one ledger row per band with each conductor's share and phase. FE17, PC22 take parts one at a time and ignore phase. Test: openEMS board + 1 m cable, 5 layouts; I_CM vs ledger.
- **CT10** Antiphase charge balancing (*new, M; CT-P1*). Minimize |Σ C_i,ext·V_i(f)|² per band by pairing complementary dv/dt nodes (bridge legs, transformer winding ends, push-pull drains) with matched external capacitance: mirrored SW copper, symmetric heatsink, matched tab exposure. "Minimize SW area" treats nodes alone; a 10% mismatch leaves only one tenth of the single-ended current [I]. Test: full bridge, mirrored vs unmirrored SW copper, simulated CM current.
- **CT11** Close-locally operator with a current-division target (*combo, S; CT-P1, CT-P4*). For every displacement edge from a board node to an external body add an internal closing edge (strap to source node, Y-capacitor, shield layer) sized so the external share Z_local/(Z_local + Z_ext) meets each band's budget. [C cm]: a 10 nH strap to DC− gives −39 dB at 30 MHz but only −20 dB at 100 MHz; adding a 4.7 nF Y-capacitor cuts both further ([D2 table](../../topics/physics/lens-ct.md#3-five-derivations)). DR28, FE14 pick a bond node but neither size the local cycle nor say where it stops working. Test: motor-drive bench with the four bond options.
- **CT12** Extended-graph cycle census per band (*new, M; CT-P4*). Extended graph: board conductors (PC1 physics model), parasitic C above a threshold, chassis, heatsinks, cable shields and conductors, LISN or earth; minimum cycle basis per band; label cycles internal/external, driven/undriven; rank by current from one nodal solve: ground loops and CM paths get names. LA12 finds schematic loops only; dangerous loops close through realization and world. A minimum-|Z| spanning tree makes each chord's fundamental cycle its least-impedance closure: in the script's half-bridge bonding the heatsink to DC− moves the driven cycle from external (through the LISN) to internal [C cm]. Test: recover the documented CM path in 10 EMC failure write-ups.
- **CT13** Frequency persistence of the reference topology (*new, S; CT-P4, CT-P6*). Per tie between reference regions (net tie, capacitor, ferrite, single via) compute where parasitic edges change cycle structure: capacitive bridge C_par across tie inductance L_tie dominates above f = 1/(2π·√(L_tie·C_par)); a ferrite tie vanishes above its crossover. Barcode per region pair: a 1 nH tie under an island with 76 pF overlap to the next plane stops being a single point near 580 MHz [I]. Says where LA20/LA34 band assignments hold. Test: SPICE of 5 split-ground boards with plane-overlap capacitance.
- **CT14** Ground-loop resonance list (*new, S; CT-P4*). Every cycle of a parasitic C in series with an inductive tie is a series LC loop (split-plane tie + overlap C, heatsink strap + tab C, shield pigtail + cable C); list resonances and add to the frequency plan (S8, FE13), which covers cavities, slots and rings but not ground loops. Test: SPICE and openEMS of a split plane with one tie.
- **CT15** Closure type for every port (*sharp, S; CT-P1*). Replace LA1's yes/no "has a named return" with a closure type per port and band: conduction (Loop, budget nH), displacement inside the board (Node, budget pF to a named neighbour), displacement to the world (Antenna, budget µA CM). FastHenry's NaN for a port without conductive return is a property of magnetoquasistatics, not physics; a binary check calls an antenna a typing error. Test: classify 200 ports on 10 boards; every CM finding should come from the third class.
- **CT16** World node mandatory in back-annotation (*sharp, S; CT-P1*). The physical netlist for the layout invisibility index must contain a world node, chassis, heatsinks and cable conductors, each joined by CT9's C_ext; without external edges KCL on the board boundary forces zero CM current and the index is blind to emissions (DR7 splits ground but keeps the board closed). Test: LII with/without world node on the 48 V half-bridge worked thought experiment.
- **CT17** Test setups as relative cycles (*combo, M; CT-P4, relative cycles*). Add each compliance test's injection and return (ESD gun and strap, EFT clamp, BCI clamp, LISN and ground plane) to the extended graph; compute least-impedance injection-to-return path through the board; list board conductors shared with sensitive cycles. DR47 does ESD only; LearnEMC asks for "each test's current path" by hand. Test: predicted shared conductors vs failure sites in 10 immunity reports.

## C. Exclusion, necks and grounding topology

- **CT18** Block–cut exclusion certificate (*sharp, S; CT-P6*). Tarjan biconnected components on the copper conduction graph: a victim region whose block lies off every aggressor's source–sink path in the block–cut tree carries exactly zero DC aggressor current, no solve; fall back to DC solve (LA7, XD72) only without a certificate. Exact, linear-time vs a field solve per aggressor. Test: certificates vs DC solves on 20 mixed-signal boards.
- **CT19** Put the neck at the aggressor's stagnation point (*new, S; CT-P6 + aperture expansion*). A peninsula neck (star point) belongs where the aggressor's tangential field along the isolating slot is zero (stagnation point or equipotential): leakage falls from ≈ w² to ≈ w⁴ in neck width w. [C neck]: 1,300× less leakage at 1.6 mm ([D3](../../topics/physics/lens-ct.md#3-five-derivations)). DR26 optimizes width only and finds a 4 mm flank neck still leaks. Test: 2-D solves on 10 ADC layouts; measured offset on one board with a movable load.
- **CT20** Cycle-space grounding synthesis per band (*new, M; CT-P4, CT-P6*). Reference topology as design variable: per region pair choose ties (net tie, capacitor, ferrite, open) so each band's cycle space is a tree below f_x and a mesh above, subject to safety bonds; small MILP over tie types and positions with CT18 certificates and CT13 crossovers as constraints. RG19, LA34 state the rule; this synthesizes ties. Test: re-design 5 mixed-signal grounds; simulated noise vs single plane and hard split.
- **CT21** Homological return pairing (*new, M; CT-P4*). Which pin returns which signal: per band a signal's return is the least-impedance cycle through its edge in the extended graph; where it enters each part are its return pins; next-best cycles are alternatives. LA1 pairs from IBIS or naming without checking the copper. Test: agreement with pin-role pairing on 20 boards; each disagreement is a finding.

## D. Coupling and closure

- **CT22** Cycle-honest coupling ledger (*sharp, S; CT-P2*). Every coupling budget is on a closed victim cycle; the validator refuses an AC "voltage between two points" unless the victim's closing conductor is named; compute gauge-invariant M(aggressor cycle, victim cycle), use partial elements only to attribute it. [C closure]: the victim sees 30–96% of the shared segment's L_p, set by its closure distance ([D4](../../topics/physics/lens-ct.md#3-five-derivations)). LA6, LA21, DR47 charge the shared segment's partial L: only a bound here, not a bound in general. Test: PEEC victim-cycle EMF vs shared-L_p rows, 20 shared-return cases.
- **CT23** Victim closure as a design lever (*combo, S; CT-P2*). Route the victim's reference through the same flux tube as the aggressor's shared segment (ground via next to the TVS via, return beside the shared trace) so it sees L_p − M and bounces together with the aggressor; co-locating the closure is sometimes cheaper than separating paths. Test: TLP or ESD-gun measurements on two layouts with the same parts.
- **CT24** Plane-aware nesting pre-screen (*sharp, S; CT-P2, which surface carries the flux*). Per aggressor–victim loop pair: shared edge -> conductive coupling (exact flag); victim nested with no plane near -> strong; else metric decay. Over a close plane each conductor closes through its own image: nesting fixes only the sign of M, magnitude follows nearest-conductor distance ([C nest], [D5](../../topics/physics/lens-ct.md#3-five-derivations)). P17 ("no loop inside another") right on two-layer, misleading over planes. Test: rank correlation with PEEC M on 2-layer and 4-layer boards.
- **CT25** Filter-cut integrity (*new, S; CT-P4*). A filter is a cut vertex between noisy and clean sides; extract displacement edges across the same cut (C between input and output traces, pours, parts) and report isolation ceiling 1/(ωC_bypass·Z): Hubing's "bypassing filters" as a number; FILTER-POS checks part order, not bypass. Test: 2-D capacitance extraction and SPICE on 10 I/O filters.
- **CT26** KCL and KVL violation numbers as regime gates (*new, S; CT-P1, CT-P2*). Per node K_C = ω·C_par·|Z_node|; per loop K_L = ω·L_par/|Z_loop|; above a budget-relative threshold the schematic stops being true and the structure gets a C or L budget. Thresholds (shunt 160 kHz, feedback node 8 MHz): [CT-P1, CT-P2](../../topics/physics/lens-ct.md#1-principles). Types nodes/loops by physics where LA4 types them by role. Test: agreement with expert criticality maps, 10 boards.

## E. Joint state, critics and verification

- **CT27** Topological gate first, metric weight second (*combo, S; CT-P7*). Each critic first evaluates an exact discrete predicate (Betti number, cut membership, cycle overlap, winding or return word), computes metric magnitude only where the gate fires. 13 of 39 physics critics have such a core: RET-COV, RET-HOP, RET-VIA, RET-REFNET, PLANE-PERF, SPLIT-X, GND-ISLAND, TOPO, PWR-TRACE, KELVIN, FILTER-POS, ANT-KEEP, VIA-STUB. Gates are exact, cost ms; proxy thresholds give false negatives near cliffs. Test: gates alone on the mutation benchmark (PC29): recall and cost vs full critics.
- **CT28** Physics topological events in the joint state (*new, M; CT-P7*). Extend JPR2/JPR3 certificates with physics events: shadow entering a reference hole, change of a reference's b0 or b1, loop becoming nested, articulation point appearing/vanishing, new cycle in the reference network, closed ring around a magnetic part; adjoint gradients between events, event costs (CT1, CT18, CT6) at them. The chained-adjoint synergy (S1) is silently wrong across events. Test: joint SA with/without event pricing; accepted moves later rejected by critics.
- **CT29** Return word per critical net (*new, S; CT-P5*). Beside each critical net's routing homotopy class store the word of reference holes its shadow crosses: empty = continuous return; each letter a priced crossing (CT1, FE2); updates incrementally as parts move and explains itself ("USB_DP crosses slot S3 and antipad chain A7"), where RET-COV recomputes geometry and returns a percentage. Test: words vs RET-COV findings on the qualified suite.
- **CT30** Topological invisibility test (*new, M; CT-P4*). Per band contract parasitic edges with impedance far below the circuit's, delete those far above; layout is structurally invisible iff the result equals the schematic band graph; any extra cycle or merged node is a named structural parasitic (ground loop, CM path, sneak path). LII measures magnitude, not structure. Test: planted structural faults (extra ground loop, heatsink to chassis) detected; benign twins quiet.
- **CT31** Cells as minimum HF cuts (*combo, M; CT-P1 on cell boundaries*). Define DR22's cells by normalized minimum cut of the HF current graph (edge weight = band current or coupling); locality index (DR42) = Σ|I| across cut / Σ|I| inside. Template cells do not follow drawn copper. Test: cut-defined cells vs expert partitions and an EMI proxy, 10 boards.
- **CT32** Ampère-loop observables in full-wave runs (*new, S; CT-P3*). In openEMS or Palace measure net current through declared cuts (cable bundles, cell and zone boundaries) as ∮H·dl on probe loops: mesh-robust, metric-free calibration observables (CM current, cell escape); port-voltage CM estimates depend on where the reference is taken. Test: agreement with port-current CM on calibration coupons.
