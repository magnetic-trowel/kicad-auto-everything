# Physics-first ideas: information, uncertainty and the epistemology of design (IN)

Scope: ideas that treat layout as decision-making under belief: think, compute, ask, measure or robustify, chosen by value of information (VOI) per cost (IN1–IN37). Not here: argument, derivations, numbers -> [topics/physics/lens-in](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md).
Keys: IN1–IN37, belief ledger, lineage, level/shape split, EVPPI, EVPI, VOI engine, decision invariance, robustify-or-learn, flip threshold, chance constraint, P(LII ≤ 1), nugget, fidelity escalation, contender calibration, two-evaluator protocol, post-selection shrinkage, gradient alignment, active subspace, common random numbers, rule posteriors, regime distance, pre-registered bring-up, coupon panel, Fisher identifiability, model discrepancy, Cooke elicitation, epistemic scorecard

Principles (defined in the topic doc): E1 value of information, E2 decisions are differential, E3 rules are compressed posteriors, E4 budget the unknown, E5 locality of knowledge, E6 independent evidence, E7 predict then observe.
Scripts: [in_decision_quality.py](../../../tools/research/physics/in_decision_quality.py), [in_emi_voi.py](../../../tools/research/physics/in_emi_voi.py). Builds on, does not repeat: SIM1/SIM7/SIM9/SIM10/SIM14 ([simulation](simulation.md)), DR15/DR16/DR19/DR33 ([reasoning](reasoning.md)), LA15/LA39/LA40 ([language](language.md)), FE19/FE56 ([si-emi-rf](si-emi-rf.md)), TM53/TM54 ([thermal-hv-mfg](thermal-hv-mfg.md)), XD110/XD111 ([cross-domain](cross-domain.md)), I5, U3, V4, S4 ([original](../original/README.md)).

## A. The epistemic state

- **IN1** Belief ledger (*combo, M; E1/E6*). Every number in `ledger.json` becomes a belief: distribution or conformal interval; validity (in/edge/out); *lineage* (idealizations it rests on: PEC image plane, quasi-static, 2-D, code base, author); links to the decisions that read it; current EVPPI and cheapest resolving action. SIM1 attaches σ, PC4 marks coverage; neither says what a number is *for* or whether two numbers share a failure mode. Test: seed five modelling faults (missed void, wrong port, wrong stackup) on two boards; count flags via lineage collisions or decision links vs σ alone.
- **IN2** Level/shape error split (*new, M; E2*). Estimator error = common factor per product class (fab, stackup, enclosure, cable, test setup, h_conv) + candidate-specific part (geometry features); rankings read only shape, compliance verdicts read both. One σ for both over-escalates ranking (common bias cancels) and under-escalates compliance. On solver-ladder loop data a common ×1.5 bias left MS2's choices unchanged [C, [D2](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#2-derivations)]. Test: on solver-ladder loop families, shape-only σ must predict pick-best accuracy and level+shape σ verdict errors.
- **IN3** Lineage-aware evidence fusion (*new, S; E6*). Fuse estimates with correlation set by shared lineage tags (identical idealization ρ → 1; different physics or measurement ρ → 0); same-lineage agreement must not shrink σ: three image-plane PEEC codes agreeing (SIM46), or critics passing same-author mutants, are ≈ one piece of evidence. Test: pairs of scripts with a shared seeded fault (no plane void modelled): fused σ must not shrink; disagreement alarm (SIM11) fires only for cross-lineage pairs.
- **IN4** Sensitivity footprint per conductor quantity (*new, M; E5*). From adjoint solves the core already runs (OP38, DR3), area where |∂q/∂geometry| > ε per budgeted quantity, reported as a locality metric. Small footprint: local models suffice, incremental re-verification valid, distant ECOs cannot break the verdict; large footprint (returns seeking a distant via or cap, slot detours) is fragile and expensive to know. DR42's locality index measures current closure, not knowability. Test: footprint vs verdict changes from random distant edits, 10 boards.
- **IN5** Conductor epistemic card (*combo, S; E1/E5/E7*). Per body: roles as hypotheses with probabilities per band; state per band (J, V, dv/dt) with rung and lineage; budgets spent as P(over budget); footprint; top unknowns by EVPPI; predictions registered for bring-up. Code writes it, the LLM reads it; answers "what is this conductor doing, how sure are we?" (today: net membership + role label only). Test: reviewers find a seeded wrong role or missing return faster with cards than with the ledger table.
- **IN6** Off-board conductor inventory (*new, S; E1*). Cable shields, enclosure panels, heatsinks, chassis bonds, mounting hardware become first-class bodies with priors and an "ask or measure" action (absent from schematic and board file). In the EMI VOI study they carry most decision value under its stated priors, far less under others [C, [§5](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#5-the-measurement-program-as-experiment-design)]; the vocabulary has atoms (Antenna, Port) but no gathering workflow. Test: five products; verdict changes with inventory filled vs defaults.

## B. Value of information and actions

- **IN7** One VOI engine (*sharp, M; E1*). Replace criticality product (I5), expected-loss ordering (U3), ∂margin/∂unknown (LA40), the |budget − value| < zσ trigger (SIM10, DR33) and the compute market (SIM40) with EVPPI per cost, by binning or regression over a Monte Carlo sample of the belief (Strong et al. 2014). Store cost ratio c/L per consequence class; z = Φ⁻¹(1 − c/L) [C, [D1](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#2-derivations)]. The heuristics disagree exactly where it matters; a large sensitivity at 30 dB margin has zero VOI. Test: rank questions and escalations on 10 boards by each rule; decision changes per action.
- **IN8** Decision-invariance test (*new, S; E1*). Before an expensive model, evaluate the decision at corners/quantiles of the belief; same best action everywhere -> skip the model. Example: f_x transition regime: if Kelvin topology and peninsula are chosen under both resistive and inductive limits, no transition solver is needed. Turns [research-gaps](../../topics/physics/research-gaps.md) gap 2 into "build only where the limits disagree". Test: 30 decisions; fraction skipped and fraction later reversed by the full model.
- **IN9** Robustify-or-learn arbitration (*new, M; E4*). For each high-EVPPI unknown θ_j take the cheaper of: shrink σ_j (measurement, simulation) or shrink s_j by geometry/topology that makes the decision insensitive (Kelvin, ratiometric, same-edge cables, adjacent return, symmetry). Robust actions cap VOI [C: cables on one edge, [§5](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#5-the-measurement-program-as-experiment-design)]; SIM10 and DR33 offer only "escalate fidelity". Test: EMI and ADC worked cases; total cost (fixes + measurements + expected respins) for learn-only, robustify-only, arbitrated.
- **IN10** Decision-flip thresholds as measurement specs (*new, S; E1*). Per binding decision compute the value of each unknown where the best action changes; specify measurements only to resolve relative to those thresholds. EMI case: choice flips at offsets 8 and 16 dB; a 3 dB pre-scan of a previous board in the same enclosure class recovers 66–81% of the offset's value [C] (a board's own scan comes after layout is fixed); full ±1 dB characterization is wasted. Test: measurement cost and decision quality, threshold-driven vs full-characterization specs, three products.
- **IN11** Margin–knowledge frontier (*new, L; E4, D5*). Joint optimizer chooses geometry *and* information actions: compliance as chance constraint P(spend ≤ budget) ≥ 1 − α under the belief; priced actions (F2/F3 runs, coupons, vendor models, bring-up probes) shrink σ; report frontier "area or layers saved vs knowledge bought". VOI = L·Φ(−m/σ) grows as margins shrink, so a physics-only optimizer squeezes margins until knowledge binds; DR16 discounts budgets by σ but never buys σ down. Test: two boards; cost-to-ship (fab + measurement + expected respin) vs deterministic optimum.
- **IN12** VOI of vendor models and data (*sharp, S; E1*). Before requesting a SPICE model, S-parameter file or NDA, compute EVPPI of the parameters it pins vs the envelope stand-in (DR5); request only if VOI exceeds effort. DR16 prices accuracy but gives no rule for paying. Test: 20 parts; how often the obtained model changed a decision vs the VOI forecast.
- **IN13** P(LII ≤ 1) instead of LII (*sharp, S; E2*). Report probability that every output is in budget (belief over common factors and model errors) beside point LII; rank layouts by expected LII, gate sign-off on the probability. LII is an absolute instrument: a ranking-grade proxy can pick right and misstate the winner (MS2 winner drift, [D4](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#2-derivations) [C]). Test: three converter boards; coverage of predicted probability vs F3 or bench.

## C. Proxies, fidelity and selection

- **IN14** Evaluator nugget audit (*new, S; E2*). Per evaluator measure variance between nearly identical inputs (nudges 0.01–0.05 mm, collinear segment splits, reseeded routers, remeshes); store resolution limit √nugget in metadata. The HPWL-vs-routed study implies a routed-J nugget, and grid-snapped MS2 loses pick-best as candidates converge while smooth MS2 holds [C, [§3](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#3-resolution-fidelity-follows-convergence-when-the-evaluator-has-a-nugget)]; PC32 tests continuity, not resolution. Test: nugget stable across a board class and predicts where proxy decisions fail.
- **IN15** Fidelity by population spread (*new, S; E2*). Escalate the evaluator when the population's true-value spread (estimated from the next rung on a few members) falls to ≈ 2× the current nugget, instead of fixed generations or successive-halving fractions (JPR45, OP45): decision value falls with spread/nugget, not time. Test: J vs CPU on [joint-place-route-experiment](../../topics/physics/joint-place-route-experiment.md) instances vs fixed schedules.
- **IN16** Contender-restricted calibration (*sharp, S; E2*). Calibrate each proxy's dispersion and bias on the top-k candidates the optimizer actually compares, not random layouts or all visited states (SIM14 still mixes in losers). SIM7 hot-loop data: PP is badly wrong only on losers and picks well; area picks poorly because its error is the omitted w/h [C, [D2 table](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#2-derivations)]. Test: predicted vs observed pick-best using contender σ (within 1–4 points for MS, MS2, PP).
- **IN17** Two-evaluator protocol (*new, S; E6, D4*). Select with one evaluator, report the winner's physics from an independent one (different idealization, at least different code base); applies to optimizer outputs, benchmark best-of-k columns (PC43), LLM-proposed repairs. Under strong selection MS2's winner error drifts with 0% regret, and a 7-equal-filament reference is worst where winners sit, so the reporting evaluator must be valid there [C, [D4](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#2-derivations)]. Test: reported vs independently re-evaluated winners, 50 optimizer runs.
- **IN18** Post-selection shrinkage (*new, S; E2*). With only the selecting evaluator, shrink the winner by expected selection bias e_k·σ_u²/√(σ_u² + s²) (independent error) plus IN16's contender-specific bias; flag winners outside the calibration hull (Smith and Winkler 2006). Turns the SIM9 Goodhart audit from alarm into correction. Test: shrunk vs raw vs F2 on optimizer winners.
- **IN19** Gradient-alignment admission test (*new, S; E2/E3*). Admit a proxy to an inner loop only if cos(∇proxy, ∇reference) is high over the visited region. Over a plane area scores cosine 0.71 (median) where MS2 is near 1 ([§3](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#3-resolution-fidelity-follows-convergence-when-the-evaluator-has-a-nugget)) [C]; rank correlation over random layouts misses the misalignment that makes optimizers drift. Test: proxies admitted by this test vs by Spearman ρ, scored by true regret after optimization.
- **IN20** Active-subspace proxy discovery (*new, M; E3 sloppiness*). For each quantity with a trusted F2 solver (via inductance, decap reach, crosstalk, thermal coupling) sample gradients over feature space, take leading eigenvectors, fit a closed form along them plus a residual surrogate (SIM34). Loop L over a plane is mostly one direction, PW25's height × squares form [C, [§4](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#4-sloppiness-why-minimal-models-work)]; automates finding the next MS2. Test: discovered vs hand-made proxies on decision quality at equal cost.
- **IN21** Common random numbers for robust comparisons (*new, S; E2*). Under tolerance ensembles (XD110, SIM42, which specify ensembles per design) evaluate every candidate on the *same* fab, part and model-error draws; paired differences cancel common noise, needing far fewer samples. Test: samples for a stable robust ranking with/without common draws, impedance and hot-loop cases.

## D. Rules as priors

- **IN22** Rule reliability posteriors by origin and regime (*new, S; E3*). Each rule record (RG1) gets a Beta posterior on "about right" seeded by origin (physics, simplicity proxy, habit: [D3](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#2-derivations)) [C]; update per regime from benchmark failures (RG39) and solver audits (RG32); use as prior weight in warm starts (RG41) and move generation (RG42). RG3's origin tags carry no uncertainty and never learn. Test: posteriors predict which rules the physics critic overrides on held-out boards.
- **IN23** Regime-distance kernel (*new, S; E3*). Weight a rule's prior by exp(−d/ℓ), d = distance from the current dimensionless-group vector (RG2) to the rule's home regime z₀ (RG4, RG7): zero far outside, strong inside; replaces the hard on/off predicate that wastes information near the boundary and over-trusts just inside. Test: calibration of rule-predicted vs physics verdicts vs regime distance.
- **IN24** Direction-vs-threshold rule classifier (*new, M; E3*). Sweep each rule's regime variables in the solver; test whether sign(∂quantity/∂rule variable) ever flips. Never flips (more spacing -> less coupling): move generator everywhere; threshold drifts: keep only the budget; direction flips ("smallest cap closest"): retire outside home regime. RG42 uses rules as moves without knowing where direction holds. Test: acceptance rate of rule-generated moves by class over a benchmark run.
- **IN25** Revealed vs stated priors (*combo, S; E3*). Fit geometry priors from the open-board corpus and confront the stated rules: human decap distance median 3.29 mm vs "≤2 mm" ([measured-priors](../../topics/data/measured-priors.md)). Looser practice without failures -> rule conservative there; looser practice with clustered failures -> sloppy practice. Corpus priors and rule packs are kept apart today; survivorship bias: published boards worked well enough to publish. Test: inferred "conservative" list vs genealogy verdicts.
- **IN26** Rule alignment score (*new, S; E3*). Cosine between the direction a rule moves the design (area: (1, 1, 0, 0) in log(a, b, w, h)) and the active-subspace direction of its quantity in the current regime; low = rule optimizes the wrong combination here. "Minimize loop area" aligns in free space, not over a plane [C, [§4](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#4-sloppiness-why-minimal-models-work)]: computable "rules fail by regime". Test: alignment predicts rules the genealogy marks M or X.

## E. The measurement program

- **IN27** Pre-registered bring-up as the F4 rung (*combo, M; E7/E2*). Before fab write interval predictions for the first article: near-field at probe points (FE19), ring frequency and amplitude, 4-wire µV between test points, TDR impedance, IR checkpoints (TM53), pre-compliance scan with real cables in the enclosure. After bring-up assimilate (common factors first, plus model-discrepancy term); store posteriors per fab and enclosure class. The product shares every common factor, so it is the highest-VOI coupon; every board becomes a calibration point (SIM12 and FE19 keep coupons and bring-up separate). Test: prediction error on the second board of a class after assimilating the first.
- **IN28** VOI-designed coupon panel (*sharp, M; E1*). Pick coupons by summed EVPPI over the expected decision portfolio of future boards, not one per basis quantity; favour model-form disputes that flip decisions (1–5 mm slots, merged antipads, where slot-line formula and Ott fit disagree) and the circuit link (overshoot vs L with real FETs) over re-measuring anchored L vs h. EMI case: the bridging-cap dispute had zero decision value, the short-slot dispute some [C, [§5](../../topics/physics/lens-in.md#5-the-measurement-program-as-experiment-design)]. Test: VOI-ranked vs SIM12 panel; decision changes per coupon.
- **IN29** Identifiability check for measurement plans (*new, M; E1/E6*). Before measuring compute Fisher information of planned observables w.r.t. parameters via the adjoint chain (S1); reject plans where decision-relevant combinations are unidentifiable and add the degeneracy-breaking experiment (second cable length separates source from antenna; two airflow states separate h_conv from emissivity). Otherwise assimilation silently trades parameters (Kennedy and O'Hagan 2001). Test: simulated bring-ups with known truth; posterior error with/without the check.
- **IN30** Discrepancy-aware assimilation (*sharp, M; E6*). When fitting stackup Dk, h_conv, via constants or EMI offset (XD111, TM54) include a model-discrepancy term per lineage so model-form error is not absorbed into transferable parameters (a Dk compensating a missing roughness model is wrong on the next stackup). Test: transfer error of fitted parameters to a second stackup with/without the term.

## F. Reasoning and people

- **IN31** Hypothesis protocol for the LLM (*sharp, S; E1/E7*). Every mechanism hypothesis (LA39) carries a prior from failure base rates (IN32), an observable that differs under hypothesis and negation, and the cheapest test of it; it becomes a budget only if posterior × consequence clears the VOI bar. Prevents over-constraint moving from rules to imagination. Test: precision/recall on planted couplings in DR51 benchmark boards; count of budgets added.
- **IN32** Failure-mechanism base rates and unknown-unknowns reserve (*new, M; E1*). Base-rate table of root causes from post-mortems, respin data, EMC lab reports as prior over mechanism hypotheses; reserve margin in proportion to the historical rate of unmodelled mechanisms. No rigorous public respin breakdown exists ([respin data](../../topics/verification/respin-and-fab-hold-data.md)), so the system currently assumes its screen is complete. Test: retrospective screen coverage on published failure write-ups.
- **IN33** Calibrated expert elicitation, Cooke's classical model (*new, M; E7*). Ask experts for probabilities of where a board fails, not rankings; score on seed questions with known answers; weight criticality maps by calibration and informativeness. I5 treats any expert's (tacit) map as truth. Test: weighted vs unweighted maps predicting critic findings, 10 boards.
- **IN34** Explanations by flip thresholds (*new, S; E1/E7*). Each decision explanation lists the top 2–3 unknowns by EVPPI and the values that flip it ("if the cable CM offset is under 8 dB, move the connector; we assumed 10 ± 6"). Explanations reduce over-reliance only when they cut verification cost ([explanations-and-alternatives](../../topics/ux/explanations-and-alternatives.md)); DR39 explains *why* with multipliers, not what would change the answer. Test: reviewer error-detection on seeded wrong decisions with/without thresholds.
- **IN35** Board epistemic scorecard (*combo, S; E1*). Beside the physics scorecard: share of spend verified per rung, share of decisions outside validated domains, P(all budgets met), remaining EVPI. Measures how much of the board is *known* (PC4 counts checks run). Test: remaining EVPI vs later ECO count on real projects.
- **IN36** Information-gain mutants (*new, S; E1 adaptive testing*). Weight/select mutants (PC29, PC30) by how well they discriminate good from bad critics (physics delta near the critic's decision boundary), not by count; gross faults every critic catches carry little information. Test: critic rankings stabilize with fewer mutants than uniform sampling.
- **IN37** Predictability premium for exotic geometry (*new, M; E4/E5*). Add λ·σ(spend/budget) to the objective so geometry outside validated model domains (organic pours near slots, unusual via topologies, any-angle copper near discontinuities) must beat convention by more than its extra uncertainty and review cost: the epistemic reason reviewers prize convention; replaces the vague "regularity regularizer" ([research-gaps](../../topics/physics/research-gaps.md) gap 15) with priced risk. Test: user acceptance and verified physics with λ = 0 vs calibrated λ.
