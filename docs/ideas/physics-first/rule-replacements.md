# Rule replacements: turning rules of thumb into physics checks (RG1–RG53)

Scope: ideas that replace layout rules of thumb with budgets, regime predicates and retirement tests. Not here: the rule evidence -> [rule-basis-set](../../topics/physics/rule-basis-set.md) and genealogies ([power-analog-thermal](../../topics/physics/rule-genealogy-power-analog-thermal.md), [si-emi-rf](../../topics/physics/rule-genealogy-si-emi-rf.md), [routing-style-dfm](../../topics/physics/rule-genealogy-routing-style-dfm.md)).
Keys: RG1–RG53, rule decompiler, regime predicate, dimensionless group, origin class (Hb, Lg, Cg, Tl, Mf), 3W, Johnson K, vendor back-solve, rule retirement, myth zoo, rule-hole, warm start, move generator, slogan

- Rule ids P01–P59, S01–S55, R01–R46 = rows of the genealogy docs. Basis codes (L, M, Cm, R, Z, Bal, Vcm, Res, Ins, Th, Mech, Fab) defined in rule-basis-set.
- Builds on, does not repeat: I1/I2 budget ledger and dimensioned constraints ([intent-and-why](../original/intent-and-why.md)); N7/N8 solvers ([priority-agents](../original/priority-agents-and-negotiation.md)).
- Tags: **new** (not in corpus), **combo** (known pieces combined), **sharp** (existing idea given a number or test). Effort S days, M weeks, L months.

## A. Rule records and regime machinery

- **RG1** Rule decompiler record (*new, S*): store each rule as {slogan, origin class, basis quantities, governing equation, regime predicate, replacement budget, solver cost} (= genealogy-table columns); slogan is only a retrieval key/explanation, only the budget compiles to constraints. Test: port the 160 audited rules; every emitted constraint traces to a budget.
- **RG2** Regime predicates as dimensionless groups (*new, S*): a rule fires only when its group crosses a threshold ([group table](../../topics/physics/rule-basis-set.md#regime-selectors-when-does-a-rule-bind)); computed from stackup, net t_r, currents, outline, so a 4-layer 100 MHz board drops most RF and corner rules. Test: removing inactive rules per benchmark board changes no physics verdict.
- **RG3** Origin-aware rule handling (*new, S*): Hb → "info" unless historic regime detected (RG4); Lg never relaxed by physics; Cg, Tl → style channel; Mf bind only against the declared fab profile. Stops habit rules blocking physics-correct layouts. Test: re-score 20 boards with/without; count false violations removed.
- **RG4** Legacy-regime archaeology (*new, S*): per habit rule store the regime where it was right: leaded capacitors (P22, P23), tape-up/photoplotters (R02), immersion etchants (R04), 1956 NBS trace tests (P31), wave soldering (R25); re-activate only in that regime (through-hole decap, hand assembly). Test: reviewer panel rates explanations vs a plain "myth" label.
- **RG5** 3W-to-K converter (*sharp, S*): re-express width-based spacing as D/h via the actual w/h, then Johnson K = 1/(1+(D/h)²) (values: row S15 of [si-emi-rf](../../topics/physics/rule-genealogy-si-emi-rf.md)) [C]. Warn when a W-rule is applied far from controlled-impedance w/h.
- **RG6** Stackup counterfactual test (*new, S*): evaluate each mm rule on its source's stackup (e.g. TI 4-layer for 200 mil return vias) and ours; a flipped verdict = stackup proxy, re-derive from budget. Test: run on all mm/mil rules in the packs; list flips.
- **RG7** Vendor-rule back-solver (*sharp, M*): infer the hidden budget from the vendor's reference stackup (200 mil return via → ~140 pH at d = 0.1 mm; CIN < 2 mm → nH on their eval board), re-apply to ours. Test: back-solved budgets across vendors for one interface; agreement within 2× = shared physics target.
- **RG8** Eval-board budget extraction (*combo, M*): import vendor eval Gerbers, compute loop nH, SW-node fF, Kelvin shared squares with our estimators, store as precedence-40 targets instead of distances. Test: 10 converter eval boards vs datasheet anchors (SNVA803, SLAA896 pH tables).
- **RG9** Physical-units lint for rule packs (*combo, S*): flag mm/mil numbers with no derivation (h, t_r, λ, budget) as "unscoped proxy", and proxy adjectives (short, close, wide, keep away) with no quantity; extends I2 to rule packs. Test: target zero unscoped numbers in hard rules.
- **RG10** Proxy-word disambiguation in the IR (*new, S*): the LLM may not emit "short", "close", "wide", "direct", "no vias", "keep away"; the parser forces a basis quantity + budget from the proxy matrix, e.g. "keep FB away from SW" → C(FB, SW) ≤ 10 fF and M(FB loop, hot loop) ≤ 0.1 nH. Test: LLM quantity vs expert key on 100 datasheet sentences.

## B. Replacement checks (one per family of rules)

- **RG11** Return-transition budget in pH (*sharp, S*): replace "return via within 200/50 mil" and layer-change order by L = via partial + (µ0d/π)·ln(s/a) per transition, budgeted from data rate and Z0; plane spacing d enters linearly, via distance only logarithmically [C]. Test: vs 3-D S-parameters of 10 transitions; rank correlation ≥ 0.9 retires the mil rules.
- **RG12** Corner reflection check scoped by f·h (*sharp, S*): bend |Γ| from Gupta/Kirschning C and L at actual w/h, f_max; fire only if f_max·h > ~3 GHz·mm (keeps 2.4 GHz on 1.5 mm, kills 4-layer digital). Test: openEMS bends at h = 0.2, 0.8, 1.5 mm to calibrate the L term (closed forms disagree 4×).
- **RG13** Decap placement by mounting + spreading inductance (*sharp, S*): score position by L_mount + (µ0d/π)·ln(s/a) to the pin; "within 2 mm" vanishes on 0.1 mm plane pairs, tightens on 2-layer. Feeds N8. Test: rank order vs PDN field solver on 3 stackups.
- **RG14** Width = max(IR, thermal) (*sharp, S*): size each current path by the larger of an IR budget (% of rail, per segment, current-weighted) and a thermal network, never IPC-2221 alone (row P31 of [power-analog-thermal](../../topics/physics/rule-genealogy-power-analog-thermal.md)) [C]. Test: list benchmark nets whose IPC width violates a 1% IR budget.
- **RG15** Fin-aware copper-area thermal check (*sharp, S*): discount copper beyond fin length √(kt/2h) (row P54 of [power-analog-thermal](../../topics/physics/rule-genealogy-power-analog-thermal.md)) [C]; suggest heavier copper, inner planes or vias instead. Replaces "15.3 cm²/W". Test: vs 2-D thermal FEM, 5 layouts, one 3 W source.
- **RG16** Kelvin shared-resistance solver (*new, S*): solve sheet current on shunt pads and taps; report error % and ppm/K from squares shared by sense and power paths (1 oz 0.49 mΩ/□ vs a 1 mΩ shunt [C]). Replaces "tap at the inner pad edge". Test: 4-wire measurements on 3 shunt land patterns.
- **RG17** SW-node capacitance ledger (*new, M*): C from switch node to each victim net, board edge, declared heatsinks and cables (2-D/3-D electrostatics); budget i = C·dv/dt per victim tolerance. Replaces "minimise SW area", "keep FB away from SW"; copper may grow where it couples to nothing. Test: vs near-field-probe measurement of a buck eval board.
- **RG18** Shared-branch impedance detector (*combo, M*): per loop pair, Z of shared copper (R + jωL at aggressor spectrum); flag Z_shared·I_aggressor > victim budget (LSB, FB noise). Replaces single-point ground, AGND/DGND, star ground, "CIN/COUT grounds apart". Test: reproduce the Ott split-plane jumper experiment in simulation.
- **RG19** Frequency-crossover ground map (*sharp, S*): f_x = R′/(2πL′) per return path (0.3–1.1 MHz, 1 oz over 0.2 mm [C]); below f_x enforce DC discipline (star, Kelvin, no shared copper), above it plane continuity. Test: 2-D current solves at 10 kHz and 10 MHz on an audio board.
- **RG20** Ground-fill admissibility (*sharp, S*): same-layer fill only with gap ≥ 2h (Z0 shift < 1% [C]) and every island stitched below λ/10 at f_max; on 2-layer, require a stitched continuous return pour. Replaces "never fill"/"fill everything". Test: islands removed/kept on 20 boards, Z0 by 2-D solve.
- **RG21** Stitching from the largest unstitched dimension (*combo, S*): per plane pair, largest via-free span vs λ/2 at f_max and cavity-mode maxima; settles perimeter vs distributed. Test: openEMS cavity, 100 mm board, both patterns.
- **RG22** ESD path inductance budget (*sharp, S*): L(connector→TVS→ground) ≤ (V_IC,max − V_clamp)/(di/dt), di/dt for 8 kV contact from row S35 of [si-emi-rf](../../topics/physics/rule-genealogy-si-emi-rf.md) [C]. Replaces "TVS near connector", "no via before TVS", the 5–10 mm blog numbers. Test: IEC 61000-4-2 bench, two layouts, same parts.
- **RG23** Crystal ppm ledger (*sharp, S*): trace C × pullability (row S51 of [si-emi-rf](../../topics/physics/rule-genealogy-si-emi-rf.md)) [C] + its tolerance + aggressor pickup vs the protocol's ppm budget; yields the placement window instead of 5 or 12 mm. Test: frequency offset at 3 trace lengths.
- **RG24** Leakage-versus-capacitance guard decision (*sharp, S*): leakage I = V/(ρ_s·N□) at the input node vs the guard's added C; guard only if leakage > budget and Cm budget allows. Resolves the two TIA regimes (P41/P42). Test: classify 20 TIA circuits vs TI reference layouts.
- **RG25** Via-count rule replaced by via budgets (*sharp, S*): per interface budget return loss, insertion loss, stub resonance, return-transition pH per via; a compliant via costs nothing. Removes "≤ 2 vias" where backdrilled/stitched vias are fine; "0 vias" only where a budget forbids. Test: TI/Qualcomm tables, PCIe and USB3 examples.
- **RG26** Edge distance from fringing and Vcm (*sharp, S*): trace-to-plane-edge distance from h and the MREMC edge term instead of 90 mil, 2h or 3W. Test: openEMS trace-near-edge at 3 offsets vs closed form.
- **RG27** Skew budget from mode conversion (*sharp, S*): skew ≤ 2·t_r·(V_cm,allow/V_d), V_cm,allow the tighter of cable emission budget and receiver CM tolerance. Replaces vendor tables that differ 5–25×. Test: flag outlier vendor tables.
- **RG28** Thermal-relief decision per pad (*new, S*): ΔL, ΔR, ΔR_th of reliefs (row R25 of [routing-style-dfm](../../topics/physics/rule-genealogy-routing-style-dfm.md#pours-planes-and-reliefs)) [C] vs the pad's loop budget, plus soldering heat balance for the declared process (reflow, wave, hand); relief or solid per pad. Test: tombstone/joint inspection on a test panel with both.
- **RG29** Tombstone heat-balance check (*sharp, S*): two pad heat-up time constants per small passive from connected copper/planes; flag ratios above a calibrated threshold. Replaces "symmetric copper". Test: reflow 0201/0402 panel with controlled asymmetry.
- **RG30** Power-pour shape objective (*combo, M*): minimise R + ωL (relevant spectrum) + thermal resistance, s.t. Cm caps on switching nodes and fab rules, via 2-D sheet solver; replaces "wide pours", "round the corners"; hand-off to [optimization](optimization.md). Test: optimised vs hand pours on a 20 A buck by IR, loop nH, SW fF.
- **RG31** Isolation physics side-checks (*new, M*): keep the legal creepage/clearance engine, add flags: Paschen at altitude/low pressure, electrochemical migration ∝ V/gap × humidity on dense LV boards, field enhancement at sharp HV copper. Test: list boards where a flag fires but the table passes.

## C. Retirement, audit and calibration tests

- **RG32** Rule-retirement test (*new, M*): paired micro-layouts satisfying/violating the rule, holding every basis quantity it does *not* claim to affect; if the physics verdict never changes in the target regime, retire to style. Test: all 11 "X" rows retire, no "R" row does.
- **RG33** Anti-conservative alarm (*new, M*): search layouts passing every active rule but failing physics (3W thin traces on 2-layer, IPC widths on 1 V rails, decaps "close" over 1 mm dielectric); each hit = documented rule hole. Test: holes per CPU-hour, random vs adversarial search.
- **RG34** Myth zoo regression suite (*new, M*): one micro-board per myth (90° corner, 20-H, split ground, three values, guard trace, ground fill) + solver script printing the magnitude; the LLM must quote it. Test: CI runs the zoo; a verdict flip needs review.
- **RG35** Rule-to-test-coupon generator (*new, M*): emit a KiCad coupon isolating the rule's mechanism (TDR corners, VNA stubs, thermal camera copper area, near-field probe SW area). Test: one panel, 10 coupons, vs solver predictions.
- **RG36** Cross-rule redundancy pruning (*new, S*): rules mapping to one budget (CIN distance, zero vias, area tier, vertical loop → L_hot) collapse to the budget. Test: LLM constraint-authoring error rate, full vs pruned set.
- **RG37** Sensitivity-ranked rules (*new, M*): ∂(observable)/∂(geometry) by finite differences or adjoints on the quasi-static model; show/solve highest first. Replaces fixed severity labels and placement order (R43). Test: top-10 vs expert ranking on 5 boards.
- **RG38** Rule weights from budget overspend (*combo, S*): weight soft rules by fraction of budget spent, not S1–S4 labels (5% spend → ~0 weight). Test: placer convergence and violations, label vs overspend weights.
- **RG39** Rule-hole catalogue from benchmark failures (*new, S*): when a rule-passing board fails physics, record nearest proxy rule and failing regime → per-regime reliability. Test: catalogue size, fraction of failures explained by known regimes.
- **RG40** Two-auditor agreement on rule classification (*new, S*): second auditor (human or another LLM run) classifies origin and verdict of the 160 rules; report Cohen's κ per column; low-κ rows need primary sources.

## D. Rules as priors for synthesis, not as judges

- **RG41** Rules as warm starts (*new, M*): rule geometry (CIN next to IC, return via beside signal via, decaps at pins) = initial guess for the physics optimiser; rules are cached solutions of the typical case. Test: iterations to feasibility with/without.
- **RG42** Rules as move generators (*new, M*): rules → local search moves (add return via, move cap to pin, pull fill back to 2h, rotate inductor dot); physics critic accepts/rejects. Test: acceptance rate per rule; ~0 on a board class → retire there.
- **RG43** Regime-switching generator (*combo, M*): near a predicate threshold (f/f_x ≈ 1, f·h ≈ 3 GHz·mm) propose both variants (star/plane, mitred/square); solver chooses. Test: frequency of solver reversals of "both tried" decisions.
- **RG44** Corner and curve geometry only where it pays (*sharp, S*): Douville–James miters for microstrip RF bends, arcs for flex and HV edges, inner fillets for pulse-current pours, 45° elsewhere. Test: reviewer acceptance, RF return loss on mixed boards.
- **RG45** Vertical-loop proposer (*sharp, S*): thin L1–L2 + tight hot-loop budget → propose the vertical loop (caps returning through several vias to L2) instead of banning vias; P03 becomes a geometry choice. Test: N7 with/without the template, in nH.

## E. Explanation and review

- **RG46** Three-line decompression on demand (*new, S*): print quantity encoded, regime predicate evaluated here, magnitude here ("3W: K=2.8% on these 50 Ω lines; budget 5%; not binding"). Test: user study on review decisions.
- **RG47** Binding-rule heat map (*new, S*): render where rules bind (predicate true, spend > 50%); most of the board greys out. Test: reviewer time to seeded defects with/without.
- **RG48** Return-current renderer at four frequencies (*combo, M*): quasi-static ground-copper current at DC, 100 kHz, 10 MHz, 1 GHz shown to reviewers. Test: expert agreement with mental model on 5 boards.
- **RG49** Slogan detector for LLM output (*new, S*): rate of unprompted myth slogans (90° corners, three values, split ground, 20-H); retrain prompts with decompressed records. Test: slogan rate before/after on 200 design-review prompts.
- **RG50** Electro-thermal feedback in sensing and IR checks (*new, S*): thermal-network T into copper R (TCR 3900 ppm/K) for IR and Kelvin checks; busbar at 60 °C has 16% more R than at 20 °C. Test: isothermal vs coupled on a 20 A path.
- **RG51** Mechanical strain map for edge and flex rules (*new, M*): plate-model board strain from depanelling, mounting holes, heavy parts, flex bends → MLCC keep-outs and teardrops from strain limits; replaces fixed 3–5 mm keep-outs. Test: crack incidence on a depanelled panel.
- **RG52** Fab-margin yield model (*combo, M*): per-feature defect probability from margin/σ (etch, drill registration, mask) summed over the board, replacing binary DRC at recommended values; router trades geometry for yield. Test: vs fab DFM reports on 20 boards.
- **RG53** Keep-away distances solved from coupling curves (*sharp, M*): M(d), Cm(d), ΔT(d) for actual geometry (trace over plane ~1/(1+(d/h)²), open-flux magnetics ~1/d³, heat by influence coefficients); solve distance meeting the victim budget. Replaces "1–3 mm from magnetics/SW/clocks" (R46); shrinks over thin planes, grows on 2-layer. Test: near-field coupling on a buck + op-amp board.
