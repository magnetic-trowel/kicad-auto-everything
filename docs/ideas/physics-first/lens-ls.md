## A. Planes in the transition band (P3)

- **LS1** Complex-image PEEC for the transition band (*sharp, S; P3*). In image-plane PEEC (SIM16) replace the perfect image at depth h with N images at complex depths h + p·e·u_i/β, p = 2R_s/(jωμ0), weights c_i from contour-rotated Gauss–Laguerre (N = 4–6, φ ≈ 20–25°, β = 2); ln, Neumann and coaxial-loop formulas accept complex distances, so one evaluation per frequency gives R(f), L(f) from the resistive (Carson) end to the mirror limit. 4–6 images cut the perfect image's L′ error to a few % and reproduce the pw_eddy 3-D coil out of sample [C, [closed-form table](../../topics/physics/lens-ls.md#3-derivation-b-the-plane-as-a-spatial-filter)]. Cost N image evaluations (ms) vs seconds–minutes for a meshed thin sheet; closes research-gaps gap 2 for intact planes (SY25 picks solvers by ℓ_d but has no closed form). Test: vs meshed-plane PEEC and FastHenry (internal only), 20 loops, 10 kHz–10 MHz; target L within 3%, R within 10%.
- **LS2** Return-width halo per band (*sharp, S; P3*). Over an intact plane the return of an aggressor harmonic at f has width ≈ 2h + ℓ_m(f), capped by plane width; draw one halo per band; a victim reference tap inside an aggressor halo gets a shared-impedance budget row. Closed form vs RG48's four solves. 0.5 oz L2 at 0.1 mm: buck 500 kHz return confined to ≈ 0.7 mm, 20 kHz content to 12 mm; mains (ℓ_m = 5 m) covers the board [C]. Test: halos vs return-share maps from ls_thin_sheet, vo_return_spread and a meshed solve, 5 boards.
- **LS3** Receding-image kernel for transients (*new, S; P3, P6*). After a current step a thin plane is an image receding at w_s = 2R_s/μ0 (782 m/s for 1 oz) ([Liu & Belcher eq. 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.0182)); coupling M(t) = M_image(depth h + w_s·t); a structure of size a stays shielded ≈ a/w_s (5 mm loop: [mirror window](../../topics/physics/lens-ls.md#3-derivation-b-the-plane-as-a-spatial-filter)); 128 ns for an h = 0.1 mm trace image at 1 oz [C]. Pulse coupling (inrush, ESD, hot-plug, load steps) without a harmonic sum; exact for thin sheets; PW24's shield credit holds for pulse length T only while a > w_s·T. Test: M(t) vs time-stepped thin-sheet PEEC or transient FEM, 3 aggressor–victim pairs.
- **LS4** Thin-sheet Carson term in the ledger (*sharp, S; P3*). In the transition band (h ≪ ℓ_m ≪ W) charge the return R′ = ωμ0/4 (≈ 2 mΩ/mm per MHz) and L′ ≈ (μ0/2π)·ln(ℓ_m/r_eq), both independent of plane copper weight; use for audio, ripple, low harmonics. No solve needed; heavier plane copper (0.5–2 oz) localizes the return rather than lowering transition-band loss [C, [Carson law](../../topics/physics/lens-ls.md#3-derivation-b-the-plane-as-a-spatial-filter)]. Test: pw_xsec with finite planes; bench R(f) of a trace over 1 oz and 2 oz planes, 1–100 kHz.

## B. The impedance-level split and the critical length (P2)

- **LS5** Impedance-level classifier with Z0 targets (*combo, S; P2*). Per structure and band: θ = ωℓ/v, m = Z0/Z (Z from the net passport's V/I, Z0 from cross-section), ε_L = θ·m, ε_C = θ/m. m > 1 L-critical: move Z0 down (lower h, widen, stack); m < 1 C-critical: move Z0 up (narrow, raise, void the plane); m ≈ 1 matched (with resistive ends a short line is pure delay to first order at any m; matching removes the second-order error). Z outside the PCB Z0 span (≈ 0.4 Ω for a 50 mm plane pair at 0.1 mm up to ~170 Ω for a thin trace on 1.6 mm two-layer) -> "unmatchable", remedy structural (plane pair, parallel paths, shorter ℓ, void). Unlike PW1 roles and CT26's K_C/K_L thresholds, one ratio gives knob and direction; contains PW2's Z0* = V/I. Test: 30 nets on 5 boards, rank vs PEEC + SPICE; moves toward Z0 = Z must lower LII.
- **LS6** Delay-only certificate for matched nets (*new, S; P2, P6*). Where |m − 1| < δ (series-terminated or matched pairs) bound amplitude error by (θ²/8)(m − 1/m)² + tolerance reflection; skip TL simulation, budget only delay and skew. Exact matched two-port has zero amplitude error at any length (topic doc §2) [C]; generalizes the USB-tolerance observation of [worked-thought-experiments](../../topics/physics/worked-thought-experiments.md) into a gate removing SI simulation from most matched nets. Test: certified nets vs IBIS/SPICE eyes on 50 nets; count false certificates.
- **LS7** Critical-length family instead of t_r/6 vs t_r/2 (*sharp, S; P2, P6*). T_crit/t_r = f(R_s/Z0, Z0·C_L/t_r, overshoot budget) from the exact lossless-line ramp response with IBIS driver impedances. Settles the contested rule ([myths](../../topics/rules/myths-and-contested.md)): t_r/6 ≈ 10% budget for typical drivers (R_s/Z0 = 0.3–0.6), t_r/2 ≥ 20% with weak ones [C, [critical-length table](../../topics/physics/lens-ls.md#6-regime-map-and-causality-c)]. Test: predicted overshoot vs SPICE, 100 nets with IBIS models.

## C. Decaps and stitching as one wire medium (P5)

- **LS8** Wire-medium model for decap and stitching fields (*new, S; P5*). γ² = Z_s·(jωC′ + n/Z_post), ω_p² = n/(L_post·C′); outputs screening length λ(f) = v/√(ω_p² − ω²), anti-resonance f_p (k = 0 mode, incl. the post's cell spreading), mode shifts to √(f_mn² + f_p²); posts are decaps (L_c, C, ESR) or vias (Belov's L_post). Unifies XD113 reach, the Belov stitching check, lumped anti-resonance and decaps raising cavity modes; lattice agrees [C, [lattice checks](../../topics/physics/lens-ls.md#4-derivation-c-decaps-and-stitching-vias-are-one-wire-medium)]; replaces "stitch at λ/20", "decap within N mm", separate anti-resonance calculators. Test: vs a PDN tool or Palace on 3 stackups with real decap sets, then bench Z(f).
- **LS9** Plasma-frequency band plan (*new, S; P5*). Each plane pair's f_p is a frequency-plan variable (S8, DR36, FE13). GND–GND: pick stitching pitch so f_p clears the highest protected band; 0.3 mm vias, 2.45 GHz band: 11 mm -> 3.03 GHz (+24%), 12 mm -> 2.74 GHz (+12%), 13 mm -> 2.50 GHz (none) [C]. GND–PWR: pick decap density and ESL so the anti-resonance avoids clock harmonics and radio channels, or damp with ESR at f_p ([lens-th](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md)). f_p is closed form in pitch, via radius, ESL, d, vs today's "λ/20" and post-layout discovery. Test: f_p vs cavity-model and full-wave first resonances on 5 boards; harmonic coincidences before/after planning.
- **LS10** Lumped-PDN validity gate (*sharp, S; P5*). "All decaps in parallel" is valid only where λ(f) > board (near f_p) or below SRF (long slow-wave wavelength); flag target-impedance analyses whose band lies between SRF and f_p with λ < board. Says when decap position matters (most of the decoupling band on thick pairs); reconciles "location matters" with "doesn't" ([decoupling-pdn](../../topics/rules/decoupling-pdn.md)). Test: lumped vs lattice Z_in, 10 configurations; error vs λ/board.
- **LS11** Point-load PDN inductance as decap and stackup objective (*sharp, S; P5, P2*). IC via cluster of radius r_eq in a decap field: L_in ≈ (μ0d/2π)·ln(1.12λ/r_eq), valid for L_c/(μ0d) ≫ 1; d linear, cluster radius and ESL (via λ) logarithmic; Z_target ≥ ω_max·L_in sets max d for stackup synthesis (DR30, PW28). Low-ESL decaps buy little next to thinner dielectric or wider via cluster [C, [point load](../../topics/physics/lens-ls.md#4-derivation-c-decaps-and-stitching-vias-are-one-wire-medium)]. Test: vs lattice or PEEC with discrete decaps on 3 boards, then vendor PDN measurements.

## D. Regime selection, order and representation (P1, P7)

- **LS12** ℓ–f regime plane as regime selector (*new, M; P1–P7*). Log size × log frequency map with boundaries ℓ = δ, δ²/t, λ/10, λ/2, λ/2π, λ_th, √(α/ω), λ_pdn(f), 1/k_p, v·t_r/6; each structure is a segment (scales h -> size, band fundamental -> knee); cells crossed pick representation (ideal, lumped R/L/C, complex images, line, wire medium or modes, antenna, thermal kernel) and solver rung. Replaces design-reasoning's fixed band table and RG2's 14 groups with four primitive ratios; one object for LLM explanations, the ledger Regime type (LA11, given a scale coordinate) and the SIM4 dispatcher. Test: dispatch 100 structures; check representation vs F3 on 10.
- **LS13** Victim-relative expansion order (*new, S; P7*). n* = ⌈ln(B/S)/ln ε⌉ (S victim signal, B budget, ε largest coupling parameter) picks the physics packs; n* ≥ 3 adds thermal EMF, plane gradients from foreign currents, leakage, dielectric absorption. At ε = 0.01: digital order 1, 16-bit order 3, 24-bit order 4 [C]; explains why precision analog is hard at low frequency and keeps compute off digital nets. Test: 10 mixed-signal boards; n* must flag every mechanism a full analysis finds binding (worked case C).
- **LS14** Truncation-error σ for every evaluator (*new, S; P7*). Beside residual σ (SIM8) each evaluator reports its first neglected term: lumped L or C θ²/6; quasi-static PEEC (kℓ)²; perfect image ℓ_m/h; thin sheet t/δ; continuum decaps μ0d/(π·L_c); Hammerstad t/h. Computable anywhere, incl. outside the calibration hull (SIM2's validity domains are empirical), and names the rung to escalate to. Test: predicted vs actual error against F2/F3 on 50 structures.
- **LS15** Non-perturbative trigger (*new, S; P1, P2*). When ε_L·Q ≥ 1, ε_C·Q ≥ 1 or any ε ≥ 1, stop adding parasitic budgets; switch the structure to circuit-plus-network co-simulation (C2) or a modal model (cavity, cable, ring modes). Linear budgets fail for a slow 48 V bridge (DR11) and the LC-step model predicts ≈ 100% overshoot regardless of L, both a diverging series; trigger predicts this in advance (RB4 hot loop: [regime map](../../topics/physics/lens-ls.md#6-regime-map-and-causality-c)) [C]. Test: worked cases + 10 converter boards; additive verdicts vs co-simulation inside/outside the trigger.
- **LS16** Causal stackup model (*sharp, S; P6*). Fit each laminate's single datasheet Dk/Df point to wideband Debye (Djordjevic–Sarkar); evaluate Z0, delay, loss at each net's knee and calibration frequencies; flag numbers computed with 1 MHz values. FR-4 Dk drift and its Z0/delay effect: [Kramers–Kronig](../../topics/physics/lens-ls.md#6-regime-map-and-causality-c) [C]; TM35 tolerance budgets and coupon calibration (gap 1) inherit the error. Test: coupon TDR and VNA impedance, causal vs constant-Dk model.
- **LS17** Loss-length gate (*sharp, S; P6*). ℓ_loss = c·t_r/(3.08√εr·tanδ); below it geometry sets the edge, above it material does (lever: laminate, equalization, shorter route) -> propose a material ECO instead of a geometry tweak. Low-loss laminate 1.3 m and 0.45 m at 100 / 35 ps (FR-4: [scales table](../../topics/physics/lens-ls.md#5-scales-of-a-board-rb4-60--40-mm-4-layers-01-mm-prepreg-05-oz-planes-c)) [C]. Test: predicted line rise times vs channel simulation, 10 SerDes nets.

## E. Insulators and slow processes (P4, P6)

- **LS18** Surface charge-relaxation regime for guards and HV DC (*new, S; P4*). Per insulating gap τ_e ≈ ε_eff·g/σ_s, σ_s from a humidity/contamination class. Below 1/(2πτ_e) surface potential follows conductivity: guards must be driven to equal potential, HV DC stress follows contamination (fix: grading or coating); above, the surface is capacitive. Adds a regime to the Ins basis quantity ([rule-basis-set](../../topics/physics/rule-basis-set.md), leakage ratio only). 1 mm gap: τ_e from [scales table](../../topics/physics/lens-ls.md#5-scales-of-a-board-rb4-60--40-mm-4-layers-01-mm-prepreg-05-oz-planes-c) [C, assumed σ_s]; same gap capacitive in the lab, resistive in the field. Test: SIR comb coupons at 40% and 85% RH under DC + AC; measure the crossover.
- **LS19** Laminate dielectric absorption near high-Z nodes (*new, S; P6, P4*). Kramers–Kronig pairs low-frequency loss with slow polarization; for integrators, sample-and-hold and electrometer inputs budget memory error ΔV ≈ DA × (C_laminate/C_total) × V_step; levers: void under the node, PTFE island, guard. Fills research-gaps gap 12 (DA unexamined); TH14 covers its noise, not its memory. Test: step-and-hold on coupons with and without a void.

## F. Locality in the placer and optimizer (P5, P7)

- **LS20** Decay-class-aware interaction lists (*sharp, S; P5*). Every placer kernel carries a decay class and length that set its cut-off: screened (thermal λ, decap λ, L_T) ≈ 3ℓ; evanescent (stripline, below cut-off) b/π; algebraic ((h/D)², r⁻³, r⁻⁵) where the budget is met (LA25); diffusive √(αt); wave: none, go modal (LS21). XD112, OP24, SY24 give kernels but not class cut-offs; list sparsity and "close" (r/ℓ < 0.5) follow. Test: runtime and objective error vs full N², 5 boards.
- **LS21** Mode-shape coordinates above cut-off (*combo, S; P5*). Above a pair's f_p or first mode score each source (fast via, switch node) by Σ |φ_mn(x)|²·S(f_mn)·Q_mn; place sources at nodes of dominant modes, dampers/posts at antinodes (TH4). Open edges are antinodes of every mode (second reason to keep fast vias off edges); position replaces distance rules in the cavity regime; FE28 moves vias by eigenvalue gradient, not sources by mode shape. Test: cavity-model excitation vs source position, 3 boards, full-wave check.
- **LS22** Homogenization validity ratios (*sharp, S; P7*). Before an effective medium (Rayleigh perforation, Peaceman via, wire-medium stitching, hatched plane, thermal-via array) compute L_T/p, p/λ_d, p/h or p/λ_th; homogenize only if ≪ 1. Via fields have L_T/p ≈ 0.4–1 (1.05 mm at 1 mm pitch): edge rows carry the current and averaged models fail (XD22, XD108, PW13). Test: homogenized vs discrete, 20 via fields and 10 stitching lattices across the ratio.
- **LS23** Physics-scaled multigrid (*combo, M; P7*). Each optimizer level's grid follows the shortest active kernel length: λ_th ~ 40 mm, λ_pdn 10–25 mm, 1/k_p ~ 7 mm, h, ℓ_m(f_sw); homogenized coefficients up, boundary values down (XD109); replaces JPR16's fixed 2 mm and 0.5 mm levels; shorter-scale terms enter as homogenized parameters. Test: J vs CPU time against fixed multigrid, 10 boards.
- **LS24** Scale-ordered decision schedule (*combo, M; P5*). Order decisions by spatial scale, least slack τ (DR17) within each: 1 system (connector edges, cable points, enclosure); 2 board (stackup, plane partition, cell placement); 3 cell interior; 4 feature. Far-field budgets see only low-order source moments: explains why in the one worked EMI case ([fields-emi-budget](../../topics/physics/fields-emi-budget.md)) removing a slot/split beat edge-rate and series-R tweaks by 20–35 dB; physical basis for JPR50's outer loop. Test: 5 boards, coarse-first vs τ-only; spend and backtracks.
- **LS25** Cells as multipole particles (*combo, M; P5*). Optimize each cell interior for local budgets and small external moments (dipole with image, net external charge Σ C_ext·V ([lens-ct](../../topics/physics/lens-ct.md)), heat); the board placer treats cells as particles with those moments coupled by far-field kernels, near pairs direct (FMM split). DR22's cells become a computational unit; far interactions O(N). Test: objective error and runtime at 50–300 parts.
- **LS26** Radiator selector by electrical size (*sharp, S; P1*). Per band pick the antenna by size vs λ: cables (λ/2 at 75–150 MHz for 1–2 m), board ground (resonant from ≈ 1–1.5 GHz at 100 mm), heatsinks, slots (λ/2 at ε_eff), cavity edges above f_p; then choose MREMC, edge-radiation, slot or full-wave model. Makes explicit and band-dependent the model choice that [fields-emi-budget](../../topics/physics/fields-emi-budget.md) fixes at MREMC. Test: vs full-wave dominant-radiator analysis, 5 board-and-cable setups.
- **LS27** Two-timescale condition for the joint optimizer (*new, S; P7*). Two-timescale stochastic approximation needs the inner loop (re-tightening, prices) to converge between outer moves: η_outer/η_inner → 0; monitor inner residual after each outer move, slow the outer loop when topological events cluster. Singular-perturbation basis for JPR13/JPR17/JPR18's heuristic hand-off thresholds. Test: convergence and final J with/without adaptive ratio on JPR synthetic boards.

## G. What each conductor is doing (P1, P3, P5)

- **LS28** Role ladder per conductor (*new, S; P1, P3*). Per body: f_mirror(a) = R_s/(πμ0a), f_x(h) = R_s/(2πμ0h), f_skin = ρ/(πμ0t²), f_TL = v/(10ℓ), f_res = v/(2ℓ), radiation (ka)³ at band top; label each band resistor, diffuser, mirror, line, resonator or antenna; store beside the [lens-cd](lens-cd.md) passport. Geometry only, before any solve; tells the LLM which physics applies per band. Test: labels vs solver-observed behavior, 50 bodies.
- **LS29** Observable-scale evaluation of conductor jobs (*new, M; P1, P5*). Evaluate a conductor's job at each budget's observable scale: at one frequency a ground plane is a mirror for a 0.1 mm-scale SI budget and an antenna arm for a 1 m cable CM budget; CD passport stores job(f, ℓ_obs); I1 attribution per scale. "Solid reference" and "drives the cable" both hold, at different scales. Test: 5 boards; does per-scale attribution reorder the EMI spenders.

## H. Calibration (gap 1)

- **LS30** ℓ_m coupon (*sharp, S; P3*). Trace over a plane with Kelvin sense traces at several offsets; lock-in 1 kHz–5 MHz; fit return width vs f to ℓ_m(f) = δ²/t -> effective plane thickness, plating, roughness; validates LS1. Isolates one kernel (transition law) as research-gaps gap 1 asks. Test: the coupon itself in 0.5, 1 and 2 oz.
- **LS31** f_p lattice coupon (*sharp, S; P5*). GND–GND and GND–PWR coupons with stitching or decap lattices at 3 pitches; VNA at a central via: first resonance (f_p) and port-to-port coupling vs distance below f_p. Validates decap reach, stitching cut-off and anti-resonance together (LS8). Test: f_p and decay length within ±10% of prediction.
- **LS32** ESD path as a non-perturbative structure (*sharp, S; P2*). Require ε_L = ω_knee·L_path/Z_clamp < 1, i.e. L_path ≤ Z_clamp/(2π·0.35/t_r) ([regime map](../../topics/physics/lens-ls.md#6-regime-map-and-causality-c)); return TVS ground through the nearest plane (0.03 nH), never through the board (1.3 nH) [C]. Adds the threshold where the parasitic becomes the circuit to RG22/DR47's path-inductance budgets. Test: SPICE with TVS models across L_path, then clamp voltage on coupons.
