## A. The board as a heat spreader

Numbers: [TP fin](../../topics/physics/thermal-physics.md#the-board-as-a-fin), [TP rules](../../topics/physics/thermal-physics.md#decompressing-the-ti-thermal-rules) [C].

- **TM1** Thermal spacing in units of λ (*sharp, S*). λ = √(G_s/h_total) per region (stackup, local copper fraction, cooling); thermal distances as r/λ. Below ≈2λ the placer stops spreading hot parts and maximises connected copper, edge and standoff exposure, and moats around victims. Test: 20 boards, FV victim ΔT for "5–10 mm apart" vs λ-aware placements.
- **TM2** Image-lattice thermal kernel (*sharp, S*). The FV 1 W response, precomputed per stackup and cooling case, superposed at each source plus mirror images for adiabatic edges ([TP superposition](../../topics/physics/thermal-physics.md#placement-scoring-by-superposition): ≈2% of a full solve), makes OP23's term rank placements. Test: rank correlation and absolute error vs FV across random placements and aspect ratios.
- **TM3** Two-tier thermal evaluation (*combo, S*). TM2 kernel for proposals, one FV solve per accepted move, the residual a learned correction for non-uniform copper (superposition is exact for a fixed copper map, so only copper edits invalidate the kernel). Test: optimiser wall time and final ΔT error vs FV-every-move.
- **TM4** Via return-on-investment critic (*sharp, S*). Add vias only while each buys > a set number of kelvin (∂T/∂N_via, ∂T/∂plating from FV): 9 vias cut the rise 13% with a top pour already spreading, 58% when the EP is the only top copper [C]. Replaces "as many vias as fit". Test: via count and ΔT vs the TH-11 rule on EP parts.
- **TM5** Heat-flux-aware cut penalty (*sharp, S*). Price every cut, slot, plane gap or trace crossing a spreader by ∫|q·n| dl of the flux −k∇T (across vs along: 20–30× [C]) instead of "no perpendicular cuts"; the router crosses spreaders radially. Test: rerun TI's SNVA419 rule-4 case; penalties vs FV ΔT on 50 random cuts.
- **TM6** Emissivity-aware copper exposure (*new, S*). Keep mask on spreaders (bare copper loses ~40% of its h, [TP h](../../topics/physics/thermal-physics.md#heat-transfer-coefficients)); expose copper only for solder or a thermal interface; use the same ε map for IR-camera checks. Test: FV with per-region ε vs IR image of masked and unmasked spreader.
- **TM7** Per-face cooling map (*sharp, S*). h per face and region from orientation, enclosure, airflow (downward face ≈ half the upward convection; less behind tall parts), carried in IR `heat_path` so every report shows it. Test: victim-ΔT sensitivity to the h map; flag decisions that flip within ±30% of h.
- **TM8** Thermal Δ-budget ledger (*combo, S*). T_J,max − T_ambient split across junction→case, pad/TIM, via array, spreading, convection as an I1 ledger in K; each object reports its spend ("via array 9 K of 40", "spreading 18 K"), the LLM moves budget, not mm. Test: 10 power boards, does the top spender predict the human's fix?
- **TM9** Pulsed-load copper mass (*new, M*). Pulses < ≈1 s diffuse only a few mm in copper ([TP pulsed](../../topics/physics/thermal-physics.md#pulsed-loads-diffusion-length-not-area)): local copper mass on the source's layer (thicker copper, a coin, dense pour) beats board area; transient Foster/Cauer ladder from the FV mesh for motor starts, transmitter bursts, inrush. Test: transient FV peak ΔT, local mass vs equal area spread wide.
- **TM10** Metal standoffs as heat sinks (*new, S*). A plated screw pad + brass M3 standoff to a metal chassis conducts like tens of cm² of board ([TP heatsinks](../../topics/physics/thermal-physics.md#heatsinks-interfaces-and-alternative-paths) [C]): hot parts near standoffs, each a placer thermal port. Test: FV with standoff boundary; thermocouple on a prototype.
- **TM11** Copper weight chosen at the margin (*combo, S*). ∂T/∂oz (1 → 2 oz cut a pad rise 22% [C]) vs the fab spacing penalty ≈ 47 µm + 1.65·t ([HV processes](../../topics/physics/hv-and-manufacturing-physics.md#manufacturing-processes-as-physics), can break fine-pitch escape), surfaced before placement. Test: decision quality on 10 boards vs the 1/2/4 oz by-watts rule.
- **TM12** Dual-purpose moats (*combo, S*). A U-slot or copper void around references, crystals, cold-junction sensors cuts heat flux and board stress (TI U-slot, [TP victims](../../topics/physics/thermal-physics.md#temperature-sensitive-victims)), the only effective thermal lever on boards < 2λ; size it from the FV flux and stress fields (TM42), not a template. Test: victim ΔT and gradient with/without moat; plate-FE stress proxy.

## B. Electro-thermal coupling and precision

- **TM13** Isotherm-aligned orientation (*new, S*). Rotate precision-DC parts (resistors, shunts, matched-pair inputs, Kovar-leaded packages) to minimise |∇T·d| between terminals and report the Seebeck µV (0603 cases: [TP victims](../../topics/physics/thermal-physics.md#temperature-sensitive-victims) [C]; ≈0 rotated 90°). Test: FV gradient × coefficient vs bench offset.
- **TM14** Thermal common-centroid (*combo, S*). Matched pairs, bridge arms, differential front ends symmetric about the axis of the computed heat field, not the geometric centre (analog-IC habit), scored by paired-terminal ΔT. Test: drift (°C, µV) of a bridge or in-amp front end in FV.
- **TM15** Victim drift budgets in their own units (*sharp, S*). Allowed drift → ΔT budget checked by THERM-COUPLE (crystal ppm/K², reference TC, electrolytic life, CJ error: TP victims table); reports read "RTC +1.2 s/day", "C12 life −45%", not "too close". Test: LLM-reviewer study, unit reports vs distance flags.
- **TM16** Electro-thermal fixed point for power copper (*sharp, S*). Iterate R(T) ↔ T(P) on the FV mesh (α = 0.393%/K; no steady state above ΔT₀ ≈ 254 K: [TP IPC](../../topics/physics/thermal-physics.md#ipc-22212152-as-a-thermal-model-vs-a-thermal-network)); flag αΔT₀ > 0.3; copper sized by its real hottest point. Test: FV fixed point vs IPC-2152 sizing, 1 oz and 2 oz stages. Complements OP11.
- **TM17** Attract and repel thermal relations (*new, S*). Typed IR relations `thermal_isolate(victim, aggressor, ΔT_max)` and `thermal_couple(sensor, target, R_th_max)`: protection NTCs, over-temperature sensors, matched transistors need coupling, references isolation; push-apart rules misplace protection sensors. Test: NTC-to-FET R_th on motor-driver boards.
- **TM18** Per-net trace thermal network instead of IPC charts (*sharp, S*). Trace = line source with line conductance from its fin and any plane below, neighbours' heat superposed, TCR fixed point (TP IPC section [C]); IPC-2221 stays the conservative fallback, log which bound. Test: FV trace-temperature map vs IR on a current-loaded coupon.
- **TM19** Hot-copper IR-drop correction (*sharp, S*). Report IR drop at the computed copper temperature (+16% R at 40 K, TP IPC section [C]); flips pass → fail on low-voltage (≤ 1.2 V) heavily loaded rails. Test: rail budgets that change verdict on FPGA and SoC boards.

## C. High-voltage insulation

Numbers: [HV field](../../topics/physics/hv-and-manufacturing-physics.md#field-enhancement-at-copper-corners-and-edges), [HV altitude](../../topics/physics/hv-and-manufacturing-physics.md#altitude-and-paschen), [HV insulation](../../topics/physics/hv-and-manufacturing-physics.md#insulation-is-three-physics-problems) [C].

- **TM20** Inception-margin critic (*new, M*). Per HV net pair: 2-D BEM field (≈1 s per corner) and the streamer integral ∫(E−E₀)²ds along the worst field lines at true air density → kV margin vs peak working + transient voltage; sharp corners raise E_max > 10× but cut inception only 30–43%. Test: predicted inception vs partial-discharge (PD) measurements on coupons.
- **TM21** Altitude as air density, with a Paschen-minimum regime switch (*sharp, S*). Clearance multiplier p_ref/p(h) (IEC factors within ~1% to 20 km); above ~15–20 km or in a low-pressure bay every node pair > ~300 V peak needs solid insulation (coating, potting, buried layers). Test: Paschen curve vs gap and altitude; chamber hi-pot of coated vs uncoated coupons.
- **TM22** HV corner fillets as a generated feature (*sharp, S*). Fillet every convex HV corner to r ≥ g/2 (field factor 1.74, ≈90% of uniform-gap inception back) as arcs; report margin gained, never a reduced legal clearance (case A already assumes a 30 µm point). Test: field factor before/after on real HV boards; PD inception filleted vs sharp coupons.
- **TM23** Three-mechanism insulation model (*combo, S*). Clearance, creepage with X-width bridging and CAF/through-laminate checks per net pair, each with its own metric and source (HV insulation table); one "spacing" number conflates them. Test: seeded-defect board where each mechanism fails alone.
- **TM24** CAF-aware via placement (*sharp, S*). Via pairs at different DC potentials (power–ground pins, HV dividers, battery stacks) get a hole-wall-gap budget, staggered off the glass-yarn axes, adjacent bias locations minimised (Isola [P], HV insulation). Test: count of in-grid opposite-polarity via pairs < 0.5 mm before/after; THB coupons.
- **TM25** Bury the HV (*new, S*). Functional HV nets on inner layers (IPC-2221B 0.25 mm internal vs 2.5 mm external at 301–500 V [P]) with stitched guard planes at layer transitions; safety isolation still follows solid-insulation rules. Test: area saved on an HV measurement board; PD and hi-pot buried vs surface coupons.
- **TM26** Coating as a modelled region (*new, S*). Conformal-coat keep-in/keep-out drawn in the layout (connectors, test points, adjustable parts out) enters the creepage solver as PD1 (IEC 60664-3), traded against distance; never credit solder mask. Test: creepage savings vs coating area and process cost.
- **TM27** Voltage-monotone placement for HV chains (*new, S*). Divider strings, bleeder chains, multiplier ladders, cell-balancing resistors placed monotone in voltage keep peak field between non-adjacent nodes low and the leakage path long where ΔV is large; folding a chain puts its highest ΔV across the shortest gap. Test: peak E and min distance-per-volt, before/after.
- **TM28** Guard rings for HV leakage (*combo, S*). Driven guards at node potential around high-impedance HV sense nodes intercept surface leakage (10⁹–10¹² Ω/□ humid surface resistivity) and shape the field; generated from `barrier` and `coupling(leakage)`. Test: leakage error vs humidity on a divider coupon with/without guard.
- **TM29** HV test coupon co-design (*new, S*). A panel coupon copying the board's TM20 worst geometry (smallest corner radius, gap, coating, via spacing) is hi-pot or PD-tested instead of the product. Test: coupon vs product PD inception correlation.

## D. Fabrication processes

Numbers: [HV processes](../../topics/physics/hv-and-manufacturing-physics.md#manufacturing-processes-as-physics) [C].

- **TM30** Critical-area yield cost in the router (*sharp, S*). D₀·Σ(L·x₀²/s + L·x₀²/w) (1/x³ defect sizes) in routing cost spreads and widens traces where room exists: "generously wide when unconstrained" made optimisable. Test: total critical area and length vs baseline router at equal completion.
- **TM31** σ-margin map instead of pass/fail DRC (*combo, S*). Every feature's margin to the fab limit in process σ (etch: width, space; drill and registration: rings; plating: vias); the critic lists the least-margin 1% and expected escapes. Test: predicted marginal features vs fab engineering queries on real orders.
- **TM32** Directional teardrops by breakout probability (*sharp, S*). Add teardrops where expected breakouts per board, Q(ring/σ), exceed a budget; skip RF and fast nets (capacitance costs more). Test: teardrop count and added C vs add-everywhere default.
- **TM33** Plating-uniformity field (*combo, S*). Per outer layer, copper density at the 5–10 mm plating-cell scale plus a plating index like Eurocircuits'; fix low density inside copper synthesis (OP18); thieving grounded and stitched or kept from signals (no floating resonator). Test: plating index and floating-copper count vs post-hoc thieving.
- **TM34** Etch-aware cross-sections everywhere (*sharp, S*). Trapezoidal conductors (undercut t/EF per side) in R, impedance and J models; request etch compensation in fab notes where it matters. Test: model vs measured R of 0.1 and 0.2 mm coupons.
- **TM35** Impedance tolerance budget by variance share (*sharp, S*). Monte Carlo: dielectric thickness dominates Z0 variance, width 13–35%, Dk 6–8% [C]; to tighten Z0 ask for thickness control and prefer thicker dielectrics (wider lines, smaller relative width error), in stackup choice and fab notes. Test: coupon Z0 spread across stackups.
- **TM36** Laminate-theory warp proxy (*sharp, S*). Curvature from per-layer copper fraction, effective CTE and modulus, and each layer pair's moment about the midplane (outer imbalance counts most); flag > ~0.3%, balance outer layers first. Test: shadow-moiré or feeler-gauge bow vs prediction on a test panel.
- **TM37** Throwing-power-aware via sizing (*sharp, S*). Drill from board thickness and bath class via TP = 1/cosh(L/2Λ): a 3.2 mm board needs larger drills than 1.6 mm at equal reliability [C], which a fixed aspect-ratio limit misses. Test: predicted vs measured barrel thickness in coupon cross-sections.
- **TM38** Acid-trap test as morphology (*sharp, S*). Flag only copper-free pockets that close under an opening of radius ≈ half the fab min space, weighted by copper thickness, on flex or ≥ 1 oz fine features, not every acute angle (failure evidence is forum-level [S]). Test: false-flag rate vs `track_angle`-style checks on 50 boards.

## E. Assembly and soldering

Numbers: [HV solder](../../topics/physics/hv-and-manufacturing-physics.md#solder-joint-physics) [C].

- **TM39** Pad thermal-environment check for tombstoning (*new, M*). Flag 2-terminal parts ≤ 0402 whose pads' reflow time constants (tied plane, heavy parts on it, shadowing) differ by Δτ above a threshold; heavy parts on the plane set the asynchrony, reliefs barely change it; fix by re-placement, symmetric environment or long neck. Test: assembler tombstone rates vs Δτ.
- **TM40** Reflow shadow and mass map (*new, M*). Each heavy part's (shields, inductors, connectors, large BGAs) effect on nearby plane temperature during the ramp, from its time constant and copper diffusion length; keep small passives off planes tied to such masses or give symmetric ties (reflow analogue of TM2). Test: oven-profile thermocouples at predicted cold spots.
- **TM41** Via-in-pad theft ratio (*sharp, S*). Open-barrel volume ÷ printed solder volume per via in a pad; above ~0.3 tent, plug, fill or shrink the drill; settles the TH-02 tenting conflict per pad. Test: voiding and starved-joint rates vs ratio from assembly X-ray.
- **TM42** Relief policy by process, not habit (*sharp, S*). Reliefs where an iron or wave must beat the plane drain (THT pins on multilayer planes), direct connect for reflow-only SMD power and hot-loop pads (reliefs cost tenths of a mΩ, tens of pH); the assembly process in the IR makes the choice automatic. Test: hand-solderability and R per policy. Complements OP9.

## F. Mechanics

Numbers: [HV mechanics](../../topics/physics/hv-and-manufacturing-physics.md#mechanics) [C].

- **TM43** Strain-field placement for brittle parts (*new, M*). Plate-bending load cases (V-score snap, screw-boss mismatch, ICT fixture, connector insertion) orient MLCCs (long axis parallel to bend lines, Murata [P]), BGAs, crystals by principal strain vs part budgets (≈2,000 µε standard MLCC, 5,000 soft termination). Test: plate-FE strain vs strain gauges.
- **TM44** Mount points designed against flatness (*sharp, S*). Standoff height mismatch as a load case: brittle parts outside the radius where ε = 6tδ/L² exceeds their budget, or compliant mounts, or a thicker board (0.8 mm boards: half the strain, lower f₁). Test: MLCC failure rate near bosses in field returns.
- **TM45** Mode-aware placement for vibration (*combo, M*). First plate modes with part masses: heavy/large parts near supports or nodal lines, mounts added to push f₁ above the excitation band, Steinberg displacement limit per part. Test: shaker-table resonance vs prediction.
- **TM46** Connector load paths (*sharp, S*). Mating and cable forces (10–100 N) go into THT anchors, bosses, nearby mounting holes, not SMT pad peel; frequently mated connectors sit a short lever arm from a mount; score moment arm and pad stress. Test: pull and lever tests, SMT-only vs anchored footprints.

## G. Curves and geometry at the fab

- **TM47** Curves where physics pays (*combo, S*). Arcs and any-angle copper for HV corners (TM22), flex bends, RF bends, inner corners of pulsed-current pours; 45° elsewhere for editability; curves cost the fab nothing [I], only router support and hand editing ([HV curves](../../topics/physics/hv-and-manufacturing-physics.md#curves-and-angles-at-the-fab)). Test: per-class physics gain vs edit time in a user study.
- **TM48** Arc-native output with exact radii (*sharp, S*). HV fillets, flex bends, synthesised pours as true arcs (Gerber G02/G03, KiCad PCB_ARC), not polylines: exact radii for critics, small files; record each fab's CAM arc support in its profile. Test: arc round-trip through KiCad and two fab CAM viewers. Related: OP53.
- **TM49** Flex bend geometry from strain (*sharp, S*). Bend zones: perpendicular arcs staggered across layers (no I-beaming); strain ε = t/(2R) from bend radius and position vs neutral axis under the copper fatigue budget (tighter for dynamic flex). Test: predicted strain vs vendor bend-radius tables.

## H. Encoding manufacturing physics

- **TM50** Origin-typed constraints (*combo, S*). Each constraint tagged with origin (physics, fab capability, standard/legal, habit) and enforcement class ([HV encoding](../../topics/physics/hv-and-manufacturing-physics.md#encoding-manufacturing-physics)): physics may tighten a legal limit, never relax it; habit goes to a style channel. Test: tag 300 checklist rows by origin; count rules that become soft or disappear.
- **TM51** Fab process model, not a fab table (*new, M*). Each fab as distributions (etch σ by copper weight, drill and registration σ, plating class by Wagner number, lamination warp tendency, impedance-coupon spread), fitted from capability pages and coupons and read by all yield critics (TM30–TM37). Test: predicted vs reported hold and query rates per fab.
- **TM52** Manufacturing floors on electrical budgets (*new, S*). Show the best value each budget can reach under the fab profile (min hot-loop L from min width, space, dielectric; min relief L under the assembly policy; max pour asymmetry under the warp budget) beside the budget; an infeasible budget becomes a requirement conflict to negotiate. Test: how often the LLM negotiates fab-infeasible budgets.
- **TM53** Layout-derived test plan (*new, S*). Hi-pot and PD levels from `barrier`, IR-camera checkpoints and emissivity notes from `heat_path`, strain-gauge locations for ICT and depanel, coupon lists for impedance, CAF, HV: verification as layout output. Test: coverage of a human test plan on 5 products.
- **TM54** Calibrate with the IR camera (*new, M*). Fit h, contact resistances, emissivity in the FV model to one emissivity-corrected IR image of the first prototype; store fitted priors per enclosure and product family. Test: prediction error on a second board after calibrating on the first.
