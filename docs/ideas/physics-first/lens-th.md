# Physics-first ideas: thermodynamics, dissipation, noise and irreversibility (TH)

Scope: TH1–TH35, the layout as a dissipative system: every unwanted joule needs a destination (designed for rings above ~50 MHz); every loss is a noise source and a heat source; every conductive heat path is an electrical element; damage accumulates where loss and stress concentrate. ## A. Where every joule goes

- **TH1** Modal Q ledger (*new, M*; TP4). Per detected mode (hot-loop rings, LC filters, PDN anti-resonances, plane cavities, cable CM) record frequency, Q, loss-channel shares (copper Q = h/δ, dielectric tanδ, devices and capacitors, designed dampers, radiation), excitation (ΔI, ΔV) and predicted ring-band emission; budget row "designed share ≥ 50%" wherever excitation × Q exceeds the emission or overshoot budget. Res carries frequencies only; ring-band emission rises with Q until the edge spectrum takes over, so damping buys dB there [C, [topic Results](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md)]. Test: 5 converter EVMs, predicted ring-band levels and fix rankings vs near-field scans.
- **TH2** Copper-only Q alarm (*sharp, S*; TP4, D1). Flag modes whose only loss channels are copper and laminate as undamped; Q_cu = h/δ from stackup and frequency at F1 cost, valid for rings above ~50 MHz (plane pairs below ~30 MHz on thin cores get Q 1–10 from copper alone [C, rt_sheet_return]). Catches LC filters, hot loops, plane pairs with no designed loss; retires "the copper will damp it": Q_cu = 8–720 for gaps 0.05–1.5 mm at 0.1–1 GHz [C]. Test: mutation benchmark deletes snubbers and ESR parts; alarm fires at the planted site.
- **TH3** Tuned snubber synthesis (*combo, S*; TP4, D3; builds on XD19, XD81, PW29). Choose R_s, C_s and the snubber's mounting inductance L_s together, L_s·C_s ≈ L·C_oss (Den Hartog tuned absorber); L_s realized by part size, via count, trace length, so snubber placement is a joint-optimizer tuning variable; accept only after a ±2× C_oss robustness check and an overshoot check. Same Q at a third of the loss, at the cost of robustness to C_oss ([D3 table](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md#2-derivation-map-then-derivations)) [C]. Test: transient SPICE with vendor GaN and Si models, then one bench half-bridge with three snubber geometries.
- **TH4** Damper placement by mode shape (*sharp, S*; TP4). Compute each target mode's eigenvector (PEEC loop network, cavity or cable-CM model); shunt dampers (RC, controlled-ESR caps, terminations) at voltage antinodes, series dampers (beads, R∥L damping links, gate resistors) at current antinodes, sites weighted by energy participation. Replaces "ferrite at the connector", "snubber close"; generalizes XD78 (cavities only) to every mode. Test: Q reduction per candidate site on cavity, LC filter, cable CM, model and one board.
- **TH5** Damping price in watts and band (*new, S*; TP4/TP1). Ledger row per damping topology: shunt RC costs C·V²·f; series R I_rms²·R; frequency-selective damper (DC-blocked RC, R across a bead, lossy edge band) ≈ the ring energy alone; pick the topology whose dissipation spectrum lies outside the working band. 12 V, 10 A, 500 kHz buck: textbook snubber 216 mW vs 25 + 36 mW unavoidable turn-off energy [C]. Test: 10 converters, ledger snubber loss vs measured efficiency ± snubber.
- **TH6** Ring term in the EMI chain (*sharp, S*; TP4). Add a ring line to the [fields-emi-budget](../../topics/physics/fields-emi-budget.md) chain (trapezoid-only today): amplitude ≈ ΔI·Z0, spectral density ∝ Q_m/ω_r at f_r, through the CM path; damping (Q) and ring steering (f_r) become levers priced in dB and W beside edge rate and topology. Rings sit at 30–300 MHz, where cables resonate. Test: chain ± ring term vs radiated or near-field scans of 5 EVMs.
- **TH7** Self-damping stackup term (*new, S*; TP4, D4). In stackup synthesis (DR30, PW28) score each PWR–GND pair by Z_pk ∝ d·Q(d), Q = 1/(tanδ + δ/d); flag a move to low-loss laminate as a PDN risk ([D4](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md#2-derivation-map-then-derivations)) [C]. "Thin = more capacitance" is the only argument used for thin cores; the damping half is missing. Test: cavity model vs VNA on two coupons (FR-4 vs low-loss, same d).
- **TH8** Hybrid-loss stackups (*combo, M*; TP4). Stackup optimizer picks dielectric loss per layer pair: lossy (FR-4 or high-tanδ embedded capacitance) in power cores, low-loss in signal layers; one material currently serves opposite goals (SI wants low loss, PDN damping wants loss). Test: SI loss budget and PDN peak impedance for three hybrid vs uniform stackups, fab cost from the cost oracle.
- **TH9** Termination pitch from ESL (*sharp, S*; TP4, D4). PWR–GND edge terminations at pitch p < η0·d/(√εr·ωL_esl), R ≈ η0·d/(√εr·p); if impractical, thinner core or resistive edge band. Makes FE31 buildable: pitch and ESL penalty in [D4](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md#2-derivation-map-then-derivations) [C]. Test: cavity model + coupon S21 at two pitches.
- **TH10** Absorptive I/O filtering (*new, S*; TP4). At cell and cable boundaries prefer filters whose Re Z dominates at the cable's CM resonance (lossy ferrites, RC); check modal Q of filter + cable; reject lossless LC that rings with the cable (reflects energy back, peaks at Q = √(L/C)/R). Test: CM current spectra on a harness, reactive vs absorptive filters, simulated and bench.
- **TH11** Damped heatsink and shield bonds (*new, S*; TP4). Heatsink or shield can = capacitance (~22 pF class) in series with bond inductance; compute resonance and Q; add damping path R ≈ √(L_bond/C) parallel to the low-inductance bond so it stays effective above resonance. DR28 picks the bond node; nobody checks the bond's own ring. Test: openEMS of heatsink + bond ± R, then near-field scan.
- **TH12** Layout-born dissipators as ECOs (*new, S*; TP4). Synthesizer may propose parts the schematic lacks (edge terminations, damping links, tuned snubber geometry, controlled-ESR caps), each priced in W, area, dB, confirmed by the human; minimizing parasitics alone cannot damp a mode, so designed dissipation must be a sanctioned action class. Test: 10 boards, count binding EMI or PDN budgets met only with layout-born parts.

## B. Every loss is a noise source

- **TH13** Fluctuation–dissipation back-annotation (*new, M*; TP3). In the LII loop extract lossy parasitics as noisy passives (R_ac(f) as Foster ladders, dielectric conductance ωC·tanδ, ferrite Re Z(f), leakage conductance), run real `.noise`, include noise outputs in LII; DR3 already runs `.noise`, so layout noise is free; passivity enforcement (SIM33) becomes a noise-correctness requirement. Test: fA TIA and LNA front ends, predicted vs measured noise floors.
- **TH14** Dielectric-noise critic for high-impedance nodes (*new, S*; TP3). Nodes above ~1 GΩ (electrometers, photodiode TIAs, charge amplifiers, ion sensors): sum C·tanδ of touched dielectric (pads, trace, guard gap), compare 4kT·ωC·tanδ with amplifier and feedback-resistor noise. Remedies: smaller pads, slots or air gaps, PTFE inserts, lifting the node; not a guard ring (removes dielectric signal current, not its thermal noise current) ([topic Results](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md)) [C]. Test: bench noise of two TIA layouts, FR-4 guard ring vs slot or PTFE island.
- **TH15** Front-end loss ledger in kelvin (*sharp, S*; TP3). Charge every element between antenna/sensor and first gain stage (matching-component Q, ESD-diode loss, FR-4 line loss) as T_add = (L − 1)·T_phys; antenna adds (1 − η)·T_phys; turns "LNA close to the antenna" into a budget. GNSS antenna η = 0.5 adds 2.7 dB noise on top of its 3 dB gain loss [C]. Test: predicted vs measured C/N0 on two GNSS boards with different antenna/LNA placements.
- **TH16** Lossy-material participation near antennas (*combo, M*; TP3/TP4). Participation of lossy regions (FR-4, batteries, ferrites, displays) in the antenna mode as in XD84, charged as efficiency and as noise temperature; move lossy parts out of high-participation regions (binary keep-outs miss that loss costs twice). Test: EPR-predicted efficiency change vs chamber, three battery positions.
- **TH17** Copper Johnson-noise keep-out for magnetometers (*new, S*; TP3). Near optically pumped or SQUID-class sensors budget δB = μ0√(kTσt)/(√(8π)·a) per copper layer: one 1 oz plane gives 72 fT/√Hz at 10 mm, 361 at 2 mm [C], above their 10–20 fT/√Hz floors; slot or remove pours, thinner copper or move the sensor; fluxgate and Hall sensors exempt. Test: magnetometer noise spectra ± nearby pours.
- **TH18** Thermal-EMF fluctuation budget (*new, M*; TP5/TP3). nV-class DC front ends: differential temperature fluctuation spectrum per junction pair = air turbulence filtered by local thermal RC × ΔS; levers: covers, thermal mass on both junctions, shared copper islands (make fluctuations common-mode). TM13 and DR27 treat thermal EMF as static offset; at a 7 nV rms ADC floor, mK fluctuations × µV/K matter. Test: 0.01–1 Hz noise of covered vs uncovered input stage.

## C. Heat paths are electrical elements

- **TH19** Wiedemann–Franz dual solve (*new, S*; TP8). Reuse the DC (IR-drop) factorization as the copper part of the thermal network, G_th = L·T·G_el; add only dielectric and convection terms (OP1 kept separate coefficient sets; for metal one constant links them; via example: [§3](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md#3-convergences-and-synergies)) [C]. Test: WF copper thermal resistance vs tm_board_thermal_fv on 10 copper shapes.
- **TH20** Electrical twin of every heat path (*combo, S*; TP8). Tag each thermal element (via arrays, TIM pads, thermal prepreg, standoffs, heatsink contacts) with its electrical shadow for EMI and isolation ledgers: metal → Ω via Wiedemann–Franz, dielectric → pF via k/ε (exchange rates: [topic Results](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md)) [C]. Unifies heatsink capacitance, standoff bonds, switch-node heat spreading (S4); blocks thermal fixes that create antennas or breach isolation. Test: 10 boards, count thermal edits changing a Vcm or isolation verdict.
- **TH21** Radiative boundary as a design input (*sharp, S*; TP8; extends TM6, TM21). Weak convection (altitude, vacuum, sealed enclosures) → radiation-dominant model; per-region emissivity (mask vs bare finishes) and solar absorptance become layout variables; conduction through standoffs first. Altitude and mask numbers: [TP8](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md#1-principles) [C, rough]. Test: thermal-vacuum test of two mask finishes on one design.
- **TH22** Warm-zone placement for leakage-critical nodes (*new, S*; TP2/TP7). Local warming lowers surface RH ([lens-th open questions](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md#open-questions)) [C]: when humidity/condensation binds, put HV creepage regions and GΩ nodes in warm regions (or add a small heater); when drift binds, cool isothermal regions. "Keep sensitive parts cool" is wrong when leakage binds. Test: dew-cycling leakage tests, warm vs cold placement.
- **TH23** Plume-aware thermal kernel (*new, M*; TP2). Vertical boards in natural convection: add an upwind (advective) term so "upstream" becomes a computed non-reciprocal coupling θ_ij ≠ θ_ji (TM2, OP23 kernels are reciprocal; rules TH-06, TH-07 encode advection). Test: IR camera on a vertical board, heater below vs above a sensor.

## D. Thermoelectric precision

- **TH24** Thermoelectric loop-integral critic (*sharp, S*; TP5). EMF = Σ_k ΔS_k·(T_k,in − T_k,out) along each precision loop; ΔS from a BOM-keyed material table (solder, nickel barrier, tin plating, Kovar or Alloy-42 leads, resistor elements, connector and relay contacts), T from the FV field; report µV and top three segments. TM13 orients single parts; this sums the loop incl. connectors, relays, leaded parts. Test: bench offset vs prediction with a controllable heater.
- **TH25** Differential congruence check (*sharp, S*; TP5). The two legs' non-copper segment chains must be congruent by translation along isotherms or reflection across the gradient direction; residual ≈ ΔS·ℓ·s·∂²T (leg separation s); placer gets it as a constraint. A P/N layout mirrored across the gradient doubles the EMF instead of cancelling (DR27 states only the good case). Test: FV + loop integral, mirrored vs translated.
- **TH26** Thermal-dipole susceptibility map (*new, S*; TP2/TP5). By reciprocity one thermal solve per precision loop (unit sources of opposite sign at each non-copper segment's ends, weighted by ΔS) gives µV per watt for every heat-source location, read by the placer as a lookup instead of a re-solve per move. Complements XD35 (EM) and TM2 (forward kernel). Test: map vs direct FV EMF for 50 source positions.
- **TH27** Shunt thermal symmetry and alloy rule (*new, S*; TP5, D5). Constrain both shunt pads to equal thermal resistance (copper area, vias, distance to heat sources); choose alloy by the S²/L0 bound at design current ([D5](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md#2-derivation-map-then-derivations)) [C]. Test: DC vs pulsed resistance on symmetric and asymmetric pads.
- **TH28** Harman coupon for current sensing (*new, S*; TP5). Measure R_DC/R_AC or the slow tail after a current step on the as-built shunt to calibrate Peltier and asymmetry terms; add to the F4 coupon panel. Test: coupon with manganin and constantan shunts.

## E. Electro-thermal stability

- **TH29** Electro-thermal margin critic (*new, S*; TP6, D6; generalizes TM16). g = ρ(D^½ΘD^½) from FV thermal matrix Θ and datasheet dP/dT (R_DS(on) TC, copper TCR, diode and BJT TC, 0 for LDOs); report amplification 1/(1−g) and board-floor bound Σα_iP_i/(h_tot·A); flag g > 0.3. On small boards the margin is board-level ([D6](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md#2-derivation-map-then-derivations)) [C]. Test: FV fixed point vs bench, four-FET board at three loads.
- **TH30** Mode-aware thermal relations for paralleled parts (*sharp, S*; TP6; sharpens TM17). Classify by sign of ∂I/∂T at fixed V: negative-TC parts (BJTs, diodes, linear-mode and hot-swap FETs) get `thermal_couple` (minimize θ11 − θ12, share a pad or die, ballast budget); positive-TC parts get spacing only if g binds. "Spread hot parts" is wrong for half the cases: moving closer or sharing a die raises the hogging limit ([D6](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md#2-derivation-map-then-derivations)) [C]. Test: current sharing of paralleled BJTs or diodes at two spacings.
- **TH31** Thermal back-annotation into SPICE (*new, M*; TP6). Per-instance TEMP from the thermal field into the netlist, iterated with losses, so LII (DR10, single temperature today) sees shifts in R_DS(on), V_f, reference drift, crystal ppm, MLCC C(T). Test: predicted vs measured output drift of a reference + ADC board with a heater.

## F. Irreversible damage

- **TH32** Two-kernel life map (*new, M*; TP7). Arrhenius damage from the DC kernel (mean T), fatigue from the complex-screening AC kernel at mission power-cycle frequencies, Miner sum per part. Swing is local, mean board-wide ([transfers](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md#4-transfers)) [C]: one "keep away" radius cannot serve both. Test: thermal-cycling coupons; transient FV vs AC kernel.
- **TH33** Fourier-number gate for corner rounding (*sharp, S*; TP7, D7). Round inside corners only where t_pulse < w²/α_Cu ([D7](../../topics/physics/lens-th.md#2-derivation-map-then-derivations)), or where fields or HV bind, replacing the fixed "~1 ms". Test: transient 2-D sheet solves at three widths.
- **TH34** Crowded-pin electromigration check (*new, S*; TP7). High-current fine-pitch pins (QFN power pins, BGA balls): pad-entry current density incl. crowding from the sheet solve at pin temperature vs ~10⁴ A/cm² for Sn-based solder [S]; lever = feed geometry (pour all around the pad vs one-sided trace). Copper is safe; the solder joint is the weak point. Test: predicted crowding sites vs published EM failure sites, then EM coupons.
- **TH35** Damage-weighted copper shapes (*combo, M*; TP1/TP7; extends XD8, OP11). After a loss-optimal pour (p = 2) continue toward a damage norm (p ≈ 4–8, Arrhenius-weighted by T) only where the life budget binds: rounds entries and corners only where life is at stake. Test: life-weighted vs loss-optimal shapes on the OP2 test pour.
