## A. Evaluator contract and infrastructure

- **SIM1** Evaluator contract with σ, validity and cost (combo, S): every physics call returns {value, σ, unit, rung, validity in/edge/out, provenance, cost, cache key}, consumed by optimizers, critics, ledger; a bare number cannot say whether it may be exploited. Test: with V1-style mutants, an out-of-domain value never raises a reward.
- **SIM2** Validity domains as code (sharp, S): each closed form ships its published range (w/h, εr, frequency, t/h, geometry class) and flags/refuses calls outside it. IPC-2141 drifts from +2% to −65% as w/h goes 1 → 6 [C]; no rule pack stops that use today. Test: validity linter over every rule-pack formula on 50 boards; count out-of-domain uses and their error vs a 2-D solve.
- **SIM3** Two-axis verdicts, field rung × circuit rung (new, S): every verdict names a pair, e.g. (F2, C1) = PEEC inductance into a lumped LC ring model; each budget declares the minimum pair for sign-off (USB3 channel might need (F3, C3)); a C0 estimate (L·di/dt) cannot pass as SPICE-level. Test: sign-off audit fails when a verdict sits below its declared pair.
- **SIM4** Regime selector from dimensionless numbers (new, S): l/λ, t/δ, ωL/R, τ_thermal/t_pulse per object pick the solver class. A hot loop with l/λ < 0.03 at 1 GHz never goes to FDTD ([cost table](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md#cost-per-sub-problem)) [C/I]. Test: on 100 objects the selected class agrees with F3 within 10%.
- **SIM5** Port synthesis from the physics IR (combo, M): IR ports are terminal pairs; deck writers for PEEC, FastHenry (internal use only), openEMS, Palace generate geometry + ports and check each reference terminal sits on the intended return conductor (wrong port references: the classic silent field-solver error [I]). Test: auto vs hand-built decks on 10 structures, plus 10 seeded port mistakes.
- **SIM6** Solver golden tests in CI (sharp, S): fixed decks with known answers every commit: Hammerstad-Jensen inside its range; the four FastHenry rectangles (PEEC within 1.3%; 4 loops, w/h 0.3–5, 100 MHz); cavity f₁₀; Ott's radiation formulas. Test: seed 10 plumbing bugs (mm vs m, εr on wrong layer, flipped port); CI catches all.
- **SIM46** One PEEC core for every tool (sharp, S): merge the three prototypes sharing Grover kernels, image-plane loops ([sl_loop_peec.py](../../../tools/research/physics/sl_loop_peec.py)), nodal R + jωL with vias ([pw_peec.py](../../../tools/research/physics/pw_peec.py)), meshed plane ([fe_peec_plane.py](../../../tools/research/physics/fe_peec_plane.py)), into one library behind SIM1 so tools cannot disagree through re-implementation. Test: the scripts' published [C] numbers reproduce within 1%.

## B. Trust, calibration and escalation

- **SIM7** Calibrate on decision quality, not R² (new, S): score each proxy by how often it picks the true best from realistic candidate sets, and regret otherwise. Best-of-10 hot loops: MS2 picks well, plan-view area poorly on mixed stackups, plain trace length well at fixed stackup ([trust radii](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md#measured-trust-radii-for-loop-inductance-c)) [C]. Correlation over random layouts overstates usefulness when choices are near-ties. Test: report both metrics per registered proxy; check which predicts final-design quality.
- **SIM8** Conformal σ per feature region (combo, S): split-conformal residual quantiles (F1 vs F2, F2 vs F3) give finite-sample coverage, widening where calibration data is sparse. Test: empirical coverage within ±3% of nominal on held-out loop families and optimizer outputs.
- **SIM9** Goodhart audit every generation (sharp, S): send top-k proxy winners (not random samples) to the next rung, track F2/F1; shrink the trust region on drift (optimizers concentrate where the proxy errs). Test: CMA-ES on area, MS, MS2 with/without audit; true-nH regret.
- **SIM10** Value-of-information escalation (combo, S): escalate only if |budget − value| < z·σ (z from the criticality field), or its interval overlaps the leader's among top-k and the choice matters downstream. Hot-loop data: MS2 misses few choices, cheaply, so escalation is rare ([VOI escalation](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md#4-value-of-information-escalation)) [C]. Test: same final quality as "always F2" with far fewer calls on 30 boards.
- **SIM11** Cross-solver disagreement as model-error alarm (new, S): two independent F2 methods on critical objects (image PEEC + meshed plane, or PEEC + FV); disagreement beyond calibrated errors → F3. Catches modelling mistakes (missed plane void, wrong port) that σ cannot. Test: detection rate on seeded modelling faults.
- **SIM12** Coupon panel per stackup (F4) (new, M): standard panel per stackup and fab, measured by VNA/TDR, thermal camera, near-field probe: hot-loop ladders of known geometry, decap mounts with 1–4 vias, via transitions, slots across references, thermal pads with via arrays; residuals train per-stackup bias models in the ledger with provenance. Test: bias fitted on lot A predicts lot B within σ.
- **SIM13** Vendor data with mounting de-embedding (combo, M): de-embed the vendor fixture from capacitor/inductor S-parameters, re-embed our pad + via + plane mounting from F2 → in-situ ESL; vector-fit and cache per part number ("an 0402 has ESL X" is a fixture statement). Test: in-situ predictions vs coupon VNA for 5 parts.
- **SIM14** Calibrate on the optimizer's own distribution (sharp, M): weight calibration samples by density ratio optimizer-visited/random (covariate-shift correction). Test: calibration error on optimizer outputs with/without weighting.

## C. Fast physics kernels (F1/F2)

- **SIM15** MS2 as default loop-inductance spend model (sharp, S): MS2 = Σ length·L′(w,h) (air Hammerstad Z0/c) − 2·overlap·(µ0/4π)·ln(1+(2h/s)²) coupled-line mutuals; µs cost; its stable bias is removed by one scalar per stackup. Replaces "minimize loop area" and µ0·h·l/w (rank and bias: [trust radii](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md#measured-trust-radii-for-loop-inductance-c)) [C]. Test: N7 micro-placer designs, MS2 vs area, scored by PEEC.
- **SIM16** Image-plane multi-filament PEEC as F2 default (sharp, S): N filaments across each width, HF current split solved, PEC image for the plane; ms per loop vs seconds for FastHenry ([cost table](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md#cost-per-sub-problem)); accuracy and filament count: [verification-and-limits](../../topics/physics/verification-and-limits.md#1-claims-verified-by-an-independent-method) (use ≥ 31 graded filaments) [C]. Escalate to meshed plane only where RET-COV finds voids within ~3h. Test: 30 extracted hot loops, some with voids, vs meshed-plane references.
- **SIM17** Hybrid image + local plane mesh (new, M): image for intact plane, mesh only cells within a few h of voids, slots, antipad rows; cost scales with the defect. Test: accuracy vs full meshed planes on slot crossings; cost vs FastHenry's 56–115 s per case.
- **SIM18** Cross-section table service (combo, S): solve each unique cross-section (stackup, layer, width and gap, neighbours, solder mask, etch profile) once by 2-D FD/BEM (0.2–1.5 s), cache RLGC; routers price per mm from it; Hammerstad-Jensen (1–2.5 µs, within 0.7%) covers plain microstrip in range [C]. Test: cache hit rate over 50 boards; error vs per-instance solves.
- **SIM19** Plane-pair modal basis for real fills (combo, M): first N eigenmodes of each plane pair's 2-D Helmholtz problem on the fill raster; port impedances anywhere are modal sums (2–300 ms per sweep, like the rectangle cavity model [C]); moving a decap re-weights terms; extends the cavity model to perforated fills. Test: vs 2.5-D solve and Palace eigenmodes on 5 boards.
- **SIM20** Differentiable F1/F2 in JAX (combo, M): MS2, PEEC and FV sheet solves with implicit differentiation give ∂nH/∂x, ∂mV/∂x, ∂°C/∂x at one adjoint solve per objective. Test: gradient check vs finite differences; N7 cell quality with/without gradients.
- **SIM21** Via-constant audit (sharp, S): Johnson's 5.08h[ln(4h/d)+1] gives 2–6× the Rosa/Goldfarb-Pucel partial inductance [C] and is used in our loop estimator ([checkers-power](../../topics/rules/checkers-power.md)); replace with loop models including return via or plane (a lone via's partial L is not a loop quantity). Test: PEEC of via pairs and via-to-plane loops vs each formula on 20 geometries.

## D. Incremental extraction and caching

- **SIM22** Canonical-geometry memoization (sharp, S): normalize each object (translate, rotate, mirror, sort terminals), hash with stackup id and band; reuse across candidates, revisions, boards (hot loops, decap mounts, launches repeat heavily). Test: cache hit rate in a placement run and over a 50-board corpus.
- **SIM23** Dirty-region invalidation from IPC commits (sharp, S): invalidate pairwise interactions within a cutoff (~10h over planes, mutual inductance falls as (2h/D)²) of changed items; recompute only touching budgets. Test: agreement and speedup, incremental vs full, over a 1,000-edit session.
- **SIM24** Adjoint price maps for copper edits (combo, S): one adjoint per objective gives first-order cost of adding/removing copper anywhere (IR loss, plane inductance, thermal resistance), rendered as a map for router, placer, LLM. Right only for compact holes; cuts need the edit's polarization tensor, finite edits exact Woodbury updates ([solver-ladder](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md#6-caching-and-incremental-re-extraction)) [C]. Test: rank 100 random edits by price vs exact re-solve.
- **SIM25** Stream-function (Δψ) screen for cuts (new, S): a line cut across the current is a topological change; first-order adjoint underestimated a 6 mm slot 30× ([solver-ladder](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md#6-caching-and-incremental-re-extraction)) [C]; current crossing the cut, Δψ along it, predicts the harm ([xd_sheet.py](../../../tools/research/physics/xd_sheet.py)). Run before accepting slots, splits, antipad rows. Test: Δψ vs exact ΔR for 100 slots.
- **SIM26** Schur-complement window macro-models (combo, M): condense everything outside an edit window onto its boundary once; later edits solve window + dense boundary block. 2-D sheets: plain refactoring already fast, faster than stale-LU-preconditioned CG in scipy ([solver-ladder](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md#6-caching-and-incremental-re-extraction)) [C]; payoff in micro-placers with thousands of moves in one window, and in 3-D. Test: speedup for 1,000 moves in a 5×5 mm window of a 4-layer PDN model.
- **SIM27** Mesh as a fidelity knob, with continuation (combo, S): coarse grids first (5 cells/h: 20–64 ms, about −2% Z0 [C]), refine as candidates converge; discretization error by Richardson extrapolation from two grids. Test: final quality vs fine-grid-only at 10× less compute.

## E. Full-wave (F3) workflow

- **SIM28** Physics component library (new, M): canonical 3-D structures (via transitions per stackup, connector launches, decap mounts, BGA breakouts, test-point stubs) solved once at F3 over sweeps, stored as parameterized surrogates or S-parameter tables with provenance; F3 cost per stackup, not per board. Test: library vs fresh F3 slice for 20 instances.
- **SIM29** Slice-and-stitch with overlap verification (combo, M): F3 slices with overlapping boundaries (si-wrapper style), S-parameters cascaded in scikit-rf; overlap computed in both slices estimates slicing error. Test: stitched vs single-domain F3 on 5 nets.
- **SIM30** Batched small-domain FDTD on GPU (new, M): pack hundreds of small candidate geometries (via, launch variants) into one batched kernel: JAX vmap/fdtdx now, openEMS GPU engine later. Test: candidates per GPU-hour vs sequential openEMS.
- **SIM31** Early stopping on decision confidence (new, S): stop FDTD once S-parameter uncertainty (energy decay or harmonic-inversion extrapolation) can no longer flip pass/fail; much F3 time is late-time ringing [I]. Test: decision agreement with full runs; time saved.
- **SIM32** Multi-fidelity Bayesian optimization for 3-D transitions (combo, M): co-kriging over F1/F2/F3 (antipad radius, launch pad, stitching-via positions), acquisition = information per CPU-second. Test: F3 calls to target return loss vs F3-only BO.
- **SIM33** Passive macromodels for SPICE (combo, M): F2 extractions (PEEC networks, plane pairs) → small passive subcircuits by PRIMA/Loewner or vector fitting + passivity enforcement, cached per block for C2 transients. Our test [C]: damped PDN fit passively with 6–9 complex pole pairs in 2–7 s (rms 2×10⁻³ to 1×10⁻⁴); an undamped cavity did not, enforcement failed: order selection and passivity must be gates. Test: passivity rate and transient error vs full network.

## F. Surrogates and learning

- **SIM34** Residual surrogates on physics baselines (sharp, S): learn log(F2/F1), not F2. 300 PEEC labels: residual on MS2 median 3.5% (p95 9.8%) vs 28% (p95 100%) on raw features [C]. Test: data-efficiency curves for loop L, crosstalk, mounting L.
- **SIM35** Label factories with coverage audits (combo, M): procedural hot loops, decap mounts, coupled sections, plane fills labelled by F2 (labels per core-hour: [solver-ladder](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md#7-spending-near-infinite-compute)) [C]; coverage vs open-board-corpus feature distributions. Test: out-of-domain rate of real-board objects vs training hull.
- **SIM36** Active learning at decision boundaries (combo, M): F3 labels where surrogate σ × |∂decision/∂value| is largest (near budgets and ties). Test: labels to target decision accuracy vs random.
- **SIM37** Graph surrogate on the physics graph (combo, L): GNN over physics-IR entities (segments, vias, pads, plane patches) predicts per-net loop L, coupling, IR drop; post-layout counterpart of ParaGraph (pre-layout, R² 0.772 [S]). Test: accuracy vs F2 on held-out boards; latency per board.
- **SIM38** Neural operator for board-scale fields (combo, L): FNO or U-Net maps layer rasters (copper, sources, sinks, h-map) to IR-drop, temperature, plane-voltage fields in ms on GPU, trained on F2; only inside its trust region with F2 spot checks. Test: field error and decision accuracy vs F2 on 100 boards.

## G. Spending compute

- **SIM39** Candidate-batched evaluation by default (sharp, S): evaluate populations (batched PEEC1 and GPU rates: [solver-ladder](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md#7-spending-near-infinite-compute)); enables CMA-ES, quality-diversity, beam search. Test: quality vs wall-clock, population vs sequential, on N7 cells.
- **SIM40** Compute market priced by expected value (combo, M): critics bid for F2/F3 time with (expected decision change × consequence)/cost; scheduler allocates the board budget; extends the N1 resource market and I5. Test: escaped physics failures per CPU-hour vs uniform.
- **SIM41** Speculative pre-evaluation (new, S): while LLM deliberates or human reviews, run F2/F3 on likely next candidates (top proposals, Pareto neighbours). Test: median verdict wait in agent sessions.
- **SIM42** Tolerance sweeps on the cheap rungs (combo, S): Monte Carlo over fab tolerances (εr, h, etch, plating, mask) and part spreads with F1/S, worst corners to F2/F3. Test: worst cases found vs F2 everywhere.
- **SIM47** GPU-resident multigrid sheet solver (new, M): layer rasters on GPU, geometric multigrid for IR-drop, thermal, plane spreading, warm-started from the previous candidate. SuperLU on one core 6–9 s at 4.4×10⁵ cells [C]; GPU V-cycle there should take milliseconds [I], putting whole-board F2 in the placer's outer loop. Test: time and agreement vs SuperLU on 20 pours and 2-layer thermal cases.

## H. Interfaces and benchmarks

- **SIM43** Open physics-evaluator benchmark (new, M): public canonical PCB structures with FastHenry-class, F3 and measured ground truth; scores accuracy, decision quality (SIM7), cost, as pattern libraries behind CNN-Cap do in IC extraction; nothing comparable for PCBs [I]. Test: ranking reproducibility across tools and machines.
- **SIM44** The LLM sees sensitivities, not fields (sharp, S): LLM gets ledger deltas and top-k sensitivities (∂budget/∂object, with units) from adjoints or finite differences, asks "what if" by naming objects. Test: LLM edit quality with/without sensitivity tables.
- **SIM45** Fidelity as a priced agent action (new, M): LLM or RL agent chooses when to call F2/F3 and pays compute in its reward, learning escalation policies. Test: cost and escaped failures vs the SIM10 rule.
