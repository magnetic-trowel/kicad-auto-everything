# Physics-first ideas: joint placement and routing (JPR)

Scope: 51 ideas for placing and routing simultaneously: topology-carrying state, coupling, search, physics objective, constraint prices, compute, evaluation. Not here: evidence and recommended architecture -> [joint-place-route](../../topics/physics/joint-place-route.md); proof of concept (PoC) -> [joint-place-route-experiment](../../topics/physics/joint-place-route-experiment.md).
Keys: JPR1–JPR51, joint place and route, band tension, rope and pulley, homotopy, kinetic certificates, topological event, PathFinder, occupancy market, channel-width legalizer, congestion prices, augmented Lagrangian, topological temperature, LNS, CP-SAT, MaxSAT, delayed acceptance, portfolio protocol, constraint price tags, three-timescale controller

Line format: `**id** name (tag, effort): mechanism; why it beats staged place-then-route; Test.` Tags (new, combo, sharp) and effort (S, M, L) -> [README](README.md). Cited original ideas P1, P7, S1, S6, R1, R2, I1, I4 -> [original catalog](../original/README.md).

Top 5 to try first: JPR14, JPR1, JPR41, JPR46, JPR34 (with JPR33).

## A. Joint state and representation

- **JPR1** Band-tension placement forces, "rope and pulley" (new, M): each net is a taut band in its homotopy class; ∂length/∂endpoint = unit tangent of its first segment; a part a band wraps feels the vector sum of the two tensions. Per-part sums replace HPWL springs in the analytical placer, so detours HPWL misses are seen. Test: rank correlation of band length vs HPWL against final routed length on 20 boards; placement quality at equal CPU.
- **JPR2** Kinetic topology certificates as trust region (new, L): constrained Delaunay triangulation + band homotopies as a kinetic data structure (Basch–Guibas–Hershberger); clip each continuous step at the first certificate failure. Inside, band linearization is exact; at the boundary a discrete topological event occurs. Test: steps and wall time to converge vs fixed step sizes.
- **JPR3** Topological event menu (new, M): when a moving pin is about to cross a band, score on joint J: band slips over pin (homotopy class changes by one generator), band shoved ahead, or move blocked; staged flows only rip up afterwards. Test: rip-ups avoided and final J vs rip-up-and-reroute on the same move sequence.
- **JPR4** Homotopy flips as SA moves (combo, M): moves "flip net n around obstacle o", "swap order of two bands in a channel", compound part move + flip of disturbed bands; topology is no longer frozen by the router's first choice. Test: joint SA with/without topology moves at equal CPU.
- **JPR5** Layer assignment as fast inner solve (sharp, M): after each topology change, min-cut on the crossing graph of the affected region (via minimization), Dayan–Dai "defer decisions"; placement sees via counts. Test: via count and runtime vs fixed preferred-direction layers.
- **JPR6** Incremental route repair with LPA*/D* Lite plus periodic renegotiation (sharp, M): update changed graph costs, repair paths with lifelong A*; every few hundred accepted moves renegotiate all routes PathFinder-style. PoC: incremental routes drifted into 2–10 failed nets at 50 parts; a fresh re-route was clean. Test: nodes expanded per move and drift vs rip-up-and-A* in the PoC script.
- **JPR7** Lock topology, not copper (new, S): freeze a critical net's homotopy class and layer plan, keep geometry elastic so part moves re-tighten it. Test: re-place iterations and critical-net length drift, copper-locked vs topology-locked.
- **JPR8** Unified occupancy market (new, M): courtyards, keep-outs, tracks, vias, pours on one per-layer resource map with one PathFinder pricing rule; parts demand area, nets demand tracks, both pay present + history congestion. Test: completion and J vs staged on dense 2-layer boards.
- **JPR9** Fanout-carrying macros (sharp, S): fine-pitch IC + escape vias + stubs as one rigid macro, ports at escape endpoints; moves drag the escape (as experts plan BGA fanout before placement). Test: joint SA move throughput and completion with/without macros on BGA boards.
- **JPR10** Planar-embedding seeds (new, M): seed placement from a crossing-minimized planarization of the netlist graph (each crossing ≥ 1 via pair on two layers). Test: vias and bottom-layer length after routing, seeded vs HPWL-seeded, 2-layer boards.

## B. Coupling, convergence and continuation

- **JPR11** Congestion prices as pressure forces (sharp, M): PathFinder history costs on cuts → pressure on bounding courtyards ∝ price × overflow, fed to the placer; prices by damped dual ascent (ADMM-style). Replaces RUDY; sharpens P1 (prices, not one-off probes). Test: price variance (oscillation) and final completion vs P1 probe forces.
- **JPR12** Augmented Lagrangian on cut capacities (sharp, M): each SURF cut flow ≤ capacity with multiplier + quadratic penalty; multipliers are channel prices the LLM reads ("the channel between U3 and J1 is worth 4 mm per track"). Test: convergence on 20 boards; multipliers vs leave-one-out channel widening.
- **JPR13** Topological temperature (new, S): separate annealing temperature for homotopy changes: early free jumps over pins, mid-run hysteresis penalty, end frozen (geometry only). Test: final J and topology-change rate across schedules.
- **JPR14** Channel-width constraint-graph legalizer (new, M): min gap between two parts = Σ(width + spacing) of bands passing between them, per layer; CP-SAT or LP compaction makes exactly the room routes need. Staged legalizers make room for nobody, why routers fail at 85% density. Test: completion and area utilization vs min-displacement legalizer on the same global placements.
- **JPR15** Route on a compact placement, then make room (combo, M): route topologically on an overlapping compact placement, then legalize with JPR14 (analog "route then migrate", Chi et al., DATE'21; TopoR "route everything while violating, then optimize"). Test: vs place-then-route on boards above 70% utilization.
- **JPR16** Multigrid continuation (sharp, M): 2 mm global-routing cells → 0.5 mm grid → gridless, warm-starting each level. Test: J vs CPU, single-level vs multigrid joint SA.
- **JPR17** Explicit continuous-to-discrete switch rule (sharp, S): leave the elastic phase when density overflow < 10% (ePlace stop rule) and < 1 topological event per 100 steps; then exact legalization and discrete LNS. Test: final J sensitivity to both thresholds.
- **JPR18** Topology-stability convergence diagnostic (new, S): fraction of nets whose homotopy class or layer plan changed since the last outer iteration; stop or escalate (larger LNS windows) on a plateau above threshold. Test: correlation with remaining J improvement.

## C. Search operators

- **JPR19** Joint LNS window, destroy parts and copper together (combo, M): remove a window's movable parts and all its tracks/vias; repair with a few CP-SAT candidate poses per part, each routed by windowed A*, accept on joint J. Test: J improvement per CPU second vs single-part SA moves.
- **JPR20** Exact joint micro-solve for critical cells (new, M): ≤ 8 parts (buck stage, crystal, RF match) in one CP-SAT model on a 0.25 mm grid, nets as flows, routed loop area as objective; result becomes a hard macro with provable joint optimum. Test: routed loop area vs staged and vs expert layouts.
- **JPR21** Joint MaxSAT for escape plus decoupling (combo, M): decap positions, their via sites and IC escape in one MaxSAT/CP-SAT model (decap vias compete with escape vias for sites). Test: decap mounting inductance and escape completion vs decaps-first.
- **JPR22** Pin and gate swaps as joint moves (sharp, S): equivalent-pin swaps (FPGA banks, MCU pin-mux, resistor-pack gates) change topology without moving parts; incremental reroute. Test: crossings and vias with/without swaps on MCU boards.
- **JPR23** Side flips with real via cost (sharp, S): evaluate bottom-side flips on the joint state (through vias, changed loop areas, thermal path) instead of a fixed second-side penalty. Test: side choices vs P4 graph cut on double-sided boards.
- **JPR24** Drag moves with copper (new, S): move a part with its private stubs, vias and band segments; cuts reroute work for passives. Test: accepted moves per second in the PoC script.
- **JPR25** MCTS over coarse joint decisions with elastic rollouts (combo, M): tree branches on block regions, sides, big-part orientations, critical homotopy picks; rollout = short elastic joint phase → J; deterministic evaluation, away from DeepPCB SMC patent claims. Test: best J at equal CPU vs random restarts of the same rollout.

## D. Physics as the shared objective

- **JPR26** Routed hot-loop inductance, not the pin triangle (sharp, S): score routed bands + return with MS2 or height × squares Σ μ0·h·dl/w near a reference plane; plan-view area + vertical extent only on planeless/far-plane boards ([solver-ladder](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md)). PoC: straight-line proxy ranks routed loop poorly (Spearman 0.23–0.79) once detours exist. Test: proxy vs routed rank correlation against PEEC on final boards.
- **JPR27** Plane-cut length as a joint term (new, S): charge every mm of copper on a should-be-solid reference layer and every slot it leaves, in budget units. Test: plane-cut length and return-path checker findings, staged vs joint.
- **JPR28** Co-routed return bands (combo, M): route each critical signal with an explicit return band (plane region or GND trace); loop area becomes a property of the pair; extends R2. Test: loop area and plane splits on mixed-signal boards.
- **JPR29** Budget-normalized objective with soft barrier (sharp, S): J = Σ w·φ(spend/budget) from the I1 ledger, φ flat below ~70% of budget, steep toward 100%; nH, mV, °C in one dimensionless currency. Test: budget violations and Pareto spread vs weighted sum of raw units.
- **JPR30** Admissible physics lower bounds for pruning (new, M): per fidelity a bound ≤ realized value (straight-line loop area ≤ routed; cut overflow ≤ routed overflow); prune moves whose bound is worse than incumbent, as A* does. Test: fraction pruned, no false prunes on logged runs.
- **JPR31** Local Pareto LNS for thermal vs loop conflicts (combo, S): two-objective LNS where spreading and loop compactness conflict (two FETs, one heatsink); front handed to LLM/user priced in nH per °C. Test: chosen point beats both single-objective solutions on the budget ledger.
- **JPR32** Coupling-aware homotopy choice (sharp, M): score victim homotopies by parallel-run length next to aggressor bands in shared channels (rule-pack coupling formula). Test: crosstalk checker findings vs spacing-rules-only router.

## E. Boundary conditions and constraint prices

- **JPR33** Board size as a priced variable (new, S): outline scale or each free edge continuous, fab cost per cm², report ∂J/∂edge. Test: area vs J curve against expert board sizes.
- **JPR34** Joint constraint price tags (sharp, M): multiplier of every fixed constraint (connector pose, mounting-hole keep-out, height zone), top k confirmed by leave-one-out joint re-solves; includes routed and physics consequences ("moving J1 4 mm left saves 3 vias and 0.6 nH"). Sharpens I4. Test: multipliers vs re-solves; designer usefulness ratings.
- **JPR35** Mechanical sensitivity map for the ME (new, M): background jobs perturb each mechanical anchor ±δ in x, y, angle and re-run a short joint optimization ("USB-C 3 mm toward the MCU: −2 vias, −0.4 nH"); quantifies the S1 mechanics loop-back. Test: any anchor move revealing a gain > 5% of J on real boards.
- **JPR36** Connectors, antennas and holes as ports (sharp, S): connectors/antenna feeds as fixed terminals injecting and returning current, mounting holes as chassis-return ports; physical boundary conditions for PDN, return-path, EMI terms. Test: return-path findings near connectors, joint vs staged.
- **JPR37** Admissible-pose sampling (sharp, S): height zones, side bans, antenna keep-out volumes, screw-head keep-outs as per-part pose masks; propose only admissible poses. Test: accepted-move rate, masks vs penalties.

## F. Compute and parallelism

- **JPR38** Speculative parallel joint moves (combo, M): evaluate many moves on all cores, commit a maximal subset with disjoint affected nets/windows (graph colouring), repeat. Test: moves/s and final J vs a single chain at equal wall time.
- **JPR39** Island model with block crossover (new, L): islands from different floorplans; crossover swaps whole blocks (parts + internal copper) when corridor ports match. Test: best J at equal CPU vs independent restarts.
- **JPR40** Shared-incumbent portfolio (combo, M): joint SA, joint LNS, price-coupled analytical placement, MCTS outer loop concurrently sharing best solution and congestion prices (as CP-SAT workers share bounds). Test: vs best single method at equal CPU.
- **JPR41** Delayed-acceptance joint SA (sharp, S): cheap first Metropolis test on HPWL, crossings, loop proxies; route only passing moves; second test on routed J corrects the first (Christen–Fox), so the chain targets routed J. PoC: ~25× per-move cost gap HPWL vs routed moves (Python, 30–50 parts; [experiment](../../topics/physics/joint-place-route-experiment.md#results-c)); most routed moves are rejected anyway. Test: J vs CPU vs plain joint SA.
- **JPR42** Online per-board surrogate for move screening (combo, S): linear model of ΔJ on ΔHPWL, Δcrossings, Δband length, Δloop proxy fitted to the board's own exact evaluations, first stage of JPR41; never pretrained (clear of learned-routability patents). Test: screening precision and recall over a run.
- **JPR43** Bandit over move types and regions (sharp, S): learn online which move types (shift, swap, homotopy flip, LNS window) and regions yield accepted improvements. Test: J vs CPU with/without bandit.
- **JPR44** GPU batched window routing (new, L): thousands of small-window shortest-path relaxations as tensor wavefronts; only once the CPU version bottlenecks on boards > 300 parts. Test: evaluations/s/W vs Rust A* on CPU.
- **JPR45** Successive halving over joint fidelity (sharp, S): seeds topology-only, survivors grid routing + joint refinement, finalists Freerouting + `kicad-cli` DRC + physics critics; extends S6 to joint candidates. Test: best final score at equal CPU vs route-all-at-low-budget.

## G. LLM role, explanation and evaluation

- **JPR46** Joint-versus-portfolio protocol (sharp, S): every joint claim must beat an equal-CPU portfolio of sequential runs (new-seed restarts, each routed and scored) and a longer single sequential run, same referee router; the PoC shows the portfolio is the control that matters. Test: adopt in the bench harness as required baseline.
- **JPR47** Planted-optimum joint instances (combo, M): draw a planar, physically good routed layout first, derive netlist, perturb placement: known optimum, measurable gap. Test: gap distribution for staged, joint, portfolio.
- **JPR48** LLM proposes topology moves from a bounded menu (sharp, S): outer-loop picks (swap two blocks' corridors, flip a block to bottom, reroute a bundle around U4's other side); inner loop evaluates; LLM never writes coordinates (relations beat coordinates). Test: J and tokens per board vs no LLM moves.
- **JPR49** Event narration from the joint state (new, S): one line per topological event or newly binding constraint ("C12 moved under U3's escape, so the USB pair now goes around the left side: +1.8 mm, +0 vias"). Test: designer ratings vs plain DRC reports.
- **JPR50** Three-timescale controller (combo, L): outer discrete loop (minutes: regions, sides, orientations, critical homotopies), middle elastic loop (seconds: band tension + congestion prices), inner incremental loop (milliseconds: re-tightening, local reroutes, LNS windows); hand-offs by JPR13, JPR17, JPR18. The recommended architecture in [joint-place-route](../../topics/physics/joint-place-route.md). Test: its phased plan, each phase with a kill criterion.
- **JPR51** Restart-or-refine controller (new, S): per budget step choose cheap restart vs joint refinement by online-estimated expected ΔJ per CPU second (two-armed bandit). PoC: restarts won from bad basins, refinement otherwise (50 parts: 2/5 each way). Test: J vs CPU vs fixed splits.
