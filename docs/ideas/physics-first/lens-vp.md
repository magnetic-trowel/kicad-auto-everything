## A. Floors and certificates

- **VP1** Beckmann floor oracle for every power net (*sharp (OP6, L2), S*; V1). For copper of area A: R ≥ R_s·(Σ_k I_k·d_k)²/(A·I²), d_k = geodesic source–load distance; Cauchy–Schwarz on Thomson's functional, any shape (not only trees), microseconds per net, differentiable in pad positions. Beats DR18 (micro-solver per structure) and JPR30 (geometric proxies); test-pour values: [D1](../../topics/physics/lens-vp.md#3-derivations) [C]. Test: floor below realized R on 200 synthesized and human pours; τ-ordering from it vs expert criticality.
- **VP2** Convex-relaxation floor with a dual certificate (*new, S*; V1, V2, BL-A). Solve variable-thickness relaxation (SIMP p = 1, no filter, convex); Lagrangian dual lower bound for any trial potential v: R ≥ (fᵀv)²/(I²·max_ρ vᵀK(ρ)v), max = fractional knapsack → interval containing the best layout of that area. 0.1 mm raster: gap 4×10⁻⁴ after 400 solves ([D1](../../topics/physics/lens-vp.md#3-derivations)) [C]. Certificate holds on that raster only; real layouts need a refinement study. Test: 20 board pours, every manufacturable design above floor, report gap distribution.
- **VP3** Avoidable-loss report (*new, S*; V1). Per pour or trace tree report (R − R_floor)/R and area elasticity −dlnR/dlnA (= 1 exactly at the Beckmann floor; lower = spreading-limited copper). Test pour: SIMP synthesis (OP2) recovers nearly all the human tree's avoidable loss, on the 0.1 mm raster only ([D1](../../topics/physics/lens-vp.md#3-derivations), [grid dependence](../../topics/physics/verification-and-limits.md#1-claims-verified-by-an-independent-method)) [C]. Below a few percent, reshaping is not worth review cost. Test: high-current nets of 30 open boards; do the largest-avoidable-loss boards match later designer rework (git history)?
- **VP4** Convex-first continuation for every compliance objective (*sharp (OP43, OP2), S*; V1, V3, BL-A). Start topology optimization at the convex p = 1 optimum, ramp p 1 → 3, sharpen projection; for loss, pour L over mapped h_eff, thermal compliance and weighted sums. Inductance-only pour over slotted reference: convex start beats uniform start on L and R at areas within 1.2% ([D1](../../topics/physics/lens-vp.md#3-derivations)) [C]. Replaces multi-start for class-A terms. Test: 20 multi-objective pour problems, final objective and CPU vs cold multi-start.
- **VP5** Multi-net floor and area budget by transport work (*sharp (OP6), S*; V1). Nets sharing a layer: P_total ≥ R_s·(Σ_n W1,n)²/A_total, W1,n = Σ I·d of net n; loss-optimal area share ∝ W1,n, pre-routing, with certificate (OP6 did this for trees only). Test: vs joint multi-rail topology optimization (OP4) on 10 shared layers; split within 10% of the optimum's.
- **VP6** Transport-work placement term for power nets (*new, S–M*; V1, envelope theorem). Add Σ_n R_s·W1,n²/A_n to the analytical placer (W1,n = current-weighted geodesic star distance); gradient on load pad k = 2·(P_floor/W1)·I_k along the unit vector away from the source. Closed-form, admissible, differentiable; HPWL treats 10 A and 1 mA alike, pour forces (OP27) need a sheet solve. Test: 10 power-heavy boards, post-route loss and worst IR drop vs HPWL placement at equal CPU.
- **VP7** Certified primal–dual evaluators (*new, M*; V1, V2 Prager–Synge). Each class-A sheet evaluation solves potential (or temperature) and its dual (stream function or equilibrated flux) on one mesh → [lower, upper], not value ± σ (SIM1's σ is statistical and can be wrong). Corner bracket check: [D2](../../topics/physics/lens-vp.md#3-derivations) [C]. Near-ties settled when brackets separate, without escalation (SIM10). Test: coverage on 100 structures vs fine solves (100% by construction), cost vs one solve, fraction of optimizer comparisons settled by brackets.
- **VP8** Bracketed C and L extraction (*new, M*; V3, V5). Capacitance: Galerkin charge MoM = lower bound (V5, Thomson form), potential FEM = upper. HF loop L: restricted-current PEEC = upper (V3), divergence-free trial B = lower. Report the bracket as the extraction's error; settles e.g. the two heatsink-C estimates 22 vs 26 pF. Test: 20 structures, brackets contain FasterCap and openEMS, width vs mesh density.
- **VP9** Infeasibility certificates from floors (*sharp (DR41), S*; V1–V6, V10). Certified floor > budget (τ < 1) ⇒ budget provably infeasible for these terminals, stackup and area; return the binding term (distance, area, h, via-set capacity). Test: certificates without optimizing on 20 infeasible budget sets, zero false certificates on 20 feasible sets.
- **VP10** Floor-gated stop rule (*sharp (OP57), S*; V1, V2). Stop refining a class-A term when (current − floor)/floor < max(budget slack, model error); OP57's marginal-improvement rule cannot tell plateau from optimum. Test: wall-clock to a budget-feasible design and wasted iterations vs OP57 and fixed iteration counts.

## B. Vias, perforation and cavities

- **VP11** Capacity model and hull floor for via sets (*sharp (PW32, N8), S*; V10). Spreading L into a plane pair = (μ0·d/2π)·ln(R/cap); closed forms for one, two and ring vias, charge simulation otherwise; cap ≤ cap(convex hull). N8 maximizes via-set capacity in the footprint; vias inside the hull flagged low-value. Reproduces PW32 grid solves within 2%; hull floor example in [D3](../../topics/physics/lens-vp.md#3-derivations) [C]. Test: vs PEEC on 50 decap via patterns; N8 decisions with/without the capacity objective.
- **VP12** Hexagonal (staggered) via fields (*new, S*; V10). Thermal, stitching and power via arrays (not BGA fields): hexagonal lattice stays near the Hashin–Shtrikman floor at equal density and antipad where a square lattice does not ([D3](../../topics/physics/lens-vp.md#3-derivations)) [C]. Larger nearest-neighbour spacing also eases drill-to-drill and CAF rules. Replaces grids chosen by CAD convenience. Test: unit-cell and board solves plus fab DRC on 10 thermal pads and power transitions.
- **VP13** Perforation floor critic (*new, S*; V10). Per perforated region report sheet-R multiplier vs floors 1/(1−f) (any pattern) and (1+f)/(1−f) (isotropic). At floor: improve only by fewer/smaller antipads; above: arrangement-limited → stagger or merge antipads along flow (PW12 finds necks but not whether rearranging helps). Test: 10 BGA and connector fields, predicted rearrangement gain vs re-solved fields.
- **VP14** Two-sided cavity-mode bracket for stitching (*sharp (FE26, FE27), S*; V6, V10). Upper bound (domain monotonicity): largest empty circle, f1 ≤ 0.766·c/(D·√εr), D to via edges, open board edges mirrored. Lower estimate (not rigorous): homogenized via-lattice capacity (wire-medium form), within 3–16% of eigen-solve on grids. Decide without eigen-solve when the bracket clears the protected band; upper bound alone can prove a pattern insufficient. FE26's ±30% estimate is really one-sided ([derivation map](../../topics/physics/lens-vp.md#3-derivations)). Test: containment on the 11 FE26 patterns + 50 random; count decisions settled.

## C. Dynamics, spectra and damping

- **VP15** Tuned magnetically coupled damping layer (*combo, M*; V4, V9). Resistive film or resistor-closed copper ring between hot loop and plane, R_s ≈ ω_r·μ0·ℓ_eff/2 (ring: R ≈ ω_r·L_ring) so its eddy mode relaxes at the ring frequency; loss peaks when a coupled mode's relaxation rate = ω, limit Q ≥ 2/k² − 1; layer is a stackup variable. Copper alone leaves Q = h/δ = 30–170 ([lens-th](lens-th.md)); 2-D film ≈ 0.1 Ω/sq gave Q_film ≈ 7.6 at 200 MHz; L′ 0.106 nH/mm with film, 0.124 without, 0.070 with solid plane at film depth [C]. Prior art Hartmann, Müsing and Kolar (2009) [S]. Complements TH3. Test: 3-D PEEC or openEMS of a GaN half-bridge loop ± layer, then double-pulse ringing and near-field on a coupon.
- **VP16** Eddy-mode Foster macromodels for plane returns (*combo (SIM33), M*; V4). Generalized eigenproblem R·v = λ·L·v on the plane's zero-net-current subspace; each mode = one RL section of a passive Foster ladder; keep modes by share c_k² of the inductance drop, ~3 per decade. Passive by construction (vector fitting's passivity enforcement fails on an undamped cavity, SIM33); reproduced the direct solve exactly [C]. The fast solver of research-gaps gap 2, for kHz-band precision analog. Test: vs full PEEC 10 Hz–10 MHz on 5 layouts, SPICE runtime, passivity checks.
- **VP17** Containment-distance regime predicate (*sharp (RG19, SY25), S*; V3, V4). Aggressor return passes under a victim at distance D only below f ≈ R_s/(2π·μ0·D). Per-pair predicate gating star-ground, separation and split rules, not one f_x = R_s/(2π·μ0·h) per stackup (different question, D5 in [topics lens-vp](../../topics/physics/lens-vp.md#3-derivations)). Containment law checked for h = 0.1–1.6 mm, ℓ ≥ 6h (D5) [C]. Complements SY25's ℓ_d map. Test: vs 2-D/3-D PEEC containment on 30 pairs; rule-gating accuracy vs expert verdicts.
- **VP18** Hidden-mode inventory, the adiabatic-elimination test (*new, M*; V4, V6, V9). List every hidden coordinate the layout adds to the circuit Lagrangian (eddy relaxation rates, cavity eigenfrequencies, CM and ground-loop resonances, ring modes) with rate/frequency, Q, participation. Layout is visible exactly where a mode lies in the circuit band with Q above budget; one test replacing separate critics. Test: 5 boards with known SI/EMI/analog faults; culprit mode listed first or second?
- **VP19** Equipartition diagnostic for power copper (*sharp (PW2), S*; V3, V5, V8). Per switching piece W_m/W_e = (½LI²)/(½CV²) at its own I, V: ≫ 1 too narrow, ≪ 1 too wide, Z0 = V/I matched; energy floor V·I·l/v shows copper vs parts dominance ([D6](../../topics/physics/lens-vp.md#3-derivations)) [C]. PW2 gives the width; this diagnoses existing copper. Test: 20 vendor EVMs, SW-copper ratios vs designer choices and EMI results.

## D. Bilevel structure, gradients and monotonicity

- **VP20** A/A′/B/C tagging of every objective term (*new, S*; BL). A (self-energy copper lowers: R, L, thermal resistance, 1/C): convex relaxed, free gradient, monotone. A′ (self-energy copper raises, e.g. dv/dt node C): monotone, free gradient, concave relaxed, floor = mandatory copper alone. B (interaction or misaligned): one adjoint, non-convex. C (non-variational): worst-frequency sensitivity. Schedule: A jointly and convexly first, continue into B, C at binding frequencies; avoids local optima that mixing all terms or ad hoc continuation (OP43) meets. Test: joint runs ± schedule on 10 boards: budget spend, runtime, local-optimum rate.
- **VP21** Maxwell-stress placement forces for all self-energy terms (*sharp (OP27), M*; V1, V3, V5). Envelope theorem: gradient of a class-A energy w.r.t. pad or part pose = boundary integral of energy density at fixed fields (conduction, magnetic, electric, thermal) → one solve per band gives forces on all parts. OP27 covers DC loss only; class-A never needs adjoints, class-B does. Test: vs finite-difference re-solves on 50 pad moves; placement quality on power-heavy boards.
- **VP22** Transfer-term (Braess) guard for copper additions (*combo (CD24, XD122), S*; V7, D7 of [topics lens-vp](../../topics/physics/lens-vp.md#3-derivations)). Before accepting a fill, bridge or stitch, sign its effect on each protected transfer resistance or coupling by Tellegen's product −ΔV_k^a·ΔV_k^v over the new copper; block additions that raise a protected term even when they lower loss. "More copper is better" holds for self terms only (D7 bridge example) [C]. CD24 makes the same point for couplings. Test: 100 fill edits on mixed-signal boards, predicted sign vs exact re-solves.
- **VP23** Self and interaction rows in the ledger (*sharp (PW30, I1), S*; V7, V8). Self rows: additive energies (W, J per event, energy to absorb). Interaction rows: bilinear energies in the victim's units. Ledger refuses to add nH across loops with different currents; one-currency framings ("nH in watts", three-commodity market, PW30, S10) are right for self terms, wrong for victims and EMI masks. Test: two-auditor review of 10 ledgers before/after; automated unit and consistency checks.
- **VP24** Monotone-convergence regression tests for solvers (*new, S*; V1–V5). Reference-free invariants: coarse PEEC L∞ ≥ fine; potential-FEM R ≤ true ≤ stream-function-FEM R; MoM C ≤ true; RL extraction L(f) non-increasing, R(f) non-decreasing; added copper never raises R or L∞. Catch sign, assembly and unit bugs; SIM6 golden tests need references; complements SY4 (reciprocity), CD5 (Foster sums). Test: inject 20 known bugs into PEEC and sheet core, detection rate.
- **VP25** Sign-aware proxies as admissible bounds (*sharp (JPR30), S*; V1–V5). A trial-field evaluation of the true functional is one-sided (uniform-current PEEC ≥ L∞, ε·A/h ≤ C, Beckmann ≤ R); other approximations have two-sided error. Use one-sided proxies as A*-admissible bounds for move pruning (JPR30 has only geometric ones). Test: logged joint runs, false-prune rate (should be 0) and pruned fraction.

## E. Conductor energy and Poynting

- **VP26** Energy columns for the conductor passport (*combo (CD1), M*; V7, V8, V9). Per piece and band add to CD1's passport: guided Poynting flux along its gap; partial magnetic energy ½·I_k·(L_p·I)_k; partial electric energy ½·q_k·φ_k; I²R; share of the cell's radiating moment; participation in each ringing mode; plus boundary energy density (= its sensitivity). Dominant column names the piece's energetic job; energy shares add across mechanisms, current shares do not. Test: explanation quality and reviewer agreement on 10 boards ± columns.
- **VP27** Energy-containment ratio per cell (*new, M*; V8). Per cell and band, fraction of E- and H-field energy stored outside the cell volume, from one PEEC + one electrostatic solve. Unifies loop area, moment-with-image and "capacitance to whom" (energy leaving the cell); puts DR42's locality index in energy units. Test: correlation with radiated-emission proxies, DR42 and expert "looks right" ratings on 20 layouts.
- **VP28** Poynting route map for power delivery (*new, M*; V8). Render S = E × H in each dielectric gap per rail and band: where power flows regulator → load and leaks at edges, slots, splits (each rail's "duct"; a split under a pour = blocked duct). Return-current renderers (RG48, FE9) show current, not the power route. Test: reviewer fault-finding on seeded slot/split faults vs return-current views.

## F. Thermal and robustness

- **VP29** Thermal transport floor (*new, S*; V1 thermal form). Heat through copper of area A_cu (sheet conductance k·t) to a sink: Σ P_k·ΔT_k ≥ (Σ P_k·d_k)²/(k·t·A_cu) (Beckmann for heat; convex relaxation tightens it); decides before layout whether a spreader area can meet a ΔT budget. Valid when heat leaves through the designated sink (sealed, vacuum or high-altitude boards, cold-plate conduction); where convection matters add it as a distributed sink in the relaxation. Replaces "15.3 cm²/W" and fin rules (no feasibility proof). Test: vs FV thermal solves for 10 parts on spreaders; floor never above solved value.
- **VP30** Two-corner etch robustness for self terms (*sharp (OP7, TM35), S*; V1, V3, V5). Self quantities are monotone in copper: eroded design = worst R and L∞, best C; dilated = reverse → two corners bound every etch outcome for class-A terms, no Monte Carlo. Transfer terms are not monotone and still need sampling (TM35, OP7 sample three projections without knowing which terms they bound). Test: vs Monte Carlo etch on 20 structures; 100% coverage for class A, violation rate for class B.
