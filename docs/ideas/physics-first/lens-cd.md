## A. The passport and its oracles

- **CD1** Piece passport ledger (*new, M; P1–P7*). Per copper piece (mesh strip, via, pad cell) and band, one sparse row in `ledger.json`: current share per source, charge share by destination (fast nodes), leverage, exact removal value per budget, transport/circulation ratio κ, heat-flux share, job verbs; bodies (net × layer) aggregate pieces. The physics IR indexes by structure (loop, node), so nothing answers "what is this copper doing?". Test: passports for 5 open boards; every DRC-clean physics fault planted by the mutation benchmark (PC29) changes ≥ 1 verb at the planted site.
- **CD2** All-budget removal table from one diagonal (*sharp of XD51 and SIM24, S; P3*). Per band compute y_bb = (N K⁻¹ Nᵀ)_bb once; exact change of every budget on removing piece b: ΔZ_qp = s_b^p s_b^q / y_bb, all pieces and budgets at once. First-order adjoint prices are off by 1/(1 − ρ) ([D1](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md#3-four-derivations)); our board's 3 mm notch: 11× [C, [prototype](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md#4-prototype-a-buck-cell-a-sensitive-trace-and-a-slotted-plane)]. Test: brute-force agreement holds on the prototype (algebraic check only); repeat on a real 4-layer board.
- **CD3** Thevenin addition oracle for non-local copper (*new, S; P3*). Exact value of adding a via, jumper or bridge of impedance z between nodes m, n: ΔZ_qp = −ΔV_mn^p ΔV_mn^q / (z + Z_th,mn); open-circuit voltages from the existing solve, Z_th one solve per site, batched. Prices non-local additions (bridge across a slot, via 10 mm away) that topological derivatives (OP41, local, first-order) miss. Our board: best of 203 via sites ~1,000× the median; brute force agrees to 0.001 pH [C, [prototype](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md#4-prototype-a-buck-cell-a-sensitive-trace-and-a-slotted-plane)]. Test: rank stitching/bridging candidates on 3 boards, top 10 vs openEMS.
- **CD4** Leverage map per band (*new, S; P4*). ρ_b = 1 − z_b·y_bb = probability the piece lies in a random spanning tree; exact by selected inversion, board-wide by Johnson–Lindenstrauss sketch (24–96 solves). ρ = 1 series path, ρ ≈ ½ square-grid sheet, ρ → 0 redundant mesh. Geometric neck finders (XD95 medial axis, PC20 min-cut) see width, not irreplaceability. Our board: the sketch found every ρ > 0.9 piece with no false flag; on a uniform slotted sheet it over-flags [C, [prototype](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md#4-prototype-a-buck-cell-a-sensitive-trace-and-a-slotted-plane)]. Test: sketch vs exact, recall of ρ > 0.9 pieces.
- **CD5** Foster self-test on every solve (*new, S; P4*). Σ_b ρ_b = n − c (nodes minus components) at DC and in thermal networks; generalized trace = n − c with mutual inductance too. A changed sum flags an unintended island, missing via or misconnected port before any budget is trusted. Test: plant 20 connectivity faults (floating fill, cut via), count detections.
- **CD6** Leverage-gated trust for first-order prices (*sharp of SIM2 and SIM24, S; P3, P4*). 1/(1 − ρ_b) = error factor of any first-order removal estimate at DC and in thermal networks; escalate to exact rank-k when > 1.2. In inductive bands never use per-piece attribution as a removal estimate ([prototype](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md#4-prototype-a-buck-cell-a-sensitive-trace-and-a-slotted-plane), first-order vs exact). Test: prediction error gated vs ungated on 1,000 random edits.

## B. Jobs, verbs and explanations

- **CD7** Share-qualified reference (*sharp of PC9, S; P1*). A fill counts as a channel's reference in a band only where it carries that channel's return share (s above a threshold) in that band; connectivity-only qualification credits connected fill the return never reaches. Test: PC33 red-team edits; count fills passing PC9 but failing CD7.
- **CD8** Job verbs from counterfactual signs (*new, S; P7*). From exact single-piece removals per band and (aggressor, victim): *carries* p (a self budget rises without it); *lowers* p→q (coupling rises without it); *raises* p→q (coupling falls without it); *images* p (no transport current, yet removal raises a self budget); *series path* (ρ = 1); *senses* (zero foreign share). Replaces top-down role labels by net name or template. Test: experts blind-label 200 pieces on 5 boards; agreement with verbs.
- **CD9** Counterfactual coupling maps instead of spender bars (*new, S; P2, P3*). Show a coupling as exact per-piece removal change, not Tellegen attribution; spender bars (LA45) stay for self budgets. Our board: per-region attributions swing past ±100% because go and return terms cancel (CD10) [C, [prototype](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md#4-prototype-a-buck-cell-a-sensitive-trace-and-a-slotted-plane)]. Test: reviewers asked "which copper should change to cut this coupling?", accuracy with bars vs maps.
- **CD10** Cancellation index as coupling-robustness metric (*new, S; P2*). χ = |Σ_b a_b| / Σ_b |a_b| over a coupling's Tellegen terms; small χ = small residual of large cancelling go/return terms, so small geometric or etch changes swing it by multiples. Use χ to pick tolerance corners and to prefer structural fixes (remove the sharing) over tuning. Values on our board: [prototype](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md#4-prototype-a-buck-cell-a-sensitive-trace-and-a-slotted-plane) [C]. Test: Monte-Carlo etch and registration on 50 couplings; does χ predict spread?
- **CD11** Loop-basis attribution for explanations (*new, M; P2, P5*). Net go and return contributions first: express aggressor current in its mesh (loop) basis and attribute per loop; per-piece attribution is ill-conditioned and mesh-dependent for couplings. Test: attribution stability under mesh refinement, per piece vs per loop.
- **CD12** Designed/parasitic per (piece, job) (*new, S*). Move LA3's flag from structures to piece–job pairs: SW pour designed for carrying inductor current and spreading heat, parasitic as an antenna. Resolves the overlap [vocabulary](../../topics/physics/vocabulary.md) leaves open (a VIN pour is feed, hot-loop member and spreader at once). Test: re-express the 6 worked examples with per-job flags; count conflicts that disappear.
- **CD13** Four standard piece queries (*combo, S; P1–P3*). Each cites piece ids, budgets and model: *Why is this copper here?* (jobs ranked by removal value ÷ budget slack); *What breaks if I cut here?* (Woodbury + detour map); *What does this piece do at f?* (shares and removal value per band); *Where would one more via help?* (CD3 ranking). Replaces geometry dumps and DRC lists the LLM must interpret. Test: LLM repair-task success with queries vs geometry dumps (SIM44 protocol).
- **CD14** Functional segmentation of copper (*new, M; P1, P7*). Cluster pieces by passport vectors into named regions ("feed trunk", "hot-loop image", "shared ADC/load return neck") used as IR bodies and in LLM context, instead of net × layer bodies that lump different jobs. Test: reviewer agreement that regions are meaningful; token cost vs geometry.
- **CD15** Passport diff, role-change alarms (*sharp of PC41, S; P7*). After an edit list pieces whose verbs changed (plane strip became a series path, via started raising a coupling); budget diffs say something changed, not which copper changed role. Test: seeded regressions, time to root cause vs budget-level diffs.
- **CD16** Passport-labelled mutants (*combo, S; P7*). Label PC29/PC30 mutants by which pieces changed jobs and how much, scoring detection per job instead of one physics delta per mutant. Test: per-job detection rates of the critic suite.

## C. Bands, circulation and the f_x transition

- **CD17** Per-piece regime map κ(f) (*new, M; P5*). κ = |I(f)| / |I_R| per piece and source at 5–7 frequencies around f_x; choose resistive, RL or inductive model per region, not one global f_x per board. Our board: jobs change piece by piece across ~0.3–10 f_x [C, [prototype](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md#4-prototype-a-buck-cell-a-sensitive-trace-and-a-slotted-plane)]. Test: model choice by κ vs global f_x on 10 converter boards vs full RL solve.
- **CD18** Cycle-rank budgeting per band (*new, S; P4, P5*). A reference's cycle rank = room for currents to circulate. Below crossover, circulation carries foreign transport current through shared pieces and lets external flux drive loops; above it, circulation is how returns image their signals. Choose single-point, multipoint or hybrid grounding per band by computing both effects, compile to `split_band` (capacitors add loops at HF only) or beads (remove them). Replaces the "star vs plane" folklore thresholds (100 kHz to 10 MHz between sources). Test: 10 published grounding schemes, predicted vs reported outcomes.
- **CD19** Transition-band passports and coupling metrics (*sharp, S; P5, P7*). Always evaluate couplings at f_x as well as switching and edge bands, judged by |Z| (or spectrum-weighted volts), not the inductive part. Near f_x a coupling is mostly common impedance (Z_ADC←hot = 35 + j18 µΩ in our board); plane strips under the hot loop are worth +82% of that coupling at f_x vs +13% at 100 MHz [C]; judged by L alone several verbs flipped sign. Pieces strong only in the transition band are where band-selective operators (stitching capacitors, beads) beat plain copper. Test: verb tables ± f_x band on 10 mixed-signal boards; which explains experts' fixes better?
- **CD20** Induced-current inventory (*new, S; P5*). List conductors with no transport current whose induced current lowers a self budget or coupling ("incidental shields"); protect them in the IR against silent removal by DC-only trimming and later edits. Test: on 10 boards, how many DC-only pour-optimization (OP2) trims destroy an incidental shield.
- **CD21** Via passports instead of via rules (*sharp of PW32 and PW35, S; P1, P3*). Each via's share of each loop per band plus exact removal and addition values, replacing "N vias per amp" and "return via within X mm". Our board: one CIN− via carries ~9× more of the hot loop at 100 MHz than of the DC feed [C, [prototype](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md#4-prototype-a-buck-cell-a-sensitive-trace-and-a-slotted-plane)]. Test: via budgets vs rules on 10 EVM layouts.

## D. Optimization and arbitration

- **CD22** Job-conflict detector and `separate_jobs` operator (*new, M; P6, P7*). Find pieces whose job vector has large opposite-sign components (heat spreading vs charge to world; DC carry vs HF coupling path); propose a split: trim the antenna part, keep the carry part; route heat through thin dielectric to the node's own return; separate DC and HF paths. A per-net rule ("minimise SW area") cannot keep the useful half: our SW pour spreads heat at its pin end and holds the most world charge at its far end [C, [prototype](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md#4-prototype-a-buck-cell-a-sensitive-trace-and-a-slotted-plane), "one pour, three jobs"]. Test: 10 converter boards, emissions proxy and ΔT before/after.
- **CD23** Job purity as the beauty metric (*new, M; P1, P2*). Per piece: overlap of unrelated structures' shares in one band; board purity = 1 − Σ overlaps. Coupling is a bilinear form over shares, so purity is locality (DR42) per piece and band, saying which copper is impure. Test: correlation with expert "looks right" ratings and EMI proxy on 30 boards.
- **CD24** Rayleigh-safe fill and stitch policy (*sharp of XD122, S; P3*). Fill or stitch only where the addition oracle shows no coupling rises; Rayleigh protects self impedances, not couplings. Our board: some via sites raise a coupling [C, [D2](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md#3-four-derivations)]. Test: policy vs blanket fills, coupling changes on 10 boards.
- **CD25** Batch-removal guard (*sharp of PW10 and XD93, S; P3, P4*). Price deletions as a batch (Woodbury), never as a sum of single costs; stop trimming when batch cost reaches slack. Batch vs summed costs on our board: [prototype](../../topics/physics/lens-cd.md#4-prototype-a-buck-cell-a-sensitive-trace-and-a-slotted-plane) [C]. Test: trimming priced by batch vs single costs, final budget errors.
- **CD26** Passport-guided LNS destroy and repair (*combo, M; P3*). In JPR19 windows destroy the lowest exact-value-per-area pieces, repair where the addition oracle is highest, not random or geometric sets. Test: joint runs passport-guided vs random destroy at equal CPU.
- **CD27** Incremental passports with topological-event refactoring (*sharp of LA27, M; P3*). A move changes k pieces → rank-k Woodbury update (k solves); refactor when cumulative rank passes a threshold or the Woodbury core is ill-conditioned (= a near-bridge forming, copper analogue of a JPR2 kinetic-certificate failure). Test: drift and speed over 10,000 joint moves.
- **CD28** Spectral sparsifier of the copper graph (*new, M; P4*). Sample pieces with probability ∝ leverage (Spielman–Srivastava) → sparse surrogate whose quadratic forms (loss, loop impedance, ΔT) stay within 1 ± ε of the full network: a guaranteed inner-loop proxy. Test: budget errors and speed-up vs full network on 5 boards.
- **CD29** Piece value in output units (*combo, M; P3*). Multiply each piece's exact ΔZ_qp by circuit adjoint ∂output/∂Z_qp (DR3): "this strip is worth 3 µV at the ADC"; covers exact finite edits, S1 gives only gradients. Test: predicted vs re-simulated output change, 100 edits on a buck + ADC board.

## E. Manufacturing, thermal and the rest of the passport

- **CD30** Physics-weighted critical area (*combo, M; P3*). Expected physics damage from fab defects = Σ P_open(b)·removal value(b) + Σ P_short(m,n)·addition value(m,n), merging TM30's yield model with the budgets, so widening and redundant stitching go where defects hurt physics (geometric critical area treats all strips alike). Test: rank defect sites on 5 boards vs brute-force defect injection.
- **CD31** Removal-cost-aware DFM fixes (*combo, S; P3*). When DFM requires removing copper (slivers, acid traps, thieving balance), choose the removal with smallest exact physics cost. Test: physics budgets after cleanup, cost-aware vs geometric.
- **CD32** Thermal passports on the same pieces (*combo, S; P2, P3*). Per piece heat-flux shares and exact thermal removal values (rank-1 on the thermal Laplacian) joined to the electrical passport, exposing electro-thermal job conflicts piece by piece. Test: thermal removal values vs FV re-solves on 5 power boards.
- **CD33** Charge-destination passport for fast nodes (*combo of PW4, M; P6*). Per piece of a dv/dt node: exact charge split among local return, quiet nets, victims, world (δq = −P_OO⁻¹ P_Oe q_e) and exact removal value (Δq = −C_:e q_e / C_ee); finds the antenna end of a pour (PW4 splits per net). Test: world share vs near-field scan on 3 converter boards.
- **CD34** Tellegen Kelvin proof across bands (*sharp of LA8 and XD72, S; P2*). V_err = Σ_b s_b^sense v_b^load at DC and every switching band, attributed to pieces; a sense path is proven when its pieces carry no load share at DC and pick up no inductive EMF at HF (DC-only current-free checks miss HF pickup). Test: tap-error predictions vs measurements on 5 shunt layouts.
