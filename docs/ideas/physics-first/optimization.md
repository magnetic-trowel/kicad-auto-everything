## A. Copper as a field: shape synthesis

- **OP1** One sheet operator for three physics (new, S): DC IR drop, the inductive limit of a pour over a reference plane, and heat spreading are one 2-D screened-Laplace problem with sheet coefficients 1/R_s, 1/(μ0·h_eff), k·t (+ convection); one sparse solver + one adjoint, three coefficient maps. Test: reproduce padne DC and a FastHenry plane-pair inductance on 5 boards within 10%.
- **OP2** Power zone by topology optimization (combo, M): SIMP + density filter + Heaviside projection + OC/MMA update turns "pour for net X with area A between these pads" into a minimum-loss shape legalized to a KiCad zone. At equal area 14.5% less loss than a well-drawn trunk-and-branch layout ([POC](../../topics/physics/copper-shape-synthesis.md#proof-of-concept) [C]). Test: 20 high-current nets from open boards, loss and peak J vs designer copper at equal area.
- **OP3** Copper priced by routing demand (new, M): per-cell area price = router congestion price; pours yield where signals pass. POC: a 4× corridor price halved corridor copper at +24% loss [C]. Test: 2-layer boards, completion and IR drop, priced vs unpriced pours.
- **OP4** Multi-rail plane partition as multi-phase optimization (combo, L): one density field per rail on a shared layer, sum-to-one per cell, clearance term on overlap of fields dilated by half the gap; phase-field or alternating priced single-rail solves; equalizes marginal loss per mm² (replaces hand splits and the Voronoi default). Test: 10 boards with 3+ rails on one plane, IR margin and split crossings vs designer.
- **OP5** Return-aware pour (new, M): add HF loop inductance with an h_eff(x,y) map (distance to nearest continuous reference) to the DC objective, so switching current avoids slots and splits. Above f_c = R_s/(2π·μ0·h) current spreads by inductance, not resistance ([crossover table](../../topics/physics/copper-shape-synthesis.md#one-operator-three-physics) [C]). Test: POC slot case, then three deliberately slotted boards in FastHenry.
- **OP6** Area allocation between nets by marginal value (new, S): with widths ∝ current, a net's minimum loss at area A is R_s·(Σ I·l)²/A, so the optimal split gives area ∝ √(weight)·Σ I·l. Equal split of 100 mm² between nets with Σ I·l = 100 and 50 A·mm costs 11% more loss [C]. Test: vs full multi-net topology optimization on 10 synthetic layers.
- **OP7** Etch-robust copper (combo, M): optimize eroded, nominal and dilated designs together (robust formulation; Qian and Sigmund, over/under-etched MEMS), erosion from the fab's ±20% width tolerance; necks keep their resistance budget at worst etch. Test: Monte Carlo erode/dilate of legalized shapes, worst-case R vs nominal-only.
- **OP8** Teardrops and fillets sized by physics (sharp, S): shape derivative of loss at an edge ∝ (tangential current density)², highest at pad entries, inside corners, junctions; grow fillets until marginal mΩ per mm² < area price, instead of a fixed teardrop %. Test: loss and peak J vs KiCad default teardrops on 50 pad entries.
- **OP9** Thermal-relief choice per pad (sharp, S): spoke resistance ([DFM table](../../topics/physics/copper-shape-synthesis.md#manufacturing-constraints-mapped-to-pcb-dfm) [C]); choose solid/4-spoke/2-spoke per pad from current and assembly heat budget. Test: reflow simulation and resistance per choice on power pads.
- **OP10** Via-field optimization (new, M): via density on a layer-pair interface as a design field (vertical conductance per cell) optimized with the two sheets, then quantized to sites with CP-SAT; vias land where current transfers ("≥1 via per amp near the pad" only approximates). Test: IR drop and via count vs the rule on 10 multi-layer power paths.
- **OP11** Electro-thermal co-synthesis (combo, M): Joule heating coupled to the heat equation, ρ(T) at +0.39%/K, hottest copper temperature as a p-norm objective; busbar studies report > 50% copper saving under temperature limits. Test: 1 oz vs 2 oz power stages, hot-spot T from an FV thermal solver vs IPC-2152 sizing.
- **OP12** Switch-node copper minimized under a thermal bound (sharp, M): SW pour is a dv/dt antenna (C to plane and chassis) and FET heat sink; minimize area × dv/dt s.t. a junction-temperature bound from the thermal sheet solve. Test: radiated proxy and Tj on 5 converter layouts.
- **OP13** Two-layer co-design of forward and return copper (new, L): no plane, so optimize top and bottom density fields together with a partial-inductance objective (coarse-grid PEEC) so they overlap where current flows. Test: FastHenry loop L and DC loss vs designer's 2-layer power path.
- **OP14** Shield copper synthesis (new, L): optimize grounded copper + stitching vias between aggressor and victim to minimize mutual C and L via the coupling adjoint; shows when guard copper does nothing. Test: FasterCap/FastHenry coupling, synthesized vs guard trace vs nothing.
- **OP15** Antenna-aware copper exclusion (new, L): replace fixed antenna keep-out by a detuning-sensitivity map: adjoint of resonant frequency (or S11 at band centre) w.r.t. copper per cell. Test: openEMS S11 shift vs keep-out area on 3 chip-antenna reference designs.
- **OP16** Plane shapes that move cavity resonances (combo, M): plane-pair eigenvalues (or impedance peaks at the decap set) in the objective with stitching-via and outline variables; Sigrity-style tools only search decap sets. Test: cavity-model Z(f) before/after on 4-layer boards with known resonance problems.
- **OP17** Current-density cap (sharp, S): p-norm constraint on |J| (no single hot neck); cap from IPC-2152 temperature rise or electromigration in fine features. Test: peak J and local ΔT, capped vs uncapped.
- **OP18** Copper balance as a manufacturing field (combo, S): per-region copper-density bands per layer (plating uniformity, warp) as constraints in the same density framework; thieving and synthesis as one problem. Test: density histograms and warp proxy vs post-hoc thieving.
- **OP19** Shape catalogue and learned warm starts (new, M): precompute optimal shapes for canonical cases (pad-to-pad, star of k loads, bus with taps, around one hole) over current and aspect ratios; small U-Net or neural operator maps boundary conditions to a warm-start density. Test: iterations and final loss, warm vs cold.
- **OP20** Level-set polish after SIMP (combo, M): thresholded density → signed distance field → a few curvature-limited shape-derivative steps; smooth arc-friendly outlines, no grey cells. Test: loss and vertex count vs SIMP + contour.

## B. Physics terms for placement (evaluated while parts move)

- **OP21** Filament-PEEC loop term with an image plane (sharp, S): Manhattan filaments, Grover closed-form parallel-filament mutuals, mirror image for the reference plane: loop L within 1.5% of Grover's rectangle and 1–10% of microstrip L′, ~0.35 ms per loop ([kernel checks](../../topics/physics/physics-in-the-loop-optimization.md#physics-terms-model-cost-gradient-regime) [C]), est. ~1 µs per loop in a GPU batch [I]; differentiable in pad positions, replaces "loop area". Reference: PEECN solver (solved current split, FastHenry within 1.3% for 4 loops, w/h 0.3–5, 100 MHz; graded filaments needed for w/h > 10) → [open-solvers](../../topics/physics/open-solvers.md). Test: rank correlation with PEECN on 30 hot loops vs area proxy.
- **OP22** Path length × plane depth, not loop area, when a plane is close (sharp, S): with a plane 0.1 mm below equal-area loops differ 1.7× in L and L tracks perimeter (±8% once h ≤ b/4, b = forward-to-return spacing); no plane: still 18% ([loop table](../../topics/physics/physics-in-the-loop-optimization.md#physics-terms-model-cost-gradient-regime) [C]). Test: as OP21, split by h/b.
- **OP23** Thermal Green's-function placement term (sharp, S): convectively cooled board = fin, λ = √(k·t/2h) ([Green's function table](../../topics/physics/physics-in-the-loop-optimization.md#physics-terms-model-cost-gradient-regime) [C]); superpose K0(r/λ) kernels with edge images as a smooth ΔT term; spacing follows λ. Test: rank correlation with an FV thermal solver on 20 boards.
- **OP24** Noise potential instead of electrostatic density (new, M): beside ePlace's density potential, an aggressor near-field potential (each hot loop's and dv/dt node's dipole moment convolved with a coupling kernel) that victims descend; one FFT per step. Test: victim coupling (FasterCap/FastHenry) vs keep-out rules on mixed-signal boards.
- **OP25** Price of a millimetre (combo, S): per part ∂(weighted budget spend)/∂(x, y, θ) from the kernels in nH/mm, K/mm, mV/mm; legalizer picks which part yields, LLM sees physically pinned parts. Test: legalization displacement cost in physics units vs plain displacement.
- **OP26** Orientation by current direction (combo, S): Gumbel-softmax orientation (as in Cypress) with the loop-L term, putting current-carrying pins on the loop's closing side. Test: hot-loop nH vs netlist-only orientation.
- **OP27** Pad forces from pour solves (new, M): ∂(pour loss)/∂(pad position) = boundary integral of energy density around the pad; coarse (0.5 mm) pour solve every k steps applied as a force, loads drift toward source along cheap copper. Test: IR drop after routing with/without pour forces on power-heavy boards.
- **OP28** Batched physics screening of placement candidates (combo, M): closed-form kernels (loop L, ΔT, coupling) for 10⁵–10⁶ candidate poses/s on a GPU reject bad placements before routing. Test: fraction of finalists failing full-solver budgets with/without screening.

## C. Routing as current flow

- **OP29** Gilbert-tree power routing (new, S): at fixed area, widths ∝ current minimize loss to R_s·(Σ I·l)²/A [C], so topology = Steiner tree minimizing Σ I·l (Gilbert network). A competent trunk-and-branch layout already obeys this; OP2's 14.5% comes from topology and 2-D spreading ([routing as current flow](../../topics/physics/physics-in-the-loop-optimization.md#routing-as-current-flow) [C]). Test: Gilbert tree + width rule vs IPC per-segment sizing vs full synthesis.
- **OP30** Constant current density, not constant width (sharp, S): taper width to segment current (loss optimum at fixed area); IPC-2221 thermal width (w ∝ I^1.38 at fixed ΔT) answers a different question, so use the larger. Test: loss and ΔT, tapered vs uniform trees at equal area.
- **OP31** Physics-priced PathFinder (sharp, M): edge costs from reference continuity (h_eff, slot crossings), local impedance deviation, coupling potential to declared victims; history cost becomes a measured shadow price. Test: return-path violations and crosstalk mV at equal completion vs plain PathFinder.
- **OP32** One copper market for signals and pours (new, L): one price field per layer updated by Lagrangian resource sharing (BonnRoute min-max resource sharing); router congestion prices = topology-optimization area multipliers. Test: completion + IR margin on 2-layer boards vs route-then-pour and pour-then-route.
- **OP33** Route the loop, not the net (combo, M): return path as a routed object (reference-layer choice, stitching vias per layer change, closing decap), each costed by loop-L increment from the filament kernel. Test: via-transition loop L and common-mode proxy vs "add a ground via nearby".
- **OP34** Elastic bands with physics forces (combo, M): after topology is fixed, relax critical paths as elastic bands (Quinlan–Khatib): tension, repelled by victims' coupling potential, attracted to continuous reference; keeps homotopy and DRC. Test: crosstalk and length vs PNS optimize on 20 buses.
- **OP35** Curves only where they pay (sharp, S): unmitred-corner |Γ| is small below tens of GHz and octilinear only ~5% longer than any-angle ([angles and curves](../../topics/physics/physics-in-the-loop-optimization.md#routing-as-current-flow) [C]). Arcs/clothoids for > 20 GHz lines, flex bend zones, clearance-limited dense escapes (arcs widen minimum gaps); octilinear elsewhere. Test: openEMS TDR/S11 per corner style at 5, 20, 40 GHz.
- **OP36** Coupling-budgeted corridor capacity (combo, M): in the multicommodity-flow or SURF cut test, cut capacity by the parallel length the crosstalk budget allows (mV per mm at that spacing), not only width + space. Test: crosstalk violations after routing with/without.
- **OP37** Diff pairs and length matching as ribbon objects (combo, M): pair = one ribbon of fixed internal geometry; length matching = equality constraint whose multiplier prices meander area. Test: meander area reserved vs needed on DDR/USB3 sets.

## D. Adjoints and sensitivities

- **OP38** Adjoint copper advisor for any layout (new, S): one extra solve gives dJ/d(every cell) for J = loss, IR drop at a load, loop L or ΔT; show top cells by mΩ per mm² and copper that does nothing. Adjoint matches central differences; adjoint-ranked growth beat uniform widening at +25% area ([POC](../../topics/physics/copper-shape-synthesis.md#proof-of-concept) [C]). Test: designer acceptance and realized mΩ per mm².
- **OP39** Neck finder by sensitivity (sharp, S): rank copper by ∂R/∂(local width); finds what the "narrowest cross-section" graph check approximates, plus 2-D necks between vias and pads. Test: agreement with designer-identified bottlenecks; false positives vs graph check.
- **OP40** Budget shadow prices from adjoints (combo, S): each budget's multiplier (nH, mV, K) from adjoint sensitivities at convergence ("relaxing the hot-loop budget by 0.1 nH frees 2 mm²") → [design-reasoning-architecture](../../topics/physics/design-reasoning-architecture.md). Test: predicted vs realized trade on relaxation.
- **OP41** Topological derivative for vias and voids (combo, M): change from inserting a small hole (antipad, slot) or small conductor (stitching via) anywhere; ranks positions in one solve. Test: predicted vs re-solved change for 50 via insertions.
- **OP42** Adjoint FDTD for small RF structures (combo, L): matching stubs, transitions, pixelated filters with MIT-licensed JAX/autograd FDTD/FDFD (FDTDX, ceviche) + fab-rule filters (cf. Princeton pixelated mm-wave inverse design). Test: one via transition and one matching network vs hand-tuned in openEMS.

## E. Search at scale

- **OP43** Physics continuation (new, S): smooth near-convex terms first (DC loss, thermal superposition, wirelength), HF loop, coupling, field terms as parts settle (cf. ePlace density ramp). Test: final budget spend and runtime vs all terms from the start.
- **OP44** Quality-diversity over physics descriptors (combo, M): MAP-Elites archive indexed by hot-loop nH, peak ΔT, worst victim coupling (mV). Test: coverage and best-in-niche vs NSGA-II at equal evaluations.
- **OP45** Multi-fidelity physics ladder with expected value of information (combo, M): kernel → 2-D sheet solve → PEEC/FastHenry → openEMS; next fidelity only where budget margin is within the cheaper model's error band ([V4 trust radius](../original/verification.md#v4-proxy-trust-radius--sharpened)). Test: full-solver calls per accepted design vs fixed-rung successive halving.
- **OP46** Adversarial excitation red team (new, M): search switching phases, edge rates, load steps maximizing victim noise or IR drop, then min-max optimize the layout; extends V2 to physics. Test: noise under found worst cases vs nominal-only.
- **OP47** Greedy shape screening, exact finish (sharp, S): energy-ranked removal from a full pour (ESO style) came within ~5% of SIMP in 14 solves ([POC](../../topics/physics/copper-shape-synthesis.md#proof-of-concept) [C]); screen pour variants for many placements, SIMP on finalists. Test: rank agreement greedy vs SIMP over 100 placements.
- **OP48** Surrogate-accelerated topology optimization (combo, M): neural operator trained on synthetic pour problems (random pads, holes, currents) labelled by the exact solver; warm starts and inner screening, every accepted design gets an exact solve. Test: speed-up and loss gap on held-out real boards.
- **OP49** Criticality-proportional solver compute (combo, S): allocate high-fidelity solves by |∂objective/∂geometry| × model uncertainty (extends I5). Test: budget violations found per CPU-hour vs uniform.

## F. Legalization and manufacturability

- **OP50** Contour-and-Minkowski legalizer with physics re-check (sharp, S): marching-squares contour at ρ = 0.5 → keep pad-connected islands → close by min gap → open by min width (round joins) → clip keep-outs → simplify → re-solve. POC: an opening wider than a neck disconnected the net, caught by the re-check ([legalization stress test](../../topics/physics/copper-shape-synthesis.md#proof-of-concept) [C]). Test: every synthesized zone, report ΔR, ΔL, DRC.
- **OP51** Minimum width inside the optimizer (sharp, S): filter radius (or robust erode/dilate thresholds) from fab min width, so no neck needs cutting. Test: legalization ΔR and disconnect rate vs filter radius.
- **OP52** Differentiable design rules (combo, M): port foundry-rule topology optimization (Hammond et al.: min width, spacing, area, enclosed area via differentiable morphology) and OptiClear-style minimum-distortion legalization to PCB rules. Test: DRC violations at convergence and objective loss vs post-hoc legalization.
- **OP53** Arc fitting at export (new, S): biarcs at the fab's min curvature radius, arcs in zone outlines/graphic polygons where KiCad accepts them, short segments otherwise; fewer vertices, no acid traps. Test: vertex count and clearance margin vs segment export.
- **OP54** CP-SAT for the discrete residue (combo, S): continuous methods for shapes and positions; CP-SAT for via counts and sites, relief styles, pad connection modes, layer choices, costs from adjoint sensitivities. Test: solve time and budget spend vs greedy rounding.
- **OP55** Zone recipes stored as intent (new, M): store each synthesized zone's recipe (net, terminals, currents, area or price, objective weights, filter radius) in the board; re-synthesize after part moves like a zone refill. Test: ECO turnaround; loss drift vs frozen polygon.

## G. Plugging into the joint placement-and-routing state

- **OP56** Copper fields as first-class state (new, M): joint state ([joint-place-route](../../topics/physics/joint-place-route.md)) carries per layer a coarse copper-density field and a price field beside poses and topologies; physics terms read them, shape synthesis writes them while parts move. Test: joint-run convergence with/without copper fields.
- **OP57** Physics stop rule (sharp, S): stop refining a term when marginal improvement per iteration × shadow price < compute cost or < its budget slack. Test: wall-clock to budget-feasible design vs fixed iteration counts.
