## A. Reciprocity and adjoints

- **SY1** Two primitives `adjoint(J)` and `lead_field(port)` (*sharp, S*; P1, P1′). Circuit adjoint = transposed solve of the linearized, possibly non-reciprocal circuit (SPICE `.noise` or our MNA); field lead field = forward solve with unit current into the victim port (valid: interconnect is reciprocal). Coupling = ∫source·lead field; output sensitivity = adjoint × lead field. Separating them shows which shortcut fails where: active parts break reciprocity but not adjoints, nonlinear victims break both. Test: 20 couplings on a buck + ADC board by per-aggressor solves vs lead fields (agree to solver tolerance); `.noise` vs transposed MNA with a non-reciprocal op-amp model.
- **SY2** Periodic adjoint for switching and sampled victims (*new, M*; P1′, P7 time translation broken to f mod f_s). Linearize converters (FB comparator, PWM), sampled ADC inputs, choppers, PLL phase detectors around periodic steady state by harmonic balance; one transposed harmonic-transfer solve per output frequency gives every site's susceptibility at every sideband f ± k·f_s (SpectreRF PXF; Telichevesky, Kundert, White 1996 [S]). LTI `.noise` at a DC point misses aliasing and frequency translation; DR36 plans frequencies with no transfer function. Test: buck FB node and SAR ADC model vs transient tone injection at 30 frequencies (±1 dB).
- **SY3** Power budgets for rectifying victims (*new, S*; linear path P1, nonlinear terminal). Flag op-amp, PGA, comparator inputs with datasheet EMIRR or rectifying models; budget coupled RF power, offset ∝ V_rf² scaled from the EMIRR test amplitude; allocate with the square-power form of DR9. 6 dB less coupling gives 12 dB less error there, so linear allocation misplaces effort. Test: SPICE with EMIRR-calibrated input model + bench tone injection; offset-vs-amplitude slope 2.
- **SY4** Reciprocity residual as a free solver audit (*sharp, S*; P1). Every extracted multiport (PEEC R and Lp, C matrices, κ_ab maps, crosstalk tables, macromodels) must satisfy Z = Zᵀ; report ‖Z − Zᵀ‖/‖Z‖ on every call, reject passive macromodels (SIM33) that break it. Asymmetric mutual ⇒ meshing, port or assembly bug. Test: all existing solvers (SIM46) + deliberately mis-meshed cases; bugs found.
- **SY5** Emission and immunity from one solve (*combo, S*; P1). The κ_ab or lead-field solve per structure–connector pair gives emission coupling and, by reciprocity, the coupling of cable CM disturbance (BCI or radiated) into the structure; one budget against the tighter of emission limit and immunity level × victim threshold; only the victim's nonlinear response needs new modelling. Immunity is thin in [research-gaps](../../topics/physics/research-gaps.md), but its linear half is already computed. Test: 3 boards with emission and BCI data; immunity weak spots vs emission hot spots and BCI failures.

## B. Duality: cuts and bridges

- **SY6** Image-exact slit cost (*sharp, S*; P3, P2b). Interior cut ΔR = −(2/π)R_s·ln cos(πΔψ/2I), edge notch twice that (images in the strip edges), replacing (π/4)R_s(Δψ/I)², which reads low at large Δψ/I and misses that a severing cut costs ∞. FD check: [D3](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#2-three-derivations) [C]. Test: 100 random cuts on real pours with non-uniform flow, log-cos with local Δψ vs FD.
- **SY7** Cut price for any objective Δψ_u·Δψ_λ (*new, S*; P1 + P2b crack polarization tensor). For any J (drop at one load, voltage across a sense pair, loss in a region): one forward + one adjoint solve; cut costs ΔJ ≈ (π/4)R_s·Δψ_u·Δψ_λ (Δψ = current crossing the cut line in each solution), sign = helps or hurts. Δψ² prices total loss only; a loss-harmless cut can wreck a load's drop or a Kelvin reading. Kelvin-pair check vs FD and plain adjoint: [D3](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#2-three-derivations) [C]. Test: 100 cuts × 3 objectives on 5 boards vs re-solves.
- **SY8** Bridge price by Δφ, dual of the slit law (*new, S*; P2b Keller). Straps, inlays, extra vias to a parallel layer, stitched parallel traces (thin, highly conducting, along the flow): ΔR ≈ (π/4)R_s·(Δφ/(R_s·I))²; lumped strap G across Δφ: ΔP ≈ −G·Δφ² for small G. Place bridges along streamlines where Δφ is largest (also where cuts are free), not "where it looks thin" [C, [D3](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#2-three-derivations)]. Test: 50 candidate straps on 5 high-current boards, predicted vs FD ΔR ranking.
- **SY9** Polarization tensors for slender defects (*sharp, S*; P2b, P3). In OP41 and SIM24 give thin voids, antipad rows, slits and thin straps anisotropic tensors (crack: π(ℓ/2)² normal to the cut, 0 along; needle: the dual) instead of "removed area × energy density". Area-based adjoint underestimates cuts by their aspect ratio; with the tensor ~10% ([D3](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#2-three-derivations)) [C], so one adjoint prices every small edit; finite edits need exact Woodbury updates. Test: SIM25's 100-slot set, error before/after.

## C. Nodes and loops once the plane breaks duality

- **SY10** Node budgets in ∫V dA with h-free coupling kernels (*new, S*; P2 broken by P3: a PEC images charges into dipoles, flat loops into quadrupoles). Describe each dv/dt node by voltage-area moment p = 2ε0∫V dA over its reference; couple to victims with ε0·A_a·A_v/(2πr³) (3-D) or ε0·w_a·w_v/(πD²) per unit length (2-D), not εA/h × K(D/h). FD slopes: [D1](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#2-three-derivations) [C]; the K-factor model credits thin prepreg with capacitive isolation it does not give low-impedance victims. Test: 2-D/3-D solves of SW pads near FB and XIN at h = 0.075–0.2 mm vs both models; E-probe scans on a buck coupon.
- **SY11** Bury the victim, not the aggressor (*new, S*; P3: an electric shield must sit in the field path, here through the air above the board). High-impedance victims (FB, XIN, ADC input, reference) passing within a few mm of dv/dt copper: route on an inner layer under the plane, or under a top shield; drop coplanar guards over thin dielectric. A grounded coplanar guard barely helps over thin dielectric ([D1](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#2-three-derivations)) [C], so FE25-style guards waste channel; a plane between the conductors removes the coupling. Test: crosstalk coupon, SW-like aggressor, victims on L1 ± guard and on L3.
- **SY12** Node–loop crossover distance sets the halo (*new, S*; P2 broken by P3). Capacitive pickup Z_v·C_m·dV/dt (∝ 1/r³) overtakes magnetic M·di/dt (∝ h²/r⁵ over a plane) beyond r* = 3·η0·h·√((A_M/A_E)·(di/dt)/(Z_v·dV/dt)); beyond r* the node sets the halo (area × swing, 1/r³), inside it the loop. Crossover distances over a 0.1 mm plane: [D1](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#2-three-derivations) [C]. Test: E- and H-probe scans at 3, 5, 10, 20 mm on a buck coupon, 50 Ω and 1 kΩ victim terminations.

## D. Mirror symmetry and balance

- **SY13** Symmetry declarations on the physics graph (*new, M*; P4). IR declares symmetry groups as port/body permutations (pair Z2, H-bridge Z2×Z2, 3-phase C3, N-phase interleaved CN, paralleled parts S_N); extractor reports residual ‖PXPᵀ − X‖/‖X‖ for X = R, Lp, C, splits edits into symmetric/antisymmetric parts, attributes the antisymmetric residual to features by adjoint. Generalizes PC16, DR44 from pairs; checks the operator's symmetry, not the drawing's. Test: planted asymmetries (one-sided via, unequal pad, one-sided pour) in pair, bridge, 3-phase layouts; localization accuracy.
- **SY14** Mode conversion as one budget, equivalent-skew currency (*sharp, S*; P4). Merge Bal and Vcm into one quantity with a level index (pair vs its reference, board vs world); express every source (asymmetry Δχ, χ = C1g/(C1g+C2g), and skew) in dB or equivalent skew Δt_eq = 2|Δχ|/(π·f). Practice budgets skew in ps and asymmetry nowhere: a 10 µm width mismatch (~6 ps at 1 GHz) can outweigh a whole skew allowance [C, [P4](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#1-principles)]. Test: rank conversion sources on 20 pairs vs openEMS Scd21.
- **SY15** Imbalance-step ledger for pairs and connectors (*sharp, M*; P4). χ(z) along each pair and single-ended net vs plane and world from 2-D cross-sections; CM sources = steps Δχ summed with phase e^(−jβz), plus a velocity-mismatch term in microstrip. Section opening and closing within ℓ ≪ λ/2π costs ∝ βℓ·Δχ; one reaching a connector costs Δχ in full. At connectors assign return pins so χ_board ≈ χ_cable ("unbalance matching", Watanabe et al. [S]). FE35's "accumulate imbalance" counts non-converting uniform asymmetry and misses the cable step. Test: openEMS Scd21 and cable CM current on 6 layouts with planted steps.
- **SY16** Antiphase node pairing, the electric figure-8 (*new, S*; P2c dual of mirrored loops, P4). Full bridges with complementary switching, push-pull, class-D outputs, 180°-interleaved phases: place antiphase switch nodes adjacent with equal copper area over the same plane; voltage-area moments cancel (every harmonic for complementary switching, odd harmonics for interleaving); budget area imbalance as XD11 budgets current imbalance. XD11 and LA35 cancel loops but have no node twin, and node coupling is what remains over a plane (SY10). Test: near-field E scan and cable CM current, adjacent equal-area vs separated full-bridge layouts.
- **SY17** Null-plane placement for cancelled aggressors (*sharp, S*; P4: odd sources null on their mirror plane). After `cancel` (mirrored halves) make the halo anisotropic and place victims on the mirror plane; score `cancel` on far-field moment (κ-weighted cable coupling), not halo radius ([P4](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#1-principles)) [C]; S5's "pack tighter" is the wrong use. Test: PEEC M at null and off-null positions; near-field scan of a dual-cap buck.
- **SY18** Feed topology from its symmetry group, Curie check (*new, S*; P4, P7). For N paralleled parts compute the automorphism group of the feed-and-return network (graph + resistances); sharing is equal only within one orbit → report orbits as the sharing guarantee; offer a transitive feed (binary tree, star, ring) when equal sharing matters, reverse return when pairs suffice. Reverse return equalizes only pairs; a binary tree shares equally ([§3](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#3-more-derivations-checked-against-computed-numbers-c)) [C]. Test: FD current share on 5 paralleled-FET and LED layouts; orbit count vs measured spread.
- **SY19** Moment-matching order for matched groups (*combo, S*; P7). Score matched devices (bridge arms, gain pairs, mirrors, sense resistors) by coinciding spatial moments: order 1 = common centroid, order 2 adds equal second moments (ABBA/BAAB); apply to every solver gradient field (temperature, plane voltage, stray B, etch density). TM14 covers thermal to order 1 only; same rule cancels DC plane gradients for precision ADCs ([worked case C](../../topics/physics/worked-thought-experiments.md)) and field gradients for sense loops (XD76). Test: FV and sheet gradients on 3 precision layouts; residual mismatch vs moment order.
- **SY20** Etch-loading symmetry around pairs (*new, S*; P4). Keep copper density within ~3h equal on both sides of each fast pair (or make one-sided copper symmetric) so etch loading does not skew leg widths. Leg-width mismatch converts more than the field effect of any nearby copper ([P4](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#1-principles)) [C]; whether one-sided copper biases width is unmeasured. Test: leg widths on a coupon with one- and two-sided pours at 1h–5h; Scd21.

## E. Apertures and islands

- **SY21** Babinet aperture–island critic (*combo, M*; P2a). One critic for slots, merged antipad rows, stitching-via gaps, can seams and floating copper fingers (one object spread over FE2, FE4, FE32, XD83, XD80, PW11): polarizability ∝ ℓ³/ln(4ℓ/g) (slot), d³/6 (hole) (Bethe [S]); half-wave resonance c/(2ℓ√ε_eff) shared by slot and complementary island; driving-point L by Booker (η0²/4)·C_strip ([§3](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#3-more-derivations-checked-against-computed-numbers-c)) [C]. Merging N holes into a slot multiplies leakage by ~N²·(p/d)³. Test: openEMS S21 through a seam and a slot vs prediction; resonance of a floating finger.

## F. Gauge

- **SY22** Every voltage names its loop (*new, S*; P5). A budget "V between A and B" must name its measurement path (sense wires, ADC reference route); critics evaluate the gauge-invariant φ(A) − φ(B) − jω∫A·dl along it; a loopless budget is a validation error like LA1's half-port. PW41's Kelvin L_err 0.55–0.87 nH, changing with sense routing, is this effect. Test: count ambiguous budgets on 5 boards; loop-EMF predictions vs probes routed two ways.
- **SY23** Gauge-invariant ledger attribution (*sharp, S*; P5). Attribute loop/port spend to segments by sensitivity (adjoint ∂y/∂geometry) or Shapley values over segment edits, not partial-inductance size (gauge-fixed accounting, A → 0 at infinity); flag rows whose top spender changes between conventions. The largest-partial-L segment need not be the most helpful edit, and the LLM acts on "top spenders". Test: 10 hot loops, both rankings vs the edit that actually lowers L most.

## G. Images, scales and lattices

- **SY24** Image-lattice kernel library (*combo, S*; P3). One library of image lattices for bounded sheets, sign by boundary (Neumann, adiabatic or open cavity edge +; Dirichlet or shorted edge −), serving IR drop, decap reach (XD113), thermal (TM2), slit prices near edges (SY6), cavity Green's functions. Shift-invariant kernels were 2–3× off near edges in thermal; XD112's PDN and IR kernels share the flaw. Test: vs FD near edges for IR drop and decap reach on 5 outlines.
- **SY25** ℓ_d map, image validity per structure (*sharp, S*; P6). Per band ℓ_d = R_s/(ωμ0) per reference layer vs each structure's scale (h for a trace return, loop size for a commutation loop, region size for an ADC ground) → choose image PEEC, resistive sheet or thin-sheet eddy solve per structure and band. Onset, half- and 90%-mirroring frequencies differ per structure ([f_x found four times](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#3-more-derivations-checked-against-computed-numbers-c)) [C]; one f_x per stackup mislabels them. Test: thin-sheet eddy solver vs image PEEC across these scales.
- **SY26** Corner-type-aware rounding (*sharp, S*; P6 + conductor/dielectric complementarity). Concave corner: current crowds as r^(−1/3), E-free → fillet on pulse, fuse, ESD paths. Convex corner: E crowds in the 270° gap, no current (r^(+1)) → round on HV nets, chamfer convex pour corners freely. PW18 and TM22 treat the effects separately; they live on opposite corners. Test: FD bends + HV field solve; pulse heating and inception vs corner type.
- **SY27** One antenna-mode map, both CM mechanisms (*combo, S*; P1, P6). From FE3's antenna-mode solution (K_ext, σ_ext), one solve per connector pair: current-driven coupling ∝ h·K_ext(x)·cos θ for every trace and loop; voltage-driven ∝ ∫V dA·σ_ext(x) for every node, pad, heatsink, SW pour; edge law Maxwell's 1/√(1−(2x/W)²) [C]. MREMC-style budgets apply the voltage-driven form mainly to heatsinks; SW pours near edges are voltage-driven antennas. Test: vs openEMS on 3 boards; ranking of top CM contributors.
- **SY28** Designed cuts that steer current off a measurement (*new, M*; P1, P2b). Given aggressor flow ψ_a and measurement lead field ψ_lead, search cuts reducing R_s∫K_a·K_lead dA within loss and return-path budgets, each priced ΔJ = (π/4)R_s·Δψ_a·Δψ_lead with sign; also says when exclusion is unnecessary (orthogonal flows). Replaces folklore "split the ground". Test: 5 mixed-signal boards, predicted vs FD change in measured offset; vs a full split.
- **SY29** Weave-lattice commensurability check (*sharp, S*; P4, P6 lattice symmetry, Weyl equidistribution). Glass cloth = 2-D lattice (warp, weft pitches); score each long pair segment's angle against directions perpendicular to strong reciprocal-lattice vectors (axes; for near-square weaves with strong crossings the diagonal atan(p_y/p_x)); prefer incommensurate angles. FE42 allows up to 45°, the worst angle in the toy square-weave model ([§3](../../topics/physics/lens-sy.md#3-more-derivations-checked-against-computed-numbers-c)) [C]. Test: skew measurements or 3-D weave model at 0°, 10°, 22°, 45° on 1080, 2116, 1067 cloth.
- **SY30** Reverse-drive near-field scan (*new, S*; P1). Drive the victim port with a generator, scan with a near-field probe; by reciprocity the map = victim pickup from a probe-like source at each point; one scan replaces point-by-point injection, F4 susceptibility data (gap 1) for the cost of an emission scan. Test: vs direct injection at 10 points on a coupon (±2 dB).
- **SY31** Scaled coupons by electromagnetic similarity (*new, M*; P6). Keep copper thickness and εr, scale other lengths by s (2–4×), measure at f/s: R = R_s·N□, ωL, ωC, kℓ and image group ℓ·ω·μ0/R_s unchanged → every impedance and coupling reproduced; only t/δ and relative copper thickness differ. Sub-mm features over 0.1 mm prepreg are hard to build and probe; scaled coupons on standard laminate are cheap (calibration programme, gap 1). Parts do not scale: mounting-inductance coupons stay 1×. Test: 1× and 3× hot-loop and slot coupons; impedance agreement at f and f/3.
- **SY32** Duality generator for rules and operators (*new, S*; P2, P3). Apply P2 mechanically to the rule and operator catalog (loop → node: L→C, ψ→φ, current→voltage, loop image→charge image; cut → bridge; slot → island), flag where the plane breaks the dual (P3), send each new dual to the solvers for a verdict. Examples from this lens: SY8, SY11, SY16, SY21, SY26. Test: duals for 40 operators; count physically valid new ones after solver checks.
