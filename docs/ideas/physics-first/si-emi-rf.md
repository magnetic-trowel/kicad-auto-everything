# Physics-first ideas: SI, EMI and RF (FE1–FE56)

## A. The return path as one metric

- **FE1** Return-path EMF ledger (new, M): per fast net, list every path discontinuity with closed-form ΔZ_k(f): slot ΔL ≈ μ0·l/(2·ln(4l/g)), via pair (Hankel radial term), edge enhancement, decap path + cavity; × the net's current spectrum → two [I1](../original/intent-and-why.md) ledger rows: SI (mV at receiver) and EMI (mV between cable points). About a dozen plane rules become one number per net with top spenders. Test: [pc_refplane](../../topics/physics/physics-critics.md)-style planted faults on 20 boards; rank correlation of spend vs PEEC/openEMS plane voltage; zero firings on benign twins.
- **FE2** Slot price, not slot ban (sharp, S): router charges ω_knee·ΔL_slot·I per void crossing, ΔL ≈ μ0·l/(2 ln(4l/g)), not linear in l: a 2 mm antipad gap is cheap, a 20 mm slot costs about 8 mm of extra trace ([slot row](../../topics/physics/fields-si-emi.md#2-discontinuities-as-extra-return-impedance) [C]). Test: price vs forbid in the [R2](../original/routing.md) shadow-net router on 20 boards: completion and ledger spend.
- **FE3** Transfer-inductance maps κ_ab(x, y, θ) (new, M): per cable-connector pair one PEEC solve (inject at a, extract at b); by reciprocity its plane-current density gives the a–b voltage from a unit return EMF at any position and direction; then O(1) EMI lookups for placer and router, replacing "nothing fast between connectors". Test: map-based V_ab vs full PEEC for 50 random trace placements, target ±3 dB.
- **FE4** Antipad web synthesis (new, S): detect antipad clusters merging into slots under fast nets; shrink, stagger or split to leave copper webs ≥ ~h wide. Separate holes are nearly free, a merged cluster is a slot (Ott's 15-hole experiment; [§2](../../topics/physics/fields-si-emi.md#2-discontinuities-as-extra-return-impedance)). Test: BGA breakouts on open boards: ΔL before/after, DRC clean, routability kept.
- **FE5** Return via sized by cavity launch (sharp, S): return via at s ≤ 0.63/k(f_N) ≈ λ_d/10 (10 dB less cavity launch than an unpaired via, [§2](../../topics/physics/fields-si-emi.md#2-discontinuities-as-extra-return-impedance)); two symmetric vias for tighter budgets; reproduces TI's 200 mil and 50 mil for any stackup and rate. Test: openEMS via transitions at three data rates vs the Hankel closed form.
- **FE6** Smooth edge cost (sharp, S): replace 2h / 3h / 90 mil edge rules with an EMI term (MREMC/PEEC edge enhancement ~1/√d, in dB of V_cm per connector pair) + an SI term zero beyond ~3h; edges with no cable nearby are not penalised. Test: decisions on 20 boards vs slogan rules, scored by PEEC V_ab.
- **FE7** Stitching-cap optimizer (new, S): for a forced split crossing, choose cap value (SRF at the dominant harmonic), count and position along the slot minimizing |Z_slot ∥ Z_cap| over the net's spectrum. Caps buy ~10 dB each near SRF ([worked cases](../../topics/physics/fields-emi-budget.md#2-worked-cases-fe_emi_budgetpy-c)); "≤ 1 µF" is not the operative parameter. Test: fe_emi_budget sweep vs openEMS on one slotted board.
- **FE8** Priced reference changes (sharp, S): every fast-net via gets a transition type (Ott 1–4) and ΔZ price (via pair, or decap path + cavity); router weighs a layer change against the detour it saves. Test: P↔G change count and ledger spend vs Freerouting baseline.
- **FE9** Return-current picture for the LLM (new, S): PEEC plane current under each critical net as a heat map, detours outlined and labelled in nH. Test: LLM fix quality on 20 planted faults with/without the map.
- **FE10** Current-aware plane partitioning (combo with [R7](../original/routing.md), M): score power-plane splits by Σ ΔL_n·I_n over fast nets; boundaries where the PEEC return-current density of all fast nets is below threshold. Test: R7 benchmark with this objective.

## B. EMI budget, cables and connectors

- **FE11** EMI margin as a placement loss (combo, M): [fe_emi_budget](../../topics/physics/fields-emi-budget.md) chain + FE3 maps → margin per (net, harmonic, connector pair); loss = softplus(E − limit + guard). Subsumes "cabled connectors on one edge" and "no fast circuitry between connectors" ([what buys dB](../../topics/physics/fields-emi-budget.md#3-what-buys-db-in-order-this-case)). Test: [P8](../original/placement.md) A/B on 10 boards with ≥ 2 cabled connectors, scored by PEEC/MREMC margin.
- **FE12** Fix ranking by sensitivity (new, S): ∂margin/∂parameter per fix (cable side, slot, edge distance, caps, t_r, series R); offer the cheapest with its dB gain; in the worked case topology beat tweaks, but the ranking is case-specific ([§3](../../topics/physics/fields-emi-budget.md#3-what-buys-db-in-order-this-case)). Test: agreement with EMC engineers' rankings on 10 cases.
- **FE13** Clock plan against resonances (new, S): clock and switching harmonic lines on one axis with cable half-wave resonances, board dipole modes, plane cavity modes, slot resonances; propose frequency, spread or layout changes keeping strong lines off peaks. Test: synthetic sweeps of the emission estimate as lines move.
- **FE14** Chassis-bond planner (new, M): choose k bond points (screws or caps) minimizing max V_ab over connector pairs, bonds as PEEC shorts (a bond shorts the antenna feed where voltage is generated). Test: vs "bond at every connector" in PEEC and on one enclosure.
- **FE15** Spectrum-defined net classes (sharp, S): I(f) envelope from IBIS or part family; nets with negligible energy 30 MHz–1 GHz drop out of EMI checks, the rest ranked by in-band energy; replaces "fast net" name regexes. Test: precision/recall vs expert labels.
- **FE16** Skew as a common-mode budget for cables (sharp, S): for pairs leaving on a cable, allocate skew from the CM budget via sin(πfΔt)/2, shared with CMC and magnetics rejection ([cable case](../../topics/physics/fields-emi-budget.md#2-worked-cases-fe_emi_budgetpy-c)). Test: allocations vs vendor skew rules on Ethernet and USB boards.
- **FE17** Voltage-driven source critic (combo, M): quick BEM free-space capacitance of heatsinks, tall magnetics, switch-node copper, long edge traces; V_cm ≈ V·C_part/C_board; flag heatsinks over fast dies near λ/4, suggest grounding points. Test: openEMS on three heatsink cases.
- **FE18** I/O-coupling budget (sharp, S): crosstalk onto nets leaving on cables budgeted against the cable antenna at ~100 µV, not receiver noise margin. Test: flagged pairs vs HyperLynx-style I/O-coupling rules.
- **FE19** Bring-up probe plan (combo, S): from the ledger, emit near-field probe points and expected spectra for the first scan, calibrating κ and the MREMC offset (FE56). Test: one real board.

## C. Crosstalk

- **FE20** Per-layer crosstalk kernels (sharp, S): field-solve K_L, K_C vs D/h per layer once per stackup ([fe_field2d.py](../../../tools/research/physics/fe_field2d.py)). Microstrip algebraic with FEXT growing with coupled length, stripline exponential with zero FEXT ([§4](../../topics/physics/fields-si-emi.md#4-crosstalk-from-field-overlap)). Test: vs commercial 2-D BEM on three stackups.
- **FE21** Per-victim noise budget in mV (sharp, S): budget = noise-margin share, LSB-based limit for ADC inputs, or 100 µV for I/O; aggressors share it; spacing is a solved output; 3W is ~60× too strict in stripline (§4). Test: area saved vs 3W at equal simulated noise on 10 boards.
- **FE22** Timing-window crosstalk (new, M): aggressor edges never coinciding with the victim's sampling window do not count; STA windows mask the coupling matrix. Test: DDR byte lanes, spacing reduction at equal eye.
- **FE23** FEXT-driven layer choice (new, S): when coupled length × (K_L − K_C)/2 / t_r exceeds budget, move the bus to stripline (FEXT vanishes) instead of spreading. Test: long parallel buses on 4- and 6-layer boards.
- **FE24** Broadside coupling map (sharp, S): overlap-weighted broadside coupling from the 2-D solve replaces "route adjacent layers at 90°"; parallel runs allowed with large vertical separation. Test: dual-stripline 6-layer boards.
- **FE25** Guard-trace gate (sharp, S): guard trace only when the 2-D solve shows spacing alone cannot meet the budget in the channel, and only as an end-shorted stripline guard. Test: proposals vs expert review.

## D. Cavities, stitching, fences, shields

- **FE26** Cavity eigenvalue critic (new, S): first resonance of each plane pair with stitching vias as shorted ports ([fe_cavity.py](../../../tools/research/physics/fe_cavity.py), seconds) vs the highest frequency at which reference-changing vias still inject energy; the largest-empty-circle proxy is only ±30% and pattern-biased ([§5](../../topics/physics/fields-si-emi.md#5-the-board-as-resonant-structures)). Test: first-mode error vs openEMS on five boards.
- **FE27** Lattice stitching synthesis (new, S): square or hex lattice with pitch from Belov cut-off f_p > f_target, or blue noise around obstacles. Grids beat random vias of the same count (§5 [C]). Test: first mode per via budget: grid, Poisson, designer layout.
- **FE28** Eigenvalue-gradient via placement (new, M): add vias greedily where the first mode's |E_z|² is largest (first-order perturbation: a short there raises the eigenvalue most). Test: first mode vs via count, vs FE27.
- **FE29** Source-weighted stitching (new, M): weight each cavity mode by modal overlap with the board's reference-changing vias and IC ground pins; suppress only excited modes. Test: aggressor–victim via transfer impedance at equal via count.
- **FE30** Fence from an isolation target (sharp, S): pitch from |T| = 2/√(4 + B²), row count from the dB target, channel width from TE10 cut-off; verify with the cavity model (finite boards differed from the closed form by −24 to +19 dB). Test: openEMS on RF compartments.
- **FE31** Lossy edge termination for PWR–GND cavities (combo, M): lower cavity Q with R–C edge terminations or a lossy edge band instead of decaps, priced by peak |Z| reduction. Test: cavity model with resistive edge ports, then one fabricated board.
- **FE32** Shield-can resonance check (sharp, S): a can is a cavity ([rf-geometry §6](../../topics/physics/rf-geometry.md#6-rf-transmission-lines-bends-fences-shields)); check modes vs radio bands, propose absorber or partition wall. Test: vs vendor can designs.
- **FE33** Perimeter-vs-distributed resolver (combo, S): compute interior first mode (Hubing's concern) and edge-fringe voltage (Ott's), split the via budget between perimeter and interior to meet both: the contested rule becomes two terms of one objective. Test: fe_cavity + openEMS edge radiation.

## E. Differential pairs

- **FE34** Coupling chosen by loss and density (sharp, S): gap from Zdiff, loss budget, channel width; loose coupling costs less conductor loss and EMI only while k·s > ε ([§6](../../topics/physics/fields-si-emi.md#6-differential-pairs)). Test: 2-D loss per inch and routed density.
- **FE35** Mode-conversion critic along the route (new, M): slice the pair into cross-sections, compute imbalance from asymmetric neighbours, plane edges, vias, weave with the 2-D solver, accumulate conversion; catches asymmetry length-skew checks miss. Test: three asymmetric cases vs 3-D simulation.
- **FE36** Turn-balanced pair placement (combo, S): placer cost on signed turn imbalance (skew ≈ pitch × Σθ), so turns cancel by topology and tuning fixes only the residual. Test: total serpentine length on 10 boards.
- **FE37** Spectrally weighted running skew (sharp, S): running-skew budget uses sin(πfΔt) at CM-relevant frequencies; compensation near each mismatch. Test: CM at connector vs end-only matching.
- **FE38** Loose-pair escape mode (new, S): pairs decouple through BGA pin fields when each leg keeps solid reference; uncoupled length budgeted by its Zdiff change, not a fixed 45 mil. Test: escape completion and eye in 2-D/3-D.

## F. Geometry, pads, vias, weave

- **FE39** Geometry relevance gate (sharp, S): per net |Γ| per feature from t_r; corner, arc, teardrop constraints only where they exceed budget, elsewhere geometry freed for length, density, DFM. Test: constraints removed with no simulated eye change.
- **FE40** Pad-void synthesis (new, S): per stackup and pad, solve void extent and depth giving Z_pad = Z0, emit as a zone. Settles 60% vs 100% per stackup ([rf-geometry §4](../../topics/physics/rf-geometry.md#4-pads-voids-and-reference-cut-outs-c-fe_field2d)). Test: TDR or 3-D simulation of AC caps on three stackups vs the 60% and 100% rules.
- **FE41** Stub price in the router (sharp, S): via-stub cost |S11| ≈ (Z0/2Z_s)·tan(βL) at Nyquist (reproduces 0.3/Gbps, [rf-geometry §5](../../topics/physics/rf-geometry.md#5-via-transitions-and-stubs-c-fe_geometry-c)); router trades layers, backdrill, microvias by price. Test: stub-budget compliance and layer use on Gen4 boards.
- **FE42** Fibre-weave angle planner (new, M): any-angle constraint θ ≥ max(asin(3P/L), Bragg bound) for long SerDes, or panel rotation chosen by fab cost. Test: statistical skew simulation with a weave model + routed-length overhead.
- **FE43** Antipad shaping for via impedance (sharp, S): antipad size and shape (oval for pairs) so via Z = Z0, checking cut-outs do not form slots for neighbours (FE4). Test: 3-D simulation at 16 GHz.
- **FE44** ESD field-enhancement corners (sharp, S): corner-radius rule only on ESD/HV-exposed nets, from corner field enhancement vs surface breakdown, not a 45° slogan. Test: number of constrained nets; review of TI's 2.6 mm example.
- **FE45** Teardrops as DFM only (sharp, S): a teardrop costs about one corner's SI; allow on all nets with edges slower than ~30 ps; RF uses computed impedance tapers. Test: the table check in [rf-geometry](../../topics/physics/rf-geometry.md).

## G. RF

- **FE46** Chu-sphere keep-out volume (new, S): 3-D keep-out from band and bandwidth, ka from Q ≤ 1/(√2·B) ([rf-geometry §8](../../topics/physics/rf-geometry.md#8-antenna-keep-outs-and-ground-under-rf)), merged with the vendor polygon; check enclosure parts, batteries, connectors in 3-D. Test: vs vendor keep-outs and measured detuning.
- **FE47** Pin-Q matching budget (new, S): l_max of the first match element from pin Q and mismatch allowance ([rf-geometry §7](../../topics/physics/rf-geometry.md#7-matching-at-the-pin-c-fe_geometry-d)); otherwise re-synthesize the match including the line. Test: scikit-rf on vendor reference designs.
- **FE48** RF reference-integrity critic (sharp, S): price holes, antipads, slots within ±3h of an RF line as jωΔL vs Z0 at the carrier; replaces "only GND under RF on all layers" when L2 is intact (rf-geometry §8). Test: flags on open RF boards vs openEMS.
- **FE49** openEMS RF-path auto-check (combo, L): crop the RF path (pin → match → feed) with its reference from KiCad, simulate S11/S21 automatically in openEMS (GPL-3.0), compare with budget. Test: five open RF boards.
- **FE50** Harmonic-to-channel frequency plan (new, S): find clock and SMPS harmonics landing in receive channels (26 MHz × 92 = 2.392 GHz); separation and shielding only for those nets. Test: synthetic BLE and Wi-Fi boards.
- **FE51** Ground plane as the antenna's other arm (sharp, S): board ground sized in the λ/4–λ/2 range for the band, chassis-mode current path kept free of slots (u-blox minimum, rf-geometry §8). Test: openEMS on module-plus-board variants.
- **FE52** RF↔SMPS coupling budget (new, M): replace "20 mm from SMPS" by a near-field estimate in dB below receiver sensitivity: inductor magnetic dipole moment (ripple current, footprint) + switch-node electric coupling (area, dV/dt). Test: dev board or openEMS.

## H. Solvers and validation

- **FE53** PEEC as a library (sharp, M): fe_peec_plane → fast hierarchical (later GPU) solver, whole-board ΔL and κ in < 1 s. Test: speed and agreement with FastHenry.
- **FE54** Rule → metric oracle tests (new, S): each classic rule becomes a compliant/non-compliant layout pair the metric must rank correctly; catches metric bugs, documents each decompression. Test: 30 pairs, full agreement or a documented regime where the rule is wrong.
- **FE55** Adjoint sensitivities for placement (new, M): ∂V_ab/∂position and ∂f_res/∂via from PEEC and cavity models by the adjoint method. Test: gradient check vs finite differences.
- **FE56** Calibration ledger (combo, S): every closed form carries a trust range (± dB) learned from openEMS or measurement; escalate to full-wave only when a margin is inside it ([solver-ladder](../../topics/physics/solver-ladder.md)). Test: escalations vs decisions they changed.
