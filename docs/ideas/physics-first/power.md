## A. Objectives from waveforms

- **PW1** Waveform-derived copper roles (*new, M*). One SPICE run (ngspice, operating point + one switching period) or a topology template gives per-node dv/dt and per-branch di/dt, I_rms, I_dc; each copper piece gets weights a (L), b (C: local/world/victim), g (R) and victim sensitivity ([PC §1](../../topics/physics/power-copper.md#1-node-and-branch-classification-the-decision-procedure)). Replaces net-name classes (PWR_HOT, SW, SENSITIVE); catches inductor-branch di/dt set by L, not edges. Test: 30 open converter boards vs expert labels and datasheet layout guides.
- **PW2** Width from the a·L + b·C + g·R optimum (*sharp, S*). w* per piece over a plane, Z0* = V/I for a piece in both a fast node and a fast loop ([PC §2](../../topics/physics/power-copper.md#2-the-width-trade-and-two-invariants): LV SW never loss-limited, 400 V PFC drain 0.8–3 mm); replaces "make power wide, keep SW small". Test: w* vs widths on 20 TI/ADI/EPC EVMs, each disagreement explained by an EMI or thermal term.
- **PW3** Zero-length SW segment inside the commutation loop (*sharp, S*). Hard constraint: SW copper between HS source and LS drain < ε (abut pads or use an integrated half-bridge pinout): the only copper both fast-dv/dt node and fast-di/dt branch, and L·C = (l/v)², so only length helps both. Test: PEEC L and SW-node C with and without abutted FET pads.
- **PW4** "Capacitance to whom" ledger for fast nodes (*new, M*). Split each fast node's C into local return (cheap), outside world (EMI item, [fields-emi-budget](../../topics/physics/fields-emi-budget.md) chain), victims (crosstalk item); replaces "SW area ≤ k × pads": the same pad radiates near a cable and is harmless over its own plane (PC §1 [C, I]). Test: rank EMI scans on 5 converter boards, ledger vs area rule.
- **PW5** Heat through the dielectric, priced in picofarads (*new, S*). A SW pad sheds heat into the plane below at G/C = k/ε: size area and dielectric for a ΔT and a ½CV²f budget together (PC §1 "Thermal via a dielectric" [C]), replacing "never heatsink via SW copper" with a trade. Test: thermal FV + switching-loss bench data on a 48 V and a 400 V half-bridge.
- **PW6** Length invariants as the first placement metric (*sharp, S*). In coarse placement score power pieces by length weighted by √(a·b) and g (invariants L·C = (l/v)², R·A = R_s·l², PC §2); optimise widths after placement, since widths trade parasitics and cannot beat length. Test: correlation of placement-stage length scores with post-route physics scores vs loop-area scores.
- **PW7** Edge rate as a layout variable (*combo, M*). When the min-L geometry still breaks the overshoot/EMI budget, the solver picks the gate or bootstrap resistor, trading efficiency (∝ t_edge) vs overshoot (L·di/dt) and spectrum; rules treat edge rate as a separate EMI knob ([gate-drive-bootstrap](../../topics/rules/power/gate-drive-bootstrap.md)). Test: SPICE + PEEC on 3 cells, Pareto fronts with/without edge rate.

## B. Trace, pour and plane

- **PW8** Squares as the unit of power copper (*combo, S*). Store N□ per port pair in the physics IR (R = R_s·N□; L = μ0·d·N□ with a return copy at depth d, PC §3): one number for IR drop, plane spreading L and overlapped-loop L, which the LLM reasons with without coordinates. Test: N□-derived R, L vs padne and PEEC on 10 boards, within 10%.
- **PW9** Feed-first pour design (*sharp, S*). Report the entry-constriction share (loss within 3 mm of each pad); widen entries first (more pads, via rows along the edge, parallel parts), the body only when that share is low; wider-than-feed pours pay a logarithmic spreading toll ([PC §3](../../topics/physics/power-copper.md#3-trace-vs-pour-vs-plane) [C]). Test: 20 high-current nets, R reduction per added mm², entry-first vs body-first.
- **PW10** Field-guided pour trimming for routing space (*combo, S*). Release copper whose removal costs < x% of the IR budget, remove islands, legalise to min width (greedy-trim numbers: PC §3; optimised shapes: OP2, [optimization](optimization.md)). Test: 2-layer route completion with/without trimmed pours at equal IR margin.
- **PW11** Dead-copper classifier (*new, S*). Label each zero-current region (from J, node dv/dt, tie count, thermal flux) as heat spreader, multi-tied shield, fab-balance copper, single-tied fast-node stub (capacitor/antenna) or floating island, and keep, stitch or delete by label (PC §3): "remove islands" and "pour everything" are each wrong half the time. Test: expert agreement on 200 labelled regions.
- **PW12** Via-field neck detector (*sharp, S*). Multiply sheet R in BGA/connector fields by the antipad factor (D/p table: PC §3 [C]); flag D/p > 0.8 on a rail path; suggest smaller antipads, a power layer elsewhere, or rerouting the rail around the field. Test: predicted IR drop vs padne on 5 BGA boards.
- **PW13** Peaceman sub-cell vias for board-scale solves (*new, S*). Via = one grid cell + (1/2π)·ln(0.198·Δx/r) squares ([PDN §4](../../topics/physics/pdn-and-commutation.md#4-pdn-islands-and-ir-drop)): a 1 mm grid handles 0.3 mm vias with ≈100× fewer cells, within 2% of the analytic log [C], so whole-board IR and spreading maps run inside the placement loop. Test: irregular boards vs fine-grid reference.
- **PW14** Reverse-return feeds for paralleled parts (*new, S*). Feed paralleled FETs, MLCCs, LED strings, shunts at one end of the bus and exit at the other (Tichelmann), or by a symmetric tree; check sharing by ladder or Laplace solve (sharing ratios: [PDN §2](../../topics/physics/pdn-and-commutation.md#2-vias-parallel-count-pitch-and-current-sharing) [C]). Test: sharing measurements or padne/PEEC on 3 multi-FET boards.
- **PW15** Trace-to-pour conversion trigger (*sharp, S*). Apply the PC §3 conversion rule per piece, with its reason. Test: proposed conversions on open boards vs expert judgement.
- **PW16** Streamline-routed power traces (*new, M*). Route a few traces along streamlines of the ideal pour's stream function, width set by carried flux: near-pour R with less copper and signal gaps left ([cross-domain-lessons L1](../../topics/physics/cross-domain-lessons.md#l1-coil-design-by-stream-functions--pours-slots-planar-coils)). Test: R and freed area vs full pour and OP2 shapes.
- **PW17** Regional copper weight and inlays (*combo, M*). At fixed length only thickness lowers R without area (R·A = R_s·l²): choose copper weight per region (selective heavy copper, embedded coins, press-fit busbars, [high-current](../../topics/rules/power/high-current.md)) against the thermal budget, cost and fab spacing penalty. Test: cost and ΔT trade on 3 motor-drive boards.

## C. Corners, curves and pulses

- **PW18** Curves only on pulse paths (*sharp, S*). Fillet/arc inside corners only on surge, ESD, inrush, short-circuit or fuse nets, by the adiabatic hot-spot factor (⟨J²⟩ over √(α·t_pulse)) (steady-state corners nil, pulse factors: [PC §4](../../topics/physics/power-copper.md#4-corners-and-curves) [C]). Test: transient 3-D check; fuse-trace bench of sharp vs filleted necks.
- **PW19** Designed PCB fuse necks (*new, M*). Size a deliberate neck from I²t (Onderdonk-type adiabatic heating), filleted, away from other copper, so it fails safely instead of a random corner of a 3 A trace; no BOM cost. Test: pulse tests, 10 coupons per geometry.

## D. AC behaviour and returns

Numbers: [PC §5](../../topics/physics/power-copper.md#5-ac-effects) [C].

- **PW20** Stacked power pairs as a router primitive (*new, M*). Route go and return of each high-di/dt branch as a broadside pair on adjacent layers (like a diff pair): ~8× lower L′ than side by side, proximity effect keeps the field in the gap. Test: PEEC loop L and near-field scans on a buck routed both ways.
- **PW21** Frequency-split return design (*sharp, S*). Below f_c = R_s/(2π·μ0·h) (≈0.1–0.5 MHz) return spreads by R, above by L: design the DC path for R and the HF path for L separately; HF must be continuous. Test: IR drop and PEEC loop L on boards with slotted DC path, continuous HF path.
- **PW22** Local eddy shields on 2-layer boards (*new, S*). An isolated or single-tied patch under a lateral hot loop where a full plane is impossible: a floating plane 0.1 mm below cut L′ ~5× at ≥ 1 MHz by mirror currents alone, +12% R [C]; cf. MPS's "noisy local PGND" island. Test: H-field probe + PEEC on a 2-layer buck with/without patch.
- **PW23** AC-resistance budgeting only where it matters (*sharp, S*). R_ac only for planar-magnetics windings and paths > ~5 A with > 5 MHz content (power copper ≤ 1.6× DC below 1 MHz); report the skip. Test: share of boards whose loss estimate changes > 1% with AC R.
- **PW24** Plane as magnetic shield credit (*new, S*). A solid plane mirrors a loop of size a above f_c = 1/(π·μ0·σ·t·a): half the field at ≈2–5·f_c, 90% at 10–45·f_c [C]; credit it in the EMI ledger's near-field term (a plane thinner than a skin depth does shield). Test: near-field scans over a loop with 18, 35, 70 µm planes, 50 kHz–5 MHz.

## E. Commutation cells

Numbers: [PDN §1](../../topics/physics/pdn-and-commutation.md#1-commutation-loop-geometry) [C].

- **PW25** Height × squares instead of loop area (*sharp, S*). Score hot loops by Σ μ0·h_i·dl/w_i whenever a plane is within ≈ half the go/return gap, area only without one: over a plane 0.1 mm away a 20× gap change moved L 1.5×, plane height 4.4× [C]. Test: rank correlation with PEEC on 200 random hot-loop placements.
- **PW26** Commutation-cell geometry search inside N7 (*combo, M*). N7 enumerates lateral, vertical, internal-vertical variants, layer pairs, via rows, terminal widths, scored by image-plane PEEC (ms each); internal-vertical beat lateral 3.5× on one footprint, matching EPC's ~65% [C]; the winner depends on stackup, so search, don't template. Test: N7 cells on 30 EVM converters vs EVM nH and overshoot.
- **PW27** Terminal-row widening and symmetric split cells (*combo, S*). Several CIN_HF across the loop (plate widening saturates at W ≈ a + l, so terminals must widen) and the cell mirrored into two opposite-orientation half-loops (half the current each, far field cancels, MPS −6 dB [P]). Test: PEEC and near-field, single vs split.
- **PW28** Stackup co-design from ∂L/∂h (*sharp, S*). Bid ∂L_loop/∂h of the L1–L2 dielectric in stackup negotiation against SI impedance needs: h is the strongest knob (L ∝ h for vertical loops), yet stackups are usually fixed before power is considered. Test: nH saved per µm vs cost on 10 four-layer boards.
- **PW29** Snubber and C_oss-aware BOM from extracted L (*new, S*). After loop placement compute f_ring, overshoot, snubber R and C (PDN §1 ringing formulas); write them back to the schematic as constraints, snubber placed in the same cell. Closes the layout-to-circuit loop (SLYT614 gives no values). Test: SPICE + bench ringing on 3 boards.
- **PW30** Loop nH in watts (*combo, S*). Express loop L as loss ½·L·I²·f per edge and as derating (overshoot / V_DS,max) so the ledger trades nH vs mΩ vs °C in one currency; EPC's > 4% efficiency step is the right order of magnitude [C, I]. Test: predicted vs measured efficiency deltas on EPC/TI layout comparisons.
- **PW31** Ring-frequency steering (*new, M*). When L cannot drop, choose L and snubber so f_ring = 1/(2π·√(L·C_oss)) (90–290 MHz, radiated band) misses protected bands (FM, GNSS sub-harmonics, cable first resonance) and board-antenna peaks. Test: EMI scan, two geometries, equal overshoot, different f_ring.

## F. Vias and decoupling

Numbers: [PDN §2](../../topics/physics/pdn-and-commutation.md#2-vias-parallel-count-pitch-and-current-sharing), [PDN §3](../../topics/physics/pdn-and-commutation.md#3-decoupling-capacitors-mounting-orientation-spreading) [C].

- **PW32** Via clusters by perimeter, not count (*sharp, S*). HF paths (decaps, hot-loop transitions, stitching at power entries): vias around a perimeter, since a cluster acts like one electrode of its outer size; count separately for DC ampacity (barrels share DC as 1/N). Test: PEEC and Laplace with N8 site choices on 20 MCU boards.
- **PW33** Alternating decap orientation as an N8 constraint (*combo, S*). Alternate power/ground pin order in decap rows, weighted by plane depth (gain grows with depth; quantifies Archambeault's rule). Test: PDN screener sweep + VNA on a 2-layer and a 6-layer coupon.
- **PW34** Side-via decap footprints (*new, S*). 0402/0603 variants with two vias beside each pad, ranked by PEEC: equal to via-in-pad on shallow planes, better on deep ones, no plugging cost. Test: VNA on coupons with three via styles.
- **PW35** PEEC-computed site inductance for N8 (*combo, S*). A ms PEEC evaluation per site (pads, traces, vias, plane depth, neighbours) plus Laplace spreading L to the IC replaces N8's mounting-L table, which misses neighbours and orientation (16–39%). Test: rank agreement with Axotron-style measurements.
- **PW36** Opposite-current via pairing in hot loops (*sharp, S*). At a hot-loop layer change pair each supply via with an opposite-current return via (negative M; via form of PW20); same-direction vias add mutual L (0.69× instead of 0.5× for two). Test: PEEC + bench L, paired vs grouped vias.

## G. PDN and power islands

- **PW37** Spreading-L heat map for decap placement (*combo, S*). One Laplace solve per rail (IC pins as sink) gives L_spread(x) = μ0·d·N□(x) at every candidate site for placer and N8; reproduces Archambeault's 1-inch anchor (PDN §3 [C]) and shows where location matters (thin, narrow, perforated planes). Test: PDN screener Z(f), map-guided vs distance-guided.
- **PW38** Islands shaped by both J and spreading L (*new, M*). Optimise the island outline for DC IR drop (follows loads) and decap-band spreading L (follows IC-to-decap paths) together; a DC-only island can leave a neck between decaps and IC. Test: two-objective OP2 run on 5 FPGA rails.
- **PW39** Adjoint IR-drop sensitivities per neck (*combo, S*). One adjoint solve gives ∂V_load/∂(width) along the rail tree; rank necks by mV per mm² so the LLM widens the right one first (adjoint machinery: [optimization](optimization.md) OP38). Test: IR reduction per mm² vs uniform widening.
- **PW40** Plane-pair capacitance and cavity as budget items (*sharp, S*). Count plane-pair C (PDN §3) into the PDN screener; compute cavity modes per island; prefer thin power/ground cores under power-dense regions; "tight plane pair" becomes an impedance at the relevant frequency. Test: PDN screener vs VNA, one coupon per core thickness.

## H. Sensing, gates and magnetics

Numbers: [PDN §5](../../topics/physics/pdn-and-commutation.md#5-kelvin-sensing-and-sense-loops), [§6](../../topics/physics/pdn-and-commutation.md#6-gate-loops-and-common-source-inductance), [§7](../../topics/physics/pdn-and-commutation.md#7-eddy-currents-under-inductors-the-contested-rule) [C].

- **PW41** Kelvin L_err extraction and filter matching (*new, S*). Inductive error of each shunt with its actual sense route, `V = φ(s₁) − φ(s₀) − jω∫A·dl`; emit τ_RC = L_err/R_shunt as a BOM constraint; flag routes where L_err·di/dt exceeds the signal budget (the "Kelvin taps" rule is silent). Test: scope the sense voltage at a switching edge with/without matched RC.
- **PW42** Sense return under the element (*sharp, S*). Route one sense trace back beneath the resistive element, or pick a 4-terminal part; offer footprint variants; cuts L_err 37% vs separate taps [C]. Test: PW41 bench.
- **PW43** Aggressor–victim M ledger (*combo, S*). PEEC M from every high-di/dt loop to every sensitive loop (FB, sense, gate return, ADC reference); charge V = M·di/dt (and C·dv/dt) to the victim's mV budget instead of fixed 1–2 mm keep-outs. Test: predictions vs measured FB/sense noise on 5 boards.
- **PW44** Per-FET gate-loop budget (*sharp, S*). L_G,max = ¼·R_G²·C_GS from the datasheet, then max driver distance for a stacked/split gate pair; GaN-like and Si budgets differ ~50×, so one "short and wide" rule fits neither. Test: SPICE + bench gate ringing at 3 distances.
- **PW45** Common-source-inductance detector (*sharp, S*). Copper shared by gate return and power source path costs V = L_cs·di/dt, invisible to DRC: force a Kelvin source net tie. Test: the detector catches the fault injected on 10 boards.
- **PW46** Inductor shield-class selector for copper under the coil (*sharp, S*). Dodd–Deeds ΔL and P_eddy from shield class, ripple, f_sw, plane depth; default keeps the plane, a cut-out (or slot pattern) only for open drum cores within ~0.5 mm with no EMI limits; resolves Rohm vs TI vs MPS. Test: LCR with/without plane for 3 inductor families (also calibrates f_ext).
- **PW47** Inductor orientation from dipole coupling (*combo, M*). Stray field as a dipole (or Dodd–Deeds loop) picks rotation and start-of-winding pin against victims and cable entries, with the dot-on-SW E-field rule; today orientation rests on one measured 8 dB anecdote ([buck](../../topics/rules/power/buck.md)). Test: near-field scans while rotating the part.

## I. Thermal coupling and calibration

- **PW48** IPC-2152 replaced by a two-term trace model (*sharp, S*). Trace ΔT = q'/(k·w/h) (local, through dielectric) + plane temperature from the board model; flag only traces whose local term > x K; a trace near a plane is cooled by the plane and board ([PC §6](../../topics/physics/power-copper.md#6-coupling-to-the-thermal-model-owner-thermal-physicsthermal-physicsmd), [thermal-physics](../../topics/physics/thermal-physics.md#ipc-22212152-as-a-thermal-model-vs-a-thermal-network)). Test: FV + IR camera on IPC-2152-style coupons.
- **PW49** Electro-thermal pour solve (*combo, S*). Iterate Laplace current and thermal solves with ρ(T) = ρ₀·(1 + 0.0039·ΔT) (necks heat, raise R, heat more). At > 20 K rise the cold-copper IR estimate is optimistic by ≈ 8% or more and the hot spot moves [I]. Test: vs padne-plus-thermal reference on 3 high-current boards.
- **PW50** Power-copper calibration set (*new, M*). 20 converter cells and decap fields with published nH, mΩ or overshoot (EPC, TI, ADI EVMs, our coupons) that PEEC, Laplace and Dodd–Deeds must match within a stated tolerance before feeding budgets ([V4](../original/verification.md) trust radius). Our [C] numbers agree with anchors within 1.3–2× (EPC 65% vs 71%, Axotron, Archambeault 1 inch); not reproduced: AN-139's flat curve, Archambeault 10 inch. Test: the set, in CI.
- **PW51** Interdigitated go/return fingers for very low-L buses (*new, M*). Split a wide bus into N interleaved go/return fingers on one layer (or alternating on two); loop L ≈ 1/N at equal area (laminated busbars, interdigitated capacitors); for boards without an adjacent-layer return (2-layer, flex) where PW20 is impossible. Test: PEEC sweep of N; VNA on a coupon.
- **PW52** "Physics diff" for power edits (*new, S*). Every proposed power-copper edit (move, widen, add via, trim) returns Δ nH, mΩ, mV pickup, K from incremental Laplace and PEEC before acceptance ("this via saves 14 pH and costs 0.3 mm of channel"), not DRC pass/fail. Test: LLM edit acceptance quality with/without the diff on 10 repair tasks.
